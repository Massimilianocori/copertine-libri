What the state has published

The “Minimum Wage History” table on the Missouri Department of Labor and Industrial Relations page lists $15.00 for 2026 and $15.00 for 2027, and the page links a 2027 poster dated January 1 to December 31, 2027.[1] We could not open the poster itself, so the 2027 figure here comes from the table.

Tipped workers

For 2026 the page says:

“Employers are required to pay tipped employees at least 50 percent of the minimum wage, $7.50 per hour, plus any amount necessary to bring the employee’s total compensation to a minimum of $15.00 per hour.”

Missouri Department of Labor and Industrial Relations, Minimum Wage[1]

The page does not print a tipped figure for 2027.

Who is not covered

“Employers engaged in retail or service businesses whose annual gross income is less than $500,000 are not required to pay the state minimum wage rate.”

Missouri Department of Labor and Industrial Relations, Minimum Wage[1]

The federal minimum wage

“The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[2]

“Many states also have minimum wage laws. Some state laws provide greater employee protections; employers must comply with both.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[2]

So where a state rate is higher than the federal rate, an employer covered by both laws pays the state rate.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the minimum wage in Missouri in 2027?

$15.00 an hour, the same as in 2026. The state’s table of minimum wage rates lists $15.00 for 2026 and $15.00 for 2027.[1]

Is the Missouri minimum wage going up in 2027?

Not in 2027: the state’s table shows $15.00 an hour for both 2026 and 2027.[1]

What is the tipped minimum wage in Missouri?

For 2026 Missouri employers must pay tipped employees at least 50 percent of the minimum wage, $7.50 an hour, plus any amount needed to bring total pay up to $15.00 an hour. The state page does not print a separate tipped figure for 2027.[1]
