Nebraska minimum wage 2027

Nebraska’s minimum wage rises from $15.00 to $15.26 an hour on January 1, 2027, according to the Nebraska Department of Labor’s Labor Standards page.

What the state has published

The Nebraska Department of Labor states the new rate in the heading of its Labor Standards page:

“Labor Standards Home - $15.26 Minimum Wage Effective January 1, 2027”

Nebraska Department of Labor, Labor Standards Home[2]

The 2026 rate of $15.00 is the one in the U.S. Department of Labor’s table of state laws, which notes that Nebraska’s law applies to employers of 4 or more employees.[1] The pages we read do not give a rate for tipped workers, so none is shown here.

The federal minimum wage

“The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[3]

“Many states also have minimum wage laws. Some state laws provide greater employee protections; employers must comply with both.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[3]

So where a state rate is higher than the federal rate, an employer covered by both laws pays the state rate.

What is the minimum wage in Nebraska in 2027?

$15.26 an hour from January 1, 2027. Until December 31, 2026 it is $15.00 an hour.[1],[2]

When does the Nebraska minimum wage go up?

On January 1, 2027, when it goes from $15.00 to $15.26 an hour.[1],[2]
