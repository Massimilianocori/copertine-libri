New Jersey · 2026 and 2027

New Jersey minimum wage 2027

New Jersey’s minimum wage for most employees rises from $15.92 to $16.48 an hour on January 1, 2027.

$16.48New Jersey minimum wage per hour from January 1, 2027
$15.92Per hour in 2026
$659.20Gross pay for a 40-hour week at the 2027 rate
$7.25Federal minimum wage per hour

Official figures read on November 18, 2026. Estimates of pay are before taxes.

New Jersey’s minimum wage for most employees rises from $15.92 to $16.48 an hour on January 1, 2027. The Department of Labor and Workforce Development announced it on September 29, 2026.[1],[2]

What the state has published

“New Jersey’s statewide minimum wage will increase by $0.56 to $16.48 per hour for most employees, effective January 1, 2027.”

New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, New Jersey’s Minimum Wage to Increase to $16.48/Hour for Most Employees on Jan. 1[2]

“The rate for employees of seasonal and small employers (less than six employees) will increase to $16.25 on Jan. 1, up from $15.23.”

New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, New Jersey’s Minimum Wage to Increase to $16.48/Hour for Most Employees on Jan. 1[2]

“Agricultural workers will reach a significant milestone on Jan. 1 when their rate increases to $15 per hour, up from $14.20.”

New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, New Jersey’s Minimum Wage to Increase to $16.48/Hour for Most Employees on Jan. 1[2]

The 2026 rate of $15.92 for most employees is the one in the U.S. Department of Labor’s table of state laws.[1]

Tipped workers

“The minimum cash wage rate for tipped workers will rise to $6.61 an hour from $6.05, with the maximum tip credit employers are able to claim remaining at $9.87.”

New Jersey Department of Labor and Workforce Development, New Jersey’s Minimum Wage to Increase to $16.48/Hour for Most Employees on Jan. 1[2]

If the cash wage plus tips does not reach the state minimum wage, the employer must pay the difference, the same release says.

The federal minimum wage

“The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[3]

“Many states also have minimum wage laws. Some state laws provide greater employee protections; employers must comply with both.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[3]

So where a state rate is higher than the federal rate, an employer covered by both laws pays the state rate.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the minimum wage in New Jersey in 2027?

$16.48 an hour from January 1, 2027. Until December 31, 2026 it is $15.92 an hour.[1],[2]

When does the New Jersey minimum wage go up?

On January 1, 2027, when it goes from $15.92 to $16.48 an hour.[1],[2]

What is the tipped minimum wage in New Jersey?

The minimum cash wage for tipped workers in New Jersey rises to $6.61 an hour on January 1, 2027, from $6.05. The maximum tip credit an employer can claim stays at $9.87. If the cash wage plus tips does not equal at least the state minimum wage, the employer must pay the difference.[2]

How much is a full-time week at the minimum wage in New Jersey?

At $16.48 an hour, the 2027 rate, 40 hours come to $659.20 before taxes and a year of 2,080 hours to $34,278.40. That is our arithmetic on the official hourly rate.[1],[2]
