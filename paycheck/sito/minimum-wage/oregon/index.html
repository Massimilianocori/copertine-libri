Oregon changes its minimum wage on July 1, not January 1. From July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 the rate is $15.55 an hour (standard), $16.80 in the Portland metro area and $14.55 in non-urban counties. The rates from July 1, 2027 were not announced as of November 18, 2026.[1]

The federal minimum wage

“The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[2]

“Many states also have minimum wage laws. Some state laws provide greater employee protections; employers must comply with both.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[2]

So where a state rate is higher than the federal rate, an employer covered by both laws pays the state rate.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the minimum wage in Oregon in 2027?

From July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027: $15.55 an hour standard, $16.80 in the Portland metro area and $14.55 in non-urban counties. The rates that start on July 1, 2027 were not announced as of November 18, 2026.[1]

When does the Oregon minimum wage go up?

On July 1 of each year. The Bureau of Labor and Industries says increases are based on inflation, if applicable, and take effect on July 1st, so the current rates run through June 30, 2027 and nothing changes on January 1, 2027.[1]
