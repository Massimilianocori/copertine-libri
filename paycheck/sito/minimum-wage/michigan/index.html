Michigan minimum wage 2027

Michigan’s minimum wage rises from $13.73 to $15.00 an hour on January 1, 2027.

Michigan’s minimum wage rises from $13.73 to $15.00 an hour on January 1, 2027. The figure is written in state law; each October from 2027 the state treasurer calculates an inflation adjustment that takes effect the following January 1.[1],[2]

What the law says

Section 408.934 of the Improved Workforce Opportunity Wage Act sets the hourly rate:

“(b) Beginning January 1, 2026, $13.73. (c) Beginning January 1, 2027, $15.00.”

Michigan Legislature, Improved Workforce Opportunity Wage Act, section 408.934: Minimum hourly wage rate[2]

The U.S. Department of Labor’s table of state laws says the same:

“The Michigan minimum wage is scheduled to increase on January 1, 2027 to $15.00 per hour and then will be adjusted annually based on a set formula.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, State Minimum Wage Laws[1]

“Every October beginning in October, 2027, the state treasurer shall calculate an adjusted minimum wage rate.”

Michigan Legislature, Improved Workforce Opportunity Wage Act, section 408.934: Minimum hourly wage rate[2]

The same section says the adjusted rate is effective beginning January 1 of the following year, so the first inflation adjustment would apply from January 1, 2028. The federal table notes that Michigan’s law applies to employers of 2 or more employees.[1]

The website of Michigan’s Department of Labor and Economic Opportunity refused our request, so this page relies on the statute and on the federal table. The rates for tipped workers and for minors are not shown: we could not read an official page that gives them.

The federal minimum wage

“The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[3]

“Many states also have minimum wage laws. Some state laws provide greater employee protections; employers must comply with both.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[3]

So where a state rate is higher than the federal rate, an employer covered by both laws pays the state rate.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the minimum wage in Michigan in 2027?

$15.00 an hour from January 1, 2027. Until December 31, 2026 it is $13.73 an hour.[1],[2]

When does the Michigan minimum wage go up?

On January 1, 2027, when it goes from $13.73 to $15.00 an hour.[1],[2]

How much is a full-time week at the minimum wage in Michigan?

At $15.00 an hour, the 2027 rate, 40 hours come to $600.00 before taxes and a year of 2,080 hours to $31,200.00. That is our arithmetic on the official hourly rate.[1],[2]
