Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the minimum wage in New York in 2027?

$17.00 an hour in New York City, Long Island and Westchester County and $16.00 an hour in the rest of New York State. The New York State Department of Labor says these rates stay the same on January 1, 2027.[1],[2]

Is the New York minimum wage going up in 2027?

Not on January 1, 2027. The Department of Labor says that for 2027 the conditions for one of the law’s “off-ramps” have been met, so the minimum wage will not receive an inflation-based increase on that date. The minimum wage for home care aides is a separate rate and does increase.[1],[2]

What is the tipped minimum wage in New York?

For tipped food service workers the cash wage as of January 1, 2027 is $11.35 an hour in New York City, Long Island and Westchester (tip credit $5.65) and $10.70 in the rest of the state (tip credit $5.30). For tipped service employees it is $14.15 and $13.30. Only hospitality employers may take a tip credit.[2]

What is the minimum wage in New York City in 2027?

$17.00 an hour, the same as in 2026. The New York State Department of Labor says that on January 1, 2027 the minimum wage will stay the same: $17.00 in New York City, Long Island and Westchester County and $16.00 in the rest of the state.[2]

How much is a full-time week at the minimum wage in New York?

At $17.00 an hour (New York City, Long Island, Westchester) 40 hours come to $680.00 before taxes and a year of 2,080 hours to $35,360.00. That is our arithmetic on the official hourly rate.[1],[2]
