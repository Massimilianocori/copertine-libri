The federal minimum wage

“The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009.”

U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, Minimum Wage[25]

“Many states also have minimum wage laws. Some state laws provide greater employee protections; employers must comply with both.”

So where a state rate is higher than the federal rate, an employer covered by both laws pays the state rate.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the minimum wage in each state in 2027?

It depends on the state. As of November 18, 2026, 13 states have an official rate for January 1, 2027 that we could read: California $17.40, Connecticut $17.48, Michigan $15.00, Minnesota $11.87, Montana $11.20, Nebraska $15.26, New Jersey $16.48, Ohio $11.40, Rhode Island $17.00, South Dakota $12.30, Vermont $14.91, Virginia $13.75, Washington $17.73. New York and Missouri keep their 2026 rates, and the table on this page gives the 2026 rate for every state.[1],[3],[4],[5],[7],[8],[9],[11],[12],[13],[14],[16],[18],[19],[20],[21],[22],[23],[24]

What is the federal minimum wage in 2027?

The U.S. Department of Labor says the federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour effective July 24, 2009. As of November 18, 2026 its page shows no other figure. Many states have higher rates, and employers must comply with both laws.[25]

Which state has the highest minimum wage?

In the Department of Labor table updated on November 5, 2026, the highest basic rate is $18.40 in the District of Columbia; among the 50 states it is $17.13 in Washington. For January 1, 2027 the highest state rate published by a source we could read is $17.73 in Washington. Some cities set higher local rates.[1],[23],[24]

Which states have the lowest minimum wage?

Alabama, Louisiana, Mississippi, South Carolina and Tennessee have no state minimum wage law, and the state rates of Georgia and Wyoming are below the federal rate. In all of them employers subject to the Fair Labor Standards Act must pay the federal minimum wage of $7.25 per hour.[1]

Is the minimum wage different for tipped workers?

In many states, yes, and the rules differ. Among the states on this site, California, Washington, Minnesota and Montana allow no tip credit, while New York, New Jersey, Ohio, South Dakota and Vermont publish a lower cash wage for tipped workers; each state page gives the official figure where one is published.[8],[11],[14],[15],[16],[19],[21],[23],[26]

Which states raise the minimum wage on January 1, 2027?

From official sources read on November 18, 2026: California, Connecticut, Michigan, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Jersey, Ohio, Rhode Island, South Dakota, Vermont, Virginia and Washington. Alaska’s rate goes to $15.00 later in the year, on July 1, 2027. Other states may also change their rate; where we could not read the state’s page or found no 2027 figure, this page does not show one.[1],[2],[3],[4],[5],[7],[8],[9],[11],[12],[13],[14],[16],[18],[19],[20],[21],[22],[23],[24]
