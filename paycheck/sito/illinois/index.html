How Illinois withholds income tax

“The income tax rate is 4.95 percent and the exemption allowance is $2,925.”

Illinois Department of Revenue, 2026 Booklet IL-700-T, Illinois Withholding Tax Tables[1]

Illinois has one flat rate. The calculator uses the automated payroll method in Booklet IL-700-T: take the allowances from Form IL-W-4 ($2,925 a year for each one on Line 1, $1,000 for each one on Line 2), divide by the number of pay periods, subtract from the pay and multiply by 4.95%.[1]

“Multiply the number of allowances your employee claimed on Form IL-W-4, Line 1, by $2,925.”

Illinois Department of Revenue, 2026 Booklet IL-700-T, Illinois Withholding Tax Tables[1]

“Multiply the number of allowances your employee claimed on Form IL-W-4, Line 2, by $1,000.”

Illinois Department of Revenue, 2026 Booklet IL-700-T, Illinois Withholding Tax Tables[1]

“Multiply the taxable amount by 4.95 percent (.0495). You must withhold this amount.”

Illinois Department of Revenue, 2026 Booklet IL-700-T, Illinois Withholding Tax Tables[1]

The Department of Revenue’s own example, recalculated

The booklet’s example: Alice is paid $800 every week and claims two allowances on Line 1 and two on Line 2. The booklet’s answer is $32.13. Our calculator gives the same result:

  • (2 × $2,925 + 2 × $1,000) ÷ 52 = $150.96 exempt per week
  • $800.00 − $150.96 = $649.04 taxable
  • $649.04 × 4.95% = $32.13

Illinois has no state disability insurance deduction, and we found no local income tax to add in the state booklet.

The federal part

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the Illinois income tax rate?

4.95%. Illinois has one flat rate for withholding, applied to pay after the allowances on Form IL-W-4: $2,925 a year for each Line 1 allowance and $1,000 for each Line 2 allowance in 2026.[1]

Does Illinois have a state income tax?

Yes. Illinois employers withhold a flat 4.95% from wages. On $2,000.00 paid every two weeks with one Line 1 allowance, $93.43 is withheld for Illinois.[1]

What payroll taxes does an Illinois employer pay?

The employer’s 6.2% Social Security and 1.45% Medicare, FUTA and Illinois unemployment contributions on the first $14,250 of each worker’s wages. The employer also withholds the employee’s Illinois income tax.[2],[4]

What is the Illinois unemployment tax rate for new employers in 2026?

Most new employers pay the entry rate of 3.35%, which includes the 0.55% Fund Building Rate, on the first $14,250 of each worker’s wages. Some sectors have a higher entry rate.[2]
