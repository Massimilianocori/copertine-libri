About US Paycheck Calculator

What this site is, what it publishes and how it is paid for.

US Paycheck Calculator is an independent publication, part of a small network of websites that turn official data into plain-language reference pages and calculators. It estimates take-home pay and employer payroll taxes for 2026 in five states, from the official withholding formulas.

We are not affiliated with the IRS, the Social Security Administration, the Department of Labor or any state agency, and we are not accountants or tax advisers. The site gives general information and estimates, not tax, legal or payroll advice.

What we publish

  • Only rates, thresholds and formulas printed in official documents, each linked to its source and quoted word for word where it matters.
  • Arithmetic on those figures (the paycheck estimates and the examples), always labelled as ours.
  • Where an official source cannot be read, or a rule is not covered, we say so instead of filling the gap.

How the site is paid for

The site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers never decide what we publish.

Corrections

If a figure does not match the official document, use the corrections form. For questions about your own pay, ask your employer’s payroll office or a tax professional: they have your records.
