DESNZ figures: deployment to September 2026 (published 29 October 2026), costs for 2025/26, local authorities to the end of 2025.

The North West had 190,968 solar installations with 1,094 MW of capacity at the end of September 2026.[1] In 2025/26, 14,142 systems up to 4 kW were installed in the region at an average of £1,800 per kW, and 7,673 systems of 4 to 10 kW at £1,627 per kW. At those averages a 3 kW system comes to about £5,399 and a 6 kW system to about £9,761 (our arithmetic).[2]
