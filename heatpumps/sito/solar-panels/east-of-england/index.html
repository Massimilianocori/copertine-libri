DESNZ figures: deployment to September 2026 (published 29 October 2026), costs for 2025/26, local authorities to the end of 2025.

The East of England had 246,790 solar installations with 3,694 MW of capacity at the end of September 2026.[1] In 2025/26, 15,982 systems up to 4 kW were installed in the region at an average of £1,796 per kW, and 10,783 systems of 4 to 10 kW at £1,663 per kW. At those averages a 3 kW system comes to about £5,387 and a 6 kW system to about £9,978 (our arithmetic).[2]
