DESNZ figures: deployment to September 2026 (published 29 October 2026), costs for 2025/26, local authorities to the end of 2025.

London had 82,923 solar installations with 432 MW of capacity at the end of September 2026.[1] In 2025/26, 4,364 systems up to 4 kW were installed in the region at an average of £2,101 per kW, and 2,948 systems of 4 to 10 kW at £1,759 per kW. At those averages a 3 kW system comes to about £6,303 and a 6 kW system to about £10,555 (our arithmetic).[2]
