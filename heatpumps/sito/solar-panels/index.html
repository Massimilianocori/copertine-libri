DESNZ solar deployment to September 2026 (published 29 October 2026) and solar cost data for 2025/26 (published 28 May 2026).

There are now more than 2.1 million solar installations in the UK. Provisionally, as of the end of September 2026 there is a total of 23.4 GW of solar capacity in the UK across 2,160,000 installations. This is an increase of 8.9% (1.9 GW) since September 2025. There have been 296,000 installations in the past year- the most in any 12 month period on record.[1]

DESNZ calculates these costs from the Microgeneration Certification Scheme database. They include the panels, installation, connection and VAT where applicable, but not batteries or other devices. The table uses the total installed capacity of the panels; DESNZ also publishes a second series per declared net capacity (the most a system can export), not used here.[2]

Common questions

Questions people in the UK search for, answered from the official sources.

How much do solar panels cost in the UK?

DESNZ puts the median cost of small solar systems up to 4 kW installed in 2025/26 at £1,595 per kW of panel capacity, including installation and VAT where applicable but not batteries. At that rate a 3 kW system comes to about £4,785 (our arithmetic). Systems of 4 to 10 kW had a median of £1,546 per kW.[2]

How much do solar panels cost per kilowatt?

In 2025/26 the average (mean) cost of systems up to 4 kW ranged from £1,760 per kW in the North East to £2,101 per kW in London, on DESNZ's total installed capacity basis. Larger systems cost less per kW: the median for 10 to 50 kW systems was £1,204.[2]
