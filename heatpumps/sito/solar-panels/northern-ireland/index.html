DESNZ figures: deployment to September 2026 (published 29 October 2026), costs for 2025/26, local authorities to the end of 2025.

Northern Ireland had 38,017 solar installations with 425 MW of capacity at the end of September 2026.[1] DESNZ cost statistics cover Great Britain only, so there is no official cost per kW for Northern Ireland.[2]
