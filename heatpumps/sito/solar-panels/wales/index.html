DESNZ figures: deployment to September 2026 (published 29 October 2026), costs for 2025/26, local authorities to the end of 2025.

Wales had 148,492 solar installations with 1,836 MW of capacity at the end of September 2026.[1] In 2025/26, 12,399 systems up to 4 kW were installed in the region at an average of £1,838 per kW, and 9,144 systems of 4 to 10 kW at £1,424 per kW. At those averages a 3 kW system comes to about £5,514 and a 6 kW system to about £8,545 (our arithmetic).[2]
