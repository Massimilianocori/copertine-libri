Great British Insulation Scheme: closed, and what it installed

The scheme ended on 31 March 2026. Who it was for, the 100,940 homes it upgraded, and where help with insulation is still available.

Great British Insulation Scheme statistics, 30 March 2023 to 31 March 2026, Great Britain; provisional. DESNZ, published 25 June 2026.

The Great British Insulation Scheme has closed. Ofgem says it began in April 2023, ended on 31 March 2026 and is now closed to new installations.[1] GOV.UK described it as a way to get free or cheaper insulation, with installations to be complete by 31 March 2026, when the scheme ended.[2]

What the scheme was

The scheme placed an obligation on medium and large energy companies to install measures that cut energy use in the least energy-efficient homes in Great Britain. Unlike ECO4's “whole house” approach, it mostly delivered single insulation measures.[1]

It was open to low-income and vulnerable households and also, in Ofgem's words, to “those living in homes with an Energy Performance Certificate rating of D-G, and within Council Tax bands A-D in England and A-E in Scotland and Wales”.[1] DESNZ counts 61,900 homes upgraded under that general route and 38,673 under the low-income route; a few homes have no recorded route, so the two do not add up to the total.[3]

Help with insulation now

  • ECO4: help with insulation such as loft or cavity walls for households on certain benefits, until 31 December 2026.[4]
  • Warm Homes: Local Grant: wall, loft and underfloor insulation among the improvements councils pay for, in England only.[5]
  • Scotland: Home Energy Scotland lists insulation grants, for example up to £1,500 for loft, cavity or underfloor insulation.[6]
  • Wales: the Welsh Government's Nest scheme includes insulation in its free improvements.[7]

GOV.UK warns of quality issues with some solid wall insulation fitted through the scheme and explains what to do about poor quality wall insulation.[2]

Common questions

Questions people in the UK search for, answered from the official sources.

Is the Great British Insulation Scheme still open?

No. Ofgem says the scheme began in April 2023, ended on 31 March 2026 and is now closed to new installations; GOV.UK says installations had to be complete by that date. Help with insulation is still available through the Energy Company Obligation until 31 December 2026 for households on certain benefits, and through the Warm Homes: Local Grant in England.[1],[2],[4],[5]

What was GBIS?

GBIS is the Great British Insulation Scheme. It placed an obligation on medium and large energy companies to install insulation in the least energy-efficient homes in Great Britain, mostly as single measures rather than ECO4's “whole house” approach. DESNZ counted 100,940 homes upgraded with 139,136 measures from 30 March 2023 to 31 March 2026 (provisional figures).[1],[3]

Who was eligible for the Great British Insulation Scheme?

Low-income and vulnerable households, and also, in Ofgem's words, “those living in homes with an Energy Performance Certificate rating of D-G, and within Council Tax bands A-D in England and A-E in Scotland and Wales”. The scheme has now closed.[1]

Can I still get a loft insulation grant?

The Great British Insulation Scheme, which fitted 39,496 loft insulation measures, has closed. GOV.UK lists loft insulation among the help available through the Energy Company Obligation until 31 December 2026, for households in private housing on certain benefits, and among the improvements the Warm Homes: Local Grant can pay for in England. In Scotland, Home Energy Scotland lists a grant of up to £1,500 for loft, cavity or underfloor insulation.[3],[4],[5],[6]
