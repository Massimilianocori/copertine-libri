Heat pump grants paid in West Lindsey, year by year

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme had paid grants for 406 heat pumps in West Lindsey by the end of September 2026: 4.3% of all those paid in the East Midlands and 0.42% of England and Wales.[1] In the 2025/26 financial year 116 were paid. That is up 12% on 2024/25 (104). From April to September 2026 another 75 were paid (our calculation: the total to date minus the four full years).

What a heat pump costs after the grant

These costs are national, not local. DESNZ publishes installation costs only for England and Wales as a whole, so the same figures appear on every area page of this site. Prices in West Lindsey can be higher or lower: the only way to know yours is a quote from an MCS certified installer.

For air source heat pumps with a grant paid in July to September 2026, the median installed cost was £12,826. Taking off the £7,500 grant leaves about £5,326 for a typical installation.[1]

Who can get the grant in West Lindsey

  • £7,500 off an air source (air-to-water) or ground source heat pump; £9,000 if the property is heated by oil or LPG and has no mains gas, until 31 March 2027; £2,500 off an air-to-air heat pump (homes only); £5,000 off a biomass boiler (rural, off the gas grid).[3],[4]
  • You must own the property (your home, a second home, a property you let or a business building) and replace fossil fuel or electric heating. Hybrid systems, most new builds and social housing are excluded.[3],[5]
  • An MCS certified installer applies for you and takes the grant off the quote; Ofgem asks you to confirm within 14 days.[5]

Solar panels in West Lindsey

At the end of 2025 West Lindsey had 5,102 solar PV installations with a combined capacity of 59.5 MW, up from 2,855 installations at the end of 2020. That is 121 installations per 1,000 households.[2] The count is mostly rooftop systems on homes; the capacity also includes business roofs and any solar farms in the area. DESNZ warns that many 2025 installations could not yet be placed in a local authority, so the latest figures will be revised upwards.[2]

In the East Midlands, small solar systems up to 4 kW installed in 2025/26 cost on average £1,840 per kW and systems of 4 to 10 kW £1,642 per kW, including installation and VAT where applicable but not batteries.[6] That puts a 3 kW system at about £5,519 and a 6 kW system at about £9,854 (our arithmetic on the regional average).
