Heat pump grants in the East of England, year by year

The East of England accounts for 13.6% of the 96,185 heat pumps paid for by the Boiler Upgrade Scheme in England and Wales. Counting biomass boilers too, 13,128 installations had a grant paid, from 20,717 applications and 17,315 vouchers issued.[1] DESNZ puts the region at 48.5 grants paid per 10,000 households, against 37.3 for England and Wales.[1]

What a heat pump costs after the grant

These costs are national, not local. DESNZ publishes installation costs only for England and Wales as a whole, so the same figures appear on every area page of this site. Prices in the East of England can be higher or lower: the only way to know yours is a quote from an MCS certified installer.

Who can get the grant

  • £7,500 off an air source (air-to-water) or ground source heat pump; £9,000 if the property is heated by oil or LPG and has no mains gas, until 31 March 2027; £2,500 off an air-to-air heat pump (homes only); £5,000 off a biomass boiler (rural, off the gas grid).[3],[4]
  • You must own the property (your home, a second home, a property you let or a business building) and replace fossil fuel or electric heating. Hybrid systems, most new builds and social housing are excluded.[3],[5]
  • An MCS certified installer applies for you and takes the grant off the quote; Ofgem asks you to confirm within 14 days.[5]
