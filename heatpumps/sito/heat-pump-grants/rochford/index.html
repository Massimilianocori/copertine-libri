Heat pump grants paid in Rochford, year by year

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme had paid grants for 70 heat pumps in Rochford by the end of September 2026: 0.5% of all those paid in the East of England and 0.07% of England and Wales.[1] In the 2025/26 financial year 25 were paid. That is up 32% on 2024/25 (19). From April to September 2026 another 11 were paid (our calculation: the total to date minus the four full years).

What a heat pump costs after the grant

These costs are national, not local. DESNZ publishes installation costs only for England and Wales as a whole, so the same figures appear on every area page of this site. Prices in Rochford can be higher or lower: the only way to know yours is a quote from an MCS certified installer.

For air source heat pumps with a grant paid in July to September 2026, the median installed cost was £12,826. Taking off the £7,500 grant leaves about £5,326 for a typical installation.[1]

Who can get the grant in Rochford

  • £7,500 off an air source (air-to-water) or ground source heat pump; £9,000 if the property is heated by oil or LPG and has no mains gas, until 31 March 2027; £2,500 off an air-to-air heat pump (homes only); £5,000 off a biomass boiler (rural, off the gas grid).[3],[4]
  • You must own the property (your home, a second home, a property you let or a business building) and replace fossil fuel or electric heating. Hybrid systems, most new builds and social housing are excluded.[3],[5]
  • An MCS certified installer applies for you and takes the grant off the quote; Ofgem asks you to confirm within 14 days.[5]

Solar panels in Rochford

At the end of 2025 Rochford had 2,750 solar PV installations with a combined capacity of 95.9 MW, up from 1,187 installations at the end of 2020. That is 77 installations per 1,000 households.[2] The count is mostly rooftop systems on homes; the capacity also includes business roofs and any solar farms in the area. DESNZ warns that many 2025 installations could not yet be placed in a local authority, so the latest figures will be revised upwards.[2]

In the East of England, small solar systems up to 4 kW installed in 2025/26 cost on average £1,796 per kW and systems of 4 to 10 kW £1,663 per kW, including installation and VAT where applicable but not batteries.[6] That puts a 3 kW system at about £5,387 and a 6 kW system at about £9,978 (our arithmetic on the regional average).
