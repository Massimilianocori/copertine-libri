How much a heat pump costs after the grant

Official installed costs for heat pumps with a grant paid in England and Wales: median £12,826 in July to September 2026, about £5,326 after the £7,500 grant.

£34,630median ground source heat pump, July to September 2026

Official DESNZ figures to the end of September 2026, published 29 October 2026. Next update 26 November 2026.

These are the costs installers reported for every installation with a Boiler Upgrade Scheme grant paid, published by DESNZ. They include the grant value and cover England and Wales as a whole: there are no official figures by region or council.[1] The price paid by the property owner is the total cost of the installation less the value of the grant.[1]

Air source heat pump costs since 2022

In cash terms the median has stayed between £12,082 and £13,600 a quarter since the scheme began. After inflation it has fallen: DESNZ says the median in 2026 Q3 was 14% lower than in 2022 Q3, when it was £14,989 in today's prices.[1] DESNZ notes that the mix of heat pump sizes also moves the median.

Ground source heat pumps

Ground source systems cost much more than air source ones. The median was £34,630 in July to September 2026 and £26,500 over 2025/26; with far fewer installations, the quarterly figure moves a lot.[1]

Common questions

Questions people in the UK search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the average cost of a heat pump installation in the UK?

For air source (air-to-water) heat pumps paid through the grant in July to September 2026, the median installed cost in England and Wales was £12,826, with half of installations between £10,717 and £15,461. These costs include the grant value, so the typical price to the owner after £7,500 was £5,326. DESNZ publishes costs only for England and Wales as a whole.[1]

How much is a heat pump after the grant?

Taking the £7,500 grant off the official median cost of £12,826 leaves £5,326 for a typical air source heat pump (£3,217 to £7,961 across the middle half of installations). For an oil or LPG home that gets £9,000, the same median leaves £3,826. The official cost of the installation already includes the grant, which the installer deducts from your bill.[1],[2]

How much does a ground source heat pump cost in the UK?

The median installed cost of ground source heat pumps paid through the grant was £34,630 in July to September 2026 and £26,500 over the 2025/26 financial year. Ground source costs move a lot from quarter to quarter because far fewer are installed (365 in 2025/26 against 30,516 air source).[1]
