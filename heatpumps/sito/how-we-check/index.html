How we check

How we check every figure

Our sources, what we calculate, how small numbers are handled and how to report an error.

Every figure on this site comes from four official spreadsheets published by the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, and every rule from GOV.UK and Ofgem. On 12 November 2026 we downloaded the latest files, recorded their fingerprints, and checked 52 exact sentences on the official rule pages. Before any update, a separate program re-reads the spreadsheets and recalculates every number printed on the site.

The files

  • Boiler Upgrade Scheme statistics: grants paid by council and region, applications by month, installed costs. Monthly; council and cost tables every three months.[1]
  • Renewable electricity by local authority: solar installations and capacity in every UK council at the end of each year, with household estimates.[2]
  • Solar photovoltaics deployment (Energy Trends table 6.3): installations and capacity by region, monthly.[3]
  • Solar PV cost data: cost per kW by size and region, every year.[4]
  • The rules: GOV.UK “Apply for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme” and Ofgem's scheme and property owner pages.[5],[6],[7]

What we calculate ourselves

  • Cost after the grant: the official installed cost minus £7,500 (or £9,000). DESNZ costs include the grant value.
  • Grants paid per 1,000 households: grants paid to date divided by the household estimates in the DESNZ local authority renewables file. Region pages also show DESNZ's own rate per 10,000 households, which counts homes only.
  • 2026/27 so far: the total to date minus the four complete financial years, only where none of them is hidden.
  • Example solar system costs: kW times the official cost per kW.

Small numbers and missing figures

DESNZ hides counts of 1 to 4 (and some others, so they cannot be worked out) to protect privacy. We show these as “not published” and never estimate them. Council pages are listed for search engines only when the council has at least 200 grants paid and every year is published: 174 of 318 councils today. The others stay available from the tables.

DESNZ does not publish heat pump costs by region or council, so the cost on every area page is the national figure, and each page says so.

When we update

DESNZ publishes the Boiler Upgrade Scheme statistics once a month (next: 26 November 2026); the council and cost tables change each quarter. Solar deployment is monthly, solar costs every May and the local authority solar file every September. The site is regenerated after each release, once every figure has passed the checks.

Report an error

Seen a figure that does not match the official file, or a rule that has changed? Tell us which page and, if you can, link the source. Your email is optional.
