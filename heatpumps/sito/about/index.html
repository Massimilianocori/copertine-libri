About Heat Pump Grants UK

What this site is, what it publishes and how it is paid for.

Heat Pump Grants UK is an independent publication, part of a small network of websites that turn official data into plain-language guides. It shows what the Boiler Upgrade Scheme pays, what heat pumps cost after the grant, and how many grants have been paid in each council area of England and Wales, using the statistics DESNZ publishes every month. The council figures exist in the official spreadsheets but are hard to find; this site puts each one on its own page.

We are not a government website, an installer or an energy company, and we are not linked to Ofgem, DESNZ or MCS.

What we publish

  • Only figures that are in an official file or on an official page, each with its source and date.
  • Where DESNZ hides a small number, or does not publish a figure at all (costs by region, for example), we say so instead of estimating it.
  • Our own arithmetic (cost after the grant, rates per household) is labelled as ours.

How the site is paid for

The site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. In future it may show a labelled “get a quote” link to installers through partner programmes. Partners never decide what we publish or change a figure.

Corrections

If you spot an error, use the corrections form: we check it against the official source and fix the page.

General information, not advice. Grant amounts and rules are taken from GOV.UK and Ofgem; counts and costs from the official DESNZ statistics linked on each page. Whether a property qualifies is checked by your installer and by Ofgem, who runs the scheme. This site is independent and is not a government website.
