How TAS payroll tax is worked out

Tasmania has two thresholds: $1,250,000 and $2,000,000. Wages from $1,250,001 to $2,000,000 are taxed at 4% and wages above $2,000,000 at 6.1%.[1]

The 6.1% rate applies only to the part above the upper threshold. Schedule 1 of the Payroll Tax Act 2008 adds 4% of the band between the two thresholds to 6.1% of the wages above the upper threshold, with both thresholds multiplied by the Tasmanian share of Australian wages and the share of the year you paid wages.[2],[3]

T1 = $1,250,000 × days ÷ 365;  T2 = $2,000,000 × days ÷ 365;  share = TAS wages ÷ W
if W ≤ T1:        no tax
if T1 < W ≤ T2:   tax = (TAS wages − T1 × share) × 4%
if W > T2:        tax = (T2 − T1) × share × 4% + (TAS wages − T2 × share) × 6.1%

Formulas used
  1. Tasmania: nil if W ≤ $1.25m × days; 4% on wages above the lower threshold ($1.25m × share × days); above $2m × days, 4% on the band between the two thresholds and 6.1% above the upper one.

Where W is total Australian wages (yours or your group's), the share is the state's wages ÷ W and days is the days you paid wages ÷ 365. Method and sources.

Monthly threshold and registration

Monthly thresholds are the number of days in the month ÷ days in the year × $1.25 million (and × $2 million), rounded to the dollar.[1] You are liable once Australian wages (or group wages) exceed $1.25 million a year or $24,038 a week during a month; all Tasmanian wage-paying group members must register.[5]

Due dates

Monthly returns are due by the 7th of the following month; the annual adjustment return is due by 21 July.[6]

What changed in 2026-27

Unchanged: the two thresholds and the 4% and 6.1% rates have applied since 1 July 2018.[1]

Tasmanian payroll tax: common questions

Questions people in Australia search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the Tasmanian payroll tax threshold?

$1,250,000 a year. Wages from $1,250,001 to $2,000,000 are taxed at 4% and wages above $2,000,000 at 6.1%; both thresholds are shared by your Tasmanian share of Australian wages and by days employed.[1],[2]

Is the Tasmanian 6.1% rate charged on all wages?

No. 4% applies to the band between the two thresholds and 6.1% only to wages above $2 million (as shared). A Tasmania-only employer with $2.4 million pays $30,000 + $24,400 = $54,400.[2],[4]

When is Tasmanian payroll tax due?

Monthly returns are due on the 7th of the following month; the annual adjustment return is due by 21 July each year.[6]
