How QLD payroll tax is worked out

Queensland charges 4.75% on Queensland taxable wages above your deduction if you (or your group) pay $6.5 million or less in Australian taxable wages, and 4.95% above $6.5 million.[1] Eligible regional employers get 1% off the rate until 30 June 2030.[2]

The threshold is $1.3 million. Above it, the deduction falls by $1 for every $7 of wages, reaching zero at $10.4 million.[3] The Payroll Tax Act 1971 sets the annual deduction as: Queensland share of Australian wages × (K × days ÷ days in year − (Australian wages − K × days ÷ days in year) ÷ 7), with K = $1,300,000, never below zero.[4],[5]

A mental health levy is added for employers and groups with Australian wages over $10 million.[6]

Kd        = $1,300,000 × days paid ÷ 365
deduction = (QLD wages ÷ W) × (Kd − (W − Kd) ÷ 7), not below 0
tax       = (QLD wages − deduction) × 4.75%  (4.95% if W > $6.5m; 1% less if regional)
levy      = 0.25% × (QLD wages − $10m × share × days) + 0.5% × (QLD wages − $100m × share × days), each not below 0

Formulas used
  1. Queensland: deduction = share × ($1.3m × days − (W − $1.3m × days) ÷ 7), not below nil; tax at 4.75% (4.95% if W is above $6.5m; 1% less for regional employers); mental health levy 0.25% above $10m and 0.5% above $100m (shared thresholds).

Where W is total Australian wages (yours or your group's), the share is the state's wages ÷ W and days is the days you paid wages ÷ 365. Method and sources.

Mental health levy

The levy is 0.25% of Queensland taxable wages above the primary threshold of $10 million, plus 0.5% above the additional threshold of $100 million. Both thresholds are shared by the Queensland share of Australian wages (and reduced for part years). A Queensland-only employer paying $112 million owes $255,000 + $60,000 = $315,000; an employer paying $4 million in Queensland out of $16 million owes $3,750.[6] The calculator shows it as a separate line.

Regional discount

Regional employers pay 3.75% (or 3.95% above $6.5 million) until 30 June 2030. You qualify if your registered ABN address is in regional Queensland and at least 85% of your Queensland taxable wages go to employees who live there. The discount does not apply above $350 million of wages.[2] See regional rates.

Monthly threshold and registration

The monthly deduction starts at $108,333.[3] Register within 7 days of the first month in which your Australian wages (or your group's) exceed $25,000 a week.[8]

Due dates

Periodic returns are due 7 days after the end of the month (December 2026: 14 January 2027). There is no June periodic return; the 2026-27 annual return is due on 21 July 2027.[9]

All states: see payroll tax due dates.

What changed in 2026-27

Rates and threshold are unchanged. The regional discount runs until 30 June 2030; on 11 November 2026 QRO announced a temporary amnesty, until 31 December 2026, for certain employers who incorrectly claimed it.[2],[10]

Queensland payroll tax: common questions

Questions people in Australia search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the Queensland payroll tax threshold for 2026-27?

$1.3 million of Australian taxable wages a year. Above it, the deduction falls by $1 for every $7 of wages, reaching zero at $10.4 million.[1],[3]

What is the payroll tax rate in Queensland?

4.75% if you (or your group) pay $6.5 million or less in Australian taxable wages, and 4.95% above $6.5 million. Eligible regional employers get 1% off until 30 June 2030 (3.75% or 3.95%).[1],[2]

How does the Queensland mental health levy work?

It is added to payroll tax for employers or groups with Australian taxable wages over $10 million: 0.25% of Queensland wages above the (shared) $10 million threshold, plus 0.5% of Queensland wages above the (shared) $100 million threshold. A Queensland-only employer with $112 million pays $255,000 + $60,000 = $315,000.[6]

When is Queensland payroll tax due in 2026-27?

Periodic returns are due 7 days after the end of the month (December 2026 is due on 14 January 2027). The 2026-27 annual return, which includes June, is due on 21 July 2027.[9]
