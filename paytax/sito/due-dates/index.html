Due dates · 2026-27

Payroll tax due dates 2026-27: monthly returns and annual reconciliation

When each state wants its monthly return and the annual return, with the official calendars of NSW and Queensland.

Registered employers generally lodge a return every month, due on the 7th of the following month, and an annual return at the end of the financial year: by 21 July, or by 28 July in the ACT, NSW and South Australia. When a due date falls on a weekend or public holiday, lodgement and payment are accepted on the next business day.[10],[11]

Common questions

Questions people in Australia search for, answered from the official sources.

When is the payroll tax annual reconciliation due?

21 July in Victoria, Queensland, Western Australia, Tasmania and the Northern Territory; 28 July in NSW, South Australia and the ACT. If the date falls on a weekend or public holiday, the next business day is accepted.[10],[11]

What are the payroll tax due dates in 2027?

Monthly returns are due on the 7th of each month for the month before (in 2027 NSW and Queensland list 14 January for December 2026, 8 February, 8 March, 7 April, 7 May and 7 June). The 2026-27 annual returns are due on 21 July 2027 or 28 July 2027 depending on the state.[1],[4],[6]
