Privacy notice

What we collect (very little) and why.

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Massimiliano Cori (Italy), who runs Cruise Health Scores as an independent project. Contact: the contact form.

What we collect

Only what you type in the contact form: your email, the ship or page and your message, plus the page you came from. The ship search runs in your browser: what you type is not sent to us. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is stored in your browser only.

No analytics, no cookies

This site uses no analytics or tracking tools and sets no cookies. Some pages contain an affiliate link to SafetyWing (marked “Affiliate”): our site sets no cookie for it, but if you click it you go to SafetyWing’s website, which uses its own cookies under its own privacy policy and tells SafetyWing that you came from us. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where required before any advertising cookies are used.

The data on this site

The inspection data comes from the CDC’s public records. We do not publish names of people.

Why we process your message

To answer you and correct the site. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes data for us

The site and its form are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which stores form data on our behalf and may process it outside the EU with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses). Netlify keeps technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. We do not sell or share your data.

How long

Messages are kept for up to 12 months after our last exchange.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection (Articles 15–22 GDPR) and you can complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority where you live. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information.

Cruise Health Scores

Cruise Health Scores republishes the cruise ship inspection scores and reports of the CDC Vessel Sanitation Program, in a form that is easier to read. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CDC. A CDC score shows how well a ship met sanitation standards on the day of the inspection; it is not a safety rating and does not tell you whether a ship is “safe”.

Ships fail a CDC inspection when they score 85 or lower. For the official record, always check the CDC Green Sheet.
