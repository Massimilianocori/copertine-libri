Cruise ship CDC scores, 2026 vs 2025

Year-over-year comparison of CDC sanitation inspection scores: averages, perfect scores, failures and outbreaks, by cruise line and by ship.

  • Source: CDC public records
  • Data checked on October 31, 2026
  • Pass mark: 86 out of 100
  • Independent site, not the CDC

In 2026 so far (inspections published to October 17, 2026) the average CDC cruise ship score is 96.8, against 96.0 in 2025 (+0.8 points). 25% of 2026 inspections scored a perfect 100 (13% in 2025); 3 inspections failed (1 in 2025). The CDC posted 9 outbreaks of gastrointestinal illness in 2026 (23 in 2025).

CDC inspection scores year by year

Every CDC Vessel Sanitation Program inspection from 2023, when the CDC records are complete, to October 17, 2026. “Outbreaks” are the gastrointestinal illness outbreaks the CDC posted for that year. 2026 is not over yet.

The CDC does not compare years, cruise lines or ships. These averages and changes are our own calculations from the CDC inspection scores. A score shows how a ship met sanitation standards on the inspection day: it is not a safety rating.

Cruise Health Scores

Cruise Health Scores republishes the cruise ship inspection scores and reports of the CDC Vessel Sanitation Program, in a form that is easier to read. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CDC. A CDC score shows how well a ship met sanitation standards on the day of the inspection; it is not a safety rating and does not tell you whether a ship is “safe”.

Ships fail a CDC inspection when they score 85 or lower. For the official record, always check the CDC Green Sheet.
