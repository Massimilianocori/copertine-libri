Cleanest cruise lines by CDC inspection score

Every cruise line ranked by the average of its CDC sanitation inspection scores from Oct 18, 2025 to Oct 17, 2026.

  • Source: CDC public records
  • Data checked on October 31, 2026
  • Pass mark: 86 out of 100
  • Independent site, not the CDC

Ranked by the average of every CDC inspection score from October 18, 2025 to October 17, 2026, the cruise line with the highest score is Disney Cruise Line (99.0 across 11 inspections), followed by Oceania Cruises (98.8) and Regent Seven Seas Cruises (98.6). Among the 14 lines with at least 5 inspections, the lowest average belongs to Princess Cruises (95.0).

What “cleanest” means here. The CDC does not rank cruise lines. This ranking is ours: the plain average of every CDC Vessel Sanitation Program inspection score of each line in the last 12 months of published inspections (274 inspections). It shows how the line’s ships met sanitation standards on inspection days. It is not a safety rating, and a line with few inspections can move a lot with one result.

Cruise Health Scores

Cruise Health Scores republishes the cruise ship inspection scores and reports of the CDC Vessel Sanitation Program, in a form that is easier to read. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CDC. A CDC score shows how well a ship met sanitation standards on the day of the inspection; it is not a safety rating and does not tell you whether a ship is “safe”.

Ships fail a CDC inspection when they score 85 or lower. For the official record, always check the CDC Green Sheet.
