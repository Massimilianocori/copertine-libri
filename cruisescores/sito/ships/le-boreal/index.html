Galleys (kitchens)18 findings

  • Item 16Cold Galley

    Upright refrigerators 1 and 3 along with undercounter refrigerators 2 and 4 were used for keeping potentially hazardous food on time control. The units were not labeled 'time control' and the time control plan did not specify the units used for time control. Crew reported that all cold units in this area were used for time control. In the pastry service area, upright refrigerator 3 was used for time control, but the opposite upright refrigerator was used for temperature control. The time control refrigerator was not labeled for time control, and was not specified on a time control plan. Time control plans in other food areas also did not specify which units were on time control.

  • Item 16Walk-In Cooler

    Multiple items which were being held under temperature control were found to be in the danger zone. A pan of cut cabbage was found at 47°F by the inspector/9.7°C by the crew. Crew indicated that the cut cabbage did not warm above 5°C and therefore did not have a cooling log. A pan of cooked penne pasta was found at 48°F by the inspector / 9.6°C by the crew. Cooling logs indicated that the food was properly cooled. A pan of cooked cauliflower was found at 47°F by the inspector/9.3°C by the crew. Cooked green beans were measured at 47°F by the inspector. Crew explained that the cauliflower and green beans were received cooked and frozen, so the food was only thawed; therefore, no cooling logs were found. The food was discarded.

  • Item 19Bains-Marie

    Excessive steam was flowing from the in-use bains-marie. There were areas that did not have pans over the heated water, allowing more steam to escape. Condensation accumulated above the bains-marie directly over food, including pork soup, lamb, pork, seafood gratin, and more. These items were covered with plastic wrap or metal lids. Dripping was noted onto the metal lid of the pork soup.

  • Item 19Ice Machine

    A small amount of brown debris was noted on ice in the ice bin. Crew were able to wipe the debris away. Crew stated they would remove the ice and clean the machine.

  • Item 20Ice Machine

    Three slotted screws were noted on the cuber sensor panel. The area around the screws, but not in the slotted area, was excessively soiled.

  • Item 20Walk-In Cooler

    The ambient temperature thermometer digital display outside the cooler measured 9.2°C. The ambient temperature thermometer in the back of the cooler measured 8°C. Cooked green beans, cooked cauliflower, cooked penne pasta, and cut cabbage were all found in the walk-in cooler in the danger zone (above 41°F/5°C). Crew explained this is a common problem due to the small size of the unit and crew needing to frequently open the door during service times.

  • Item 22Potwash Portside

    The front loading potwash was out of service since July 2017. The machine was stored with a wash nozzle blocked with debris. In addition, the wash manifolds were excessively soiled with a brown debris that the inspector could wipe off with an alcohol pad.

  • Item 22Potwash Portside

    The sanitizing solution of the 3 compartment sink was soiled with a grease-like film. The area was in operation but no pans were in the sink at the time of inspection.

  • Item 22Conveyor Dishwasher

    The soiled end of the dishwash machine was leaking, which caused excessive standing water on the soiled and clean ends of the machine.

  • Item 22Conveyor Dishwasher

    The third set of upper and lower wash manifolds, closest to the final rinse, did not spray any water at all while the machine was running. The area was in service and the dishwasher was being used when the inspector approached the area. The inspector was able to obtain a temperature measurement of at least 160°F at the plate surface during the final rinse. Crew contacted technical staff to assess the machine.

  • Item 26Ice Machine

  • Item 33Conveyor Dishwasher

Pools, hot tubs and water play areas5 findings

  • Item *Pool Machinery Room

    A potable water line bypassed the filtration system to refill the pool directly and the water introduced to the pool was not first halogen-treated to ensure it was within the acceptable range.

  • Item 08Pool Machinery Room

    There was no air gap under the intermediate vent of the reduced pressure zone assembly connected to the potable water line that bypasses the filtration system to refill directly the pool.

  • Item 09Pool

    The halogen measured by two different test kits and different parts of the pool was 1.1 and 1.2 around 10:00 am. The inspector discussed with the staff that since the halogen was close to the minimum acceptable value of 1 ppm it was advised to increase the set point of halogenation and monitor the pool while this was being done. The pool was reevaluated around 3:35 pm by the inspection team and the halogen measured by two different test kits and different parts of the pool was 0.26 and 0.35. It was found that no adjustments were done by the staff between evaluations. Upon inspection of the chart in the pool room, it was found that since around 9:00 am the chlorine levels dropped drastically in a steady way from about 1 ppm to almost 0 ppm. The pool was immediately closed. It was unclear if the system alarmed about the low chlorine levels.

  • Item 10Test Kit

    Secondary standards were not available for the ship's Lovibond halogen test kit. A similar violation was written on the previous report for the Hanna test kit. Also, the test kit could not measure pH and was the kit used for pool testing. Staff stated that they checked the calibration of the halogen readings of the Lovibond test kit with the readings of the Hanna test kit. To calibrate for pH, they calibrated the analyzer and compared it to the readings of the manual test kit.

  • Item 10Sedimentation Test

    Sedimentation tests for the pool sand filter was not conducted.
