Galleys (kitchens)11 findings

  • Item 21Deck 4 - Cosmopolitan Restaurant

    Wires rested on the countertop below the coffee station. This was immediately corrected.

    The wash, rinse, and sanitizing compartments of the three-compartment sink were soiled with food debris. The sanitizing solution measured within the required range. Additionally, the wash compartment was excessively cloudy, opaque, and the bottom of the sink was not visible. Corrective action began immediately.

  • Item 22Deck 14 - Oceanview Cafe - Warewashing

The final sanitizing rinse temperature measured over 160°F at the plate surface temperature. This was corrected prior to the end of the inspection.

  • Item 26Deck 5 - Raw on 5
