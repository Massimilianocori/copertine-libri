  • Item 4424-Hour Report and AGE Logs

    There were several instances where information regarding whether a specimen was requested or received, whether anti-diarrheal medication was dispensed, whether a passenger was reportable, and if a passenger had an underlying illness was not indicated on the AGE log. Additionally, the boxes at the top of the AGE log to indicate reportable crew or passengers were either blank or incorrect. Additionally, the number of reportable passengers was incorrect on submitted 24-hour reports. Finally, when asked why the numbers were incorrect, staff stated that no one on board had been exhibiting AGE symptoms when the reports were submitted, and that passengers had recovered within 24 hours of exhibiting symptoms. Staff did not know the number of reportable cases at the top of the AGE log and the number of reportable cases on the 24-hour reports should be a cumulative number of passengers and crew members meeting the case definition, not the number of passengers and crew members meeting the case definition at the time of report submission.

Ships fail a CDC inspection when they score 85 or lower. For the official record, always check the CDC Green Sheet.
