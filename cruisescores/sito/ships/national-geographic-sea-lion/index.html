Drinking water5 findings

  • Item 04Far Point/Distributiont Halogen Residual Levels

    When staff and the inspector checked, the far point analyzer measured 5.7 ppm and 5.7, and 5.7 respectively. When the inspector tested the water manually, the photo meter read 5.7 consistently. The high residual alarm was on during the inspection. Remediation began immediately. It was determined that the paddle wheel assembly that communicates to the halogen dosing pump when to shut-off during bunkering was not operating properly thus the dosing pump was still adding halogen into the system after the bunkering process was stopped. The far point halogen value dropped to 4.23 and was continuously dropping when the inspector checked the analyzer 1.5 hours later.

  • Item 06Potable Water Bunkering/Production Log/Chart

    The ship has one chart recorder on board that was responsible for bunkering and distribution of potable water. No notations were made on the charts regarding calibration values when the bunker or production process stopped and re-started. The inspector discussed a plan for the staff to follow.

  • Item 06Potable Water Bunkering/Production Log/Chart

    One chart is used for recording free halogen residual levels. On this chart, multiple days throughout April, May, and June, the chart recorder was used for more than a 24 hr. period. In some cases, the time over-lap exceeded 1-hour. The inspector discussed with the staff person in-charge and talked about the multiple duties this staff person has and how to re-organize this task so the maximum 24-hour time limit for this chart can be met. This is a repeat violation for an inspection conducted on 6 May 2018

  • Item 08Reverse Osmosis Permeate Water Line

    The permeate water line exiting the reverse osmosis unit that continues on to the dosing station for potable water storage was painted blue. Blue/Grey/Blue is needed for this technical water line.

  • Item 08Potable Water Bunkering Log

    The ship has one chart recorder on board that was responsible for bunkering and distribution of potable water. This analyzer is located at the distant point. A manual log is kept for when the ship bunkers potable water. When the inspector checked the manual log for the halogen and pH readings and compared them to the analyzer, on the 4th, 6th, 9th, 12th, 13th, 19th, 28th, and 29th of May, the halogen residual reading on the manual chart did not match the reading on the far point chart by at least 2.0 ppm. Typically, on multiple recordings for other periods, a spike would be recorded on the chart recorder when the bunkering process began; however, on the dates listed above, there was no spike and the residual values were out of sync by over 2.0 ppm. No notations were made on the charts regarding this issue nor were notations made on the charts for other anomalies seen on multiple charts. The inspector discussed a plan for the staff to follow.
