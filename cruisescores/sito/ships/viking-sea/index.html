Medical center1 finding

  • Item 02Acute Gastroenteritis (AGE) Log Review

    On the AGE log, the last symptom column was not updated to reflect a food employee's actual time of last symptom. Medical staff stated that this information automatically populates in the AGE log for the electronic medical record system. The food employee reported to medical on March 19 at 1217 after an onset of symptoms at 1150. The AGE log listed the employee's last symptom as March 19 at 2100. However, a review of the employee's medical record showed the crew member's actual last symptom as March 21 at 1400. The crew member was isolated for the appropriate amount of time.
