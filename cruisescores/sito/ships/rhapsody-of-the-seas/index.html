Ventilation1 finding

  • Item 43HVAC Units Condensate Drip Tray

    All HVAC units (approximately 30-40) were equipped with small, removable metal panels with self-capping screws for inspection; however, when open, visibility was extremely limited, which did not allow for a full inspection of the area. Additionally, the design of the units required labor intensive, time-consuming dismantling of the unit in order to perform a proper inspection and subsequent cleaning. With limited crew onboard to service the units, shoreside teams are required to help augment the cleaning process. The following four units were inspected and extremely soiled with excessive black debris: 1) ACR 210, serving the theater stage area on Decks 5-6, was soiled with an excessive amount of black debris on the droplet collector. 2) ACR 27, serving the wheelhouse, was soiled with an excessive amount of black debris on the coil frame and in the condensate drip pan under 1' deep water. 3) ACR 216, serving Decks 7-8 passenger cabins, was soiled with an excessive amount of black debris on the droplet collector. 4)ACR 211, serving Decks 2-4 passenger cabins, was soiled with an excessive amount of black debris on the droplet collector.
