Food safety, general4 findings

  • Item 13Deck 8 Buffet Marketplace

    Crew members who were handling food items were observed not washing their hands before touching clean equipment and food items. The following areas were involved: Burger Station: Two crew members placed burgers on the self-service counters, then proceeded to touch passengers’ plates to serve hot dogs and to operate the hot grills without removing their gloves or washing their hands. Pizza Station: A crew member placed ready to eat pizza on the passenger service counter without gloves, then touched raw dough to prepare new pizzas without washing their hands.

  • Item 31Waiter Stations Countertops

    More than 10 disinfectant spray bottles were stored on multiple waiter stations throughout dining rooms and food preparation areas, including where eating utensils were present. Many bottle labels were faded or either peeling making it difficult to identify the product. Crew members stated these disinfectants were intended for use on nonfood-contact surface. However, there was no proper chemical storage for the disinfectants, and no documentation or labeling was provided to confirm that the products were “food-contact safe” or otherwise approved to be stored in close proximity to food, food-contact surfaces, or utensils. A similar finding was reported per the last inspection.

  • Item 38Equipment

    The following items have not been used for a long time and are no longer necessary for food operations: Deck 19, Yacht Club Galley: counter-mounted brew-and-juice machine. Deck 16, Yacht Club Bar: counter-mounted brew machine. Deck 16, Hola Cantina Galley: multi-flow soda cabinet.
