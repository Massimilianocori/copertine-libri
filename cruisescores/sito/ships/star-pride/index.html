Drinking water8 findings

  • Item *Engine Room - Deck 1 - Potable Water Tank "48 P/S"

    The sample cocks for these newly installed tanks were located approximately 16 inches from the top of the tank and require the tank to be ~75% full in order to use.

  • Item 04Halogen Residual - Distribution

    Distant point records for 6 NOV and 11 NOV 2022 indicated the halogen levels for the upper loop (Deck 8) exceeded 5ppm from ~0500 to 0600. According to crew, the non-return valve to the halogen injection system failed and additional chlorine was unintentionally drawn into the line. Additionally, no notation was written on either chart when this occurred to indicate corrective action was taken.

  • Item 05Deck 7 - Distant Point

    The distant point halogen analyzer was greater than 1ppm out of calibration when compared with measurements taken with both the inspector's and crew member's photometers. At the time of the measurements, the analyzer read 1.43ppm and the inspector's test measured 2.53ppm and the crew member's measured 2.45ppm. The analyzer was recalibrated.

  • Item 06Halogen Residual - Distribution

    Distant point records for 6 NOV and 24 NOV 2022 indicated the halogen levels for the upper loop (Deck 8) exceeded 5ppm from ~0500 to 0600. According to crew the non-return valve to the halogen injection system failed and additional chlorine was unintentionally drawn into the line. Additionally, no notation was written on either chart when this occurred to indicate corrective action.

  • Item 08Cross-Connection Control Log

    The last test dates for six testable backflow prevention devices were not listed on the cross-connection log. Individual review of each of the backflow prevention devices on the ship's electronic maintenance system indicated the tests were completed within the required timeframe. Staff stated they would ensure the log is updated with the test dates.

  • Item 08Machinery Room - Deck 4 Foward

    The air gap to the potable water line for whirlpool filling was not twice the diameter of the supply line and at least one inch.

  • Item 08Machinery Room - Deck 4 Forward

    The potable water piping in this room was not striped blue/green/blue to indicate potable water.

  • Item 08Engine Room - Deck 1 - Pipe Striping

    Permeate water piping from the reverse osmosis units was striped blue/green/blue, indicating potable water instead of blue/grey/blue up to the dosing point. Additionally, the permeate water line directed to laundry was not striped blue/grey/blue.

Pools, hot tubs and water play areas3 findings

  • Item 08Deck 7 - Pool Pump Room

    The potable water filling line for the pool was striped blue/grey/blue instead of blue/green/blue. Additionally, other potable water lines in this room were missing potable water striping.

  • Item 10Deck 7 - Pool - Shepherd's Hook

    The pole of the rescue or shepherd's hook was not long enough to reach the center of the deepest portion of the pool from the side plus 0.6 meters (2 feet).

  • Item 10Whirlpool Shock Halogenation

    Dating back to September 2022, the ship's two whirlpools were not shock halogenated every third day. According to crew, the ship has a low supply of bromine and in order to conserve, they do not shock halogenate the system on the day they drain and replace the water (every 72 hours).
