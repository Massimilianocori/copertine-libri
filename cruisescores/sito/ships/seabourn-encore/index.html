Drinking water5 findings

  • Item 08Pipe Maintenance Records

    The exact free halogen residual value after flushing and prior to return to service was not recorded for backflow prevention devices replaced on 31 May, 8 May, 24 April (4 devices), and 17 April. The values were recorded as 'N/A.'

  • Item 08Aft Evaporator Distillate Line

    The air gap under the backflow prevention device was not twice the diameter of the delivery fixture opening and at least one inch. Crew initiated corrective action.

  • Item 08Tank Maintenance Records

    The measured free halogen residual after flushing potable water tank #14S following disinfection was recorded as “discharged” instead of a specific measured value. Additionally, the measured free halogen residual after flushing potable water tank #14P following disinfection was recorded as 50 mg/L (PPM).

  • Item 08Tank Maintenance Records

    The tank maintenance record for potable water tank #14P identified curing started on 27 January at 0830 and finished the same day at 1100, a time of 2 hours and 30 minutes. The manufacturer's documentation required a curing time between 4 to 7 days.

  • Item 08Tank Maintenance Records

    According to the tank maintenance record for potable water tank #14P, filled tank disinfection started on 3 February at 1700 and ended at 2000, providing only 3 hours of the required 4 hours for contact time.

  • Item 09Aft Swimming Pool

Crew initiated corrective action.
