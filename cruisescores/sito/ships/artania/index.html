Pools, hot tubs and water play areas5 findings

  • Item 09Whirlpool Spa

    The pH values of four water samples taken by the inspector in different areas of the whirlpool spa were 6.63, 6.78, <6.50, and 6.57. The inspector's water test kit did not measure pH values below 6.50, so lower values were displayed as <6.50. The pH values of two water samples taken by the crew member in different areas of the whirlpool spa were also below 6.50. The crew member's water test kit did not measure pH values below 6.50, so lower values were displayed as LOW. The whirlpool spa was open during the inspection and was immediately closed.

  • Item 10Whirlpool Spa Mechanical Room

    The pH analyzer for the whirlpool spa was reading a value of 7.49 even though pH values in the whirlpool spa was measured around 6.50 by both the inspector and crew. When the pH value was tested at the sample point of the analyzer, the inspector had a value of 6.69 and the crew member had a value below 6.50. According to the chart recorder, the whirlpool spa was calibrated in the morning when it opened.

  • Item 10Fecal/Vomit Accident Plan

    The fecal/vomit accident plan did meet or exceed Annex 13.9.1 of the 2018 VSP Operations Manual. The ship's current plan stated to maintain pH between 7.2 and 7.5 during disinfection and it did not mention a required water temperature. This is a new requirement in the 2018 VSP Operations Manual.

  • Item 10Artania Pool

    A significant amount of debris had accumulated around the floor drain at the bottom of the pool, which made it difficult to see and evaluate the condition of the anti-entrapment drain cover. This is a new requirement in the 2018 VSP Operations Manual.

  • Item 10Artania Pool

    The water level in the pool was approximately 8-inches above the lower level skim gutter of the pool. The water level did not reach the high-level (beach access) skim gutter around the pool.

Drinking water9 findings

  • Item 06E. Coli Testing

    The procedures for E. coli testing (random and bunkering) were not done in accordance with the manufacturer's recommendations. The test kit was for 18 hour processing which requires a warm water bath. The staff reported not using a warm water bath but rather that they use the incubator to warm the sample then they leave the sample in the incubator to process.

  • Item 06E. Coli Testing

    The random testing for E.coli did not meet the definition of random. All samples were taken only from cabin rooms.

  • Item 07Evaporator High Saline Discharge

    The connection from the evaporator line to overboard had no protection against backflow. Staff produced documentation that showed a reduced pressure assembly was ordered on 28 September but it was not yet received.

  • Item 07Potable Water Skin Tank Variance

    A skin tank variance was requested by the ship in Augusts 2017. Information provided to the inspector showed that the variance was not yet issued by VSP review staff. Staff did however produce documentation that the salinometers were calibrated.

  • Item 08Potable Water Pipe Striping

    The pipes leading into the mineralizers were striped in four different areas with different colors. Staff stated that these were striped to indicate potable water. One stripe was greenish blue, another was blue green blue as well as blue grey blue, the next, blue green blue and just before chlorine injection, the striping was blue. This was corrected by the end of the inspection.

  • Item 08Potable Water Pipe Maintenance/Repair

    There were no records for any years to show disinfection done after maintenance or replacement of potable water lines. Staff stated that there had been maintenance which occurred but they do not document it.

  • Item 08Tank Maintenance Records

    There was no written documentation that the manufacturer's recommendations had been followed for the application, drying, and curing of the interior coating for potable water tanks: forward portside 3, forward starboard 3, portside 2, starboard 2, center 12, and center 13.

  • Item 08Tank Maintenance Records

    The method of disinfection, the concentration, and the contact time of the disinfectant were not listed on the tank maintenance records for tanks 13c, 4ps, 14sb, and 14ps. The record referenced a separate document, which stated that a halogen level of 50 ppm for 4 hours or 200 ppm for 2 hours may be used. However, it was unclear which method was being used to disinfect the tanks.

  • Item 44Potable Water Skin Tank Variance

    Records were missing for pipe maintenance conducted onboard. Information regarding the concentration and contact time for disinfection of potable water tanks and for application, drying, and curing of interior coatings was also missing. Additionally, the pipes leading to the mineralizer were striped in four different areas with different colors. One stripe was greenish/blue, another was blue/green/blue as well as blue/grey/blue, the next blue/green/blue, and just before the chlorine injection, the striping was blue.
