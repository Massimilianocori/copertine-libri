  • Item 17Deck 3

    Three different but overlapping time control plans were presented for the Crew Galley and Splash Cafe (crew mess.)The three plans presented included many of the same units, but all had different time control periods. The time control periods for the galley did not match either of the buffet time control periods, which may result in food being out for service for greater than 4 hours. 1) The Time Control Plan posted near the beverage station and ice cream machine included overlapping time periods of 0600-1000 and 0930-1330 for all counters/tables (portable or fixed), all hot holding countertops, all undercounter hold holding units, the pizza warmer, soup kettles fixed, and ice cream machine for turn around day. The ice cream machine had an affixed label to denote it as a time control unit. However, there was no discard time control label for the ice cream mix during the lunch service. Crew explained that the ice cream is only out for lunch and did not exceed 4 hours. 2) The Time Control Plan posted near the show galley grill included time periods of 0530-0930 and 1000-1400 for counter/tables (portable or fixed), all hot holding counter tops, all undercounter hot holding units, cold food counter top, soup kettles fixed, ice cream machine, and pizza warmer on turn around day. Food out for service at the buffet did not have time control labels due to the gap in service time described in this plan. 3) The Time Control plan posted in the crew galley included time control periods of 0530-0930 and 0930-1330 for countertops (portable or fixed), all bains maries, all hot holding under counters, all hot holding counter tops, all cold tops, heating lamps, and trolley. Food on the countertops were appropriately labeled with dicard times.
