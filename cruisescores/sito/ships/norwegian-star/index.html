Drinking water2 findings

  • Item 08Tank Coating Curing Time

    The manufacturer's instruction for the potable water tank coating cure time was not followed before washing and sanitizing for multiple projects in 2024. The manufacturer's required curing time was 12 days at 20°C and 7 days at 30°C. For example: (1) Potable water tank 5 P (PW3) cured for only 10 days from 31 May to 11 June at 20°C; (2) Potable water tank 6 STBD cured in February for only 6 days at 20°C' (3) Potable water tank 7 STB (PW14) cured for only 5 days from 1-7 March at an undocumented temperature; and (4) Potable water tank 6 Center cured for only 5 days from 17-22 February at 25°C.

  • Item 44Tank Coating Curing Time

    During multiple projects in 2024, the person in charge of potable water tank maintenance did not follow the manufacturer's instruction for the tank coating curing time prior to washing and sanitizing the tanks. The manufacturer's required curing time was 12 days at 20°C and 7 days at 30°C. Recorded cure times were less than the required time and tank temperature.
