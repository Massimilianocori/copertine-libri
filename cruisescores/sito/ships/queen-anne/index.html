Buffets6 findings

  • Item 19Deck A - Staff Mess

    The sides of the carving station were not protected by a sneeze shield. The area was not open during the inspection, and staff stated a new carving station with appropriate sneeze shields had been ordered.

  • Item 19Deck A - Upright Self-service Display Case

    Two plates of Bakewell tarts were out for self-service and not adequately protected. The tarts were removed.

  • Item 30Deck 9 - Artisan's Foodhall Toast Station/Portside

    There were no paper towels at the crew handwashing station. The area was in operation. This was corrected.

  • Item 33Deck 9 - Artisan's Foodhall Toast/Portside and Dessert Stations/Starboard Side

    More than one day's worth of dust accumulated in the deckhead's technical space.

  • Item 33Deck 9 - Artisan's Foodhall Storage Cabinets/Portside

    There was no deck coving below the dry food storage and clean equipment storage cabinets.

  • Item 33Deck 9 - Artisan's Foodhall Pizza Station/Portside

    Dust accumulated in the roller shutter compartment above the deck mounted ovens.

Food storage (provisions)4 findings

  • Item 19Deck A - Provisions - Shell Door Area/Portside

    Multiple cases of bottled water provisioned the day prior to the inspection, were stored near the portside shell doors under an area open to the plenum and without a deckhead. Additionally, sealed bags of food waste were stored among the cases of bottled water.

  • Item 19Deck A - Dry Storage

    The top of a cardboard box of packaged flour was wet. The source of the wetness could not be determined, and the packages of flour were not impacted. The flour was re-boxed.

  • Item 33Deck A - Dry Storage

    More than one day's worth of dust and food debris accumulated on the deck under the shelves. Whole raisins and a dried yellow liquid were under the pallets on the left side of the storage room.

  • Item 39Deck A - Provisions - Shell Door Area/Portside

    Multiple small flies were seen around the sealed bags of food waste in the provisioning area.

Food safety, general3 findings

  • Item 21Tilting Pans and Kettles

    Many tilting pans and kettles were missing the black collars used to close the gaps between the tilting pan/kettle arms and their technical compartment. This created a difficult to clean area. Staff showed a purchase order for these parts and were waiting for their delivery.

  • Item 21Ice Machines

    The ice machines used in the food and beverage areas were not constructed to be easily accessible for cleaning and maintenance. Several of the machines require the side panel to be removed for access, but the side panels were welded and could not be removed.

  • Item 21Buffet - Island Countertops

    Soft sealant was applied around the hot press counters across the vessel. The sealant was not durable or easily cleanable. It could not be determined if this sealant was heat resistant.

Drinking water3 findings

  • Item 08Device/Assembly Log

    Four backflow prevention devices were listed as non-testable devices for the spa aroma injection equipment; however, they were reduced pressure assemblies needing manual testing annually. Staff stated this was the backflow prevention device/assembly log received from the shipyard upon handover.

  • Item 08Deck A - Garbage Room

    Three continuous pressure non-testable backflow prevention devices for the biodigesters were installed but not listed in the backflow prevention device/assembly log. Staff stated this was the backflow prevention device/assembly log received from the shipyard upon handover.

  • Item 08Deck A - Garbage Room

    The air gap between the relief vent and water catch tray was insufficient for the portside biodigester. The relief vent was positioned inside the catch tray, which was hard plumbed into the bilge system.

Pantries3 findings

  • Item 21Housekeeping Pantry Ice Machines

    The ice machine drain line extensions throughout all housekeeping pantries were not sloped to drain.

  • Item 28Housekeeping Pantry Clean Storage Shelf

    Previously cleaned and sanitized room service ice buckets were stored in pooled water on the clean storage shelf above the utility sink.

  • Item 33Housekeeping Decks

    Deck tiles were in disrepair under previously removed clean storage utensil racks in all housekeeping deck pantries.

Galleys (kitchens)4 findings

  • Item 16Deck 9 - Cold Room #40

    Pans of grilled zucchini chimichurri, miso cauliflower with tahini, cauliflower, farro, and pistachio salad were not marked with the correct preparation date of the first time control for safety (TCS)/potential hazardous food (PHF) ingredient. The pans were labeled with a preparation date of 5 April; however, a review of the cooling logs showed the grilled zucchini, roasted cauliflower, and farro were prepared on 4 April. The ship was using a 3-day discard system, so the discard date was still within the required 7 days.

  • Item 21Deck A - Hot Galley

    The metal panel on the back of the tilting pan handles was not flush, creating a difficult to clean area.

  • Item 22Deck 9 - Potwash Machine

    The potwash machine was out of order since the end of March. Staff stated they are waiting for replacement parts.

  • Item 22Deck 2 - Brittania

    The rack-type glasswash machine was out of order since the end of March. Staff stated they were waiting for spare parts.

  • Item 33Deck 11 - Cabana Bar

Drinking water4 findings

  • Item 08Testable Assembly Results

    There were no testing results or records for testable backflow prevention assemblies with measured pressure differences on both sides of the valves.

  • Item 08Non-testable Device Inspection

    The visual inspection results for the non-testable backflow prevention devices were not documented.

  • Item 08Tank Sample Cocks

    Some of the potable water tank sampling cocks were not completely installed. Engineers explained the ship was delivered with approximately half of the sampling cocks turned downward and equipped with appropriate backflow prevention devices. Engineers identified the other half of the sampling cocks were not installed, with open tank attachments and no means for sampling.

  • Item 08Tank Coating

    There was no documentation of the potable water tank interior coating application, drying, curing, and disinfection prior to the ship being placed in service after delivery from the shipyard.
