Pools, hot tubs and water play areas1 finding

  • Item 10Maintenance

    The forward whirlpool was not drained every 72 hours. The chart records stated it was drained on 14, 22, and 29 July 2026. Additionally, the aft starboard whirlpool was drained on 22 July and was not recorded as drained on 25 July 2026; however, it was drained and under maintenance on 26 July 2026. It was unknown if the hair and lint strainer and hair and lint strainer housing for all recreational water facilities were cleaned, rinsed, and disinfected weekly between 3 July 2026 and 31 July 2026. The printed paper records stated the facilities were maintained weekly on 3, 10, 17, 24, and 31 July 2026. However, on the chart records, a stamp stated the hair and lint strainer and hair and lint strainer housing were maintained only on 3, 23, and 31 July 2026. The stamps were missing for 10 and 17 July 2026 and conflicted with the paper records for the 24 July 2026.
