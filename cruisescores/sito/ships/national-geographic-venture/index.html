What the inspectors found

The full findings of the latest inspection, as written in the CDC reports. Each finding refers to an item of the CDC’s Vessel Sanitation Program standards; points are deducted by item, for a single significant violation or for a pattern of similar ones (CDC FAQ).

100Passed

11 findings · 0 points deducted

No points deducted.

All 11 findings the inspectors wrote down, grouped by area of the ship. Findings with a red edge belong to an item that lost points; the others were noted without a deduction.

Galleys (kitchens)4 findings

  • Item 08Combination Ovens

    The backflow prevention device was not easily accessible for inspection.

  • Item 22Dishwash

    A water leak was below the rack-type dishwash machine's final sanitizing rinse compartment.

  • Item 28Reach-in Refrigerator

    Three foldable food storage containers were stored on the deck beside the reach-in refrigerator.

  • Item 33Walk-in Refrigerator

    The sealant was peeling between two deckhead panels.

Dining rooms2 findings

  • Item 20Beverage Station

    The coffee dispenser had slotted fasteners in the food splash area.

  • Item 21Buffet Line

    An open seam was on the hot plate/countertop juncture.

Housekeeping2 findings

  • Item 41Disinfectant Test Strips

    The test strips were not appropriate for the disinfectant used onboard during outbreaks. When the staff member applied the disinfectant to the test strip, it turned green which is not on the color comparator.

  • Item 41Outbreak Prevention and Response Plan (OPRP)

    The OPRP did not include the concentration for the disinfectant used during outbreaks.

Bars1 finding

  • Item 20Lounge Deck

    The soda gun had slotted fasteners on the food contact area.

Food safety, general1 finding

  • Item 39Flies

    Small flies were seen in the following areas on the lounge deck: One was by the beverage station. Two were at the bar. Two were at the pulper in the main galley. One was at the handwashing station near the pulper in the main galley.

Drinking water1 finding

  • Item 08Tank Coating

    There was no written documentation of approval from a certification organization for the potable water tank coating.
