Galleys (kitchens)4 findings

  • Item 18Deck 9 - Lido Marketplace Portside Cold Room

    One pan of raw marinated shrimp was stored above pans of vegan cheese and barbecue sauce. The pan was relocated.

  • Item 33Deck 10 - Fahrenheit 555 Hot Galley

    The deck tile grout was recessed underneath the flat-top grill station.

  • Item 30Deck 10 - Fahrenheit 555 Crew Toilets

    Both the male and female toilets were out of order. Staff stated the work order had been placed the morning of inspection; they were repaired before the end of the inspection. The venue was not in operation.

  • Item 24Deck 1 - Flight-type Dishwash Machine

    The machine's final sanitizing rinse was not consistently providing 160°F at the utensil surface. The inspector measured a maximum 158°F with a thermocouple device. A crew lollipop thermometer measured 168°F. An irreversible multi-temperature color changing paper thermometer placed on a dish showed a 150°F color change but only a small corner of the 160°F square changed color, indicating 160°F was not adequately achieved. The final rinse sanitizing temperature measured at the manifold with a lollipop thermometer was 181°F; crew increased the manifold temperature and then 160°F was consistently achieved at utensil surface. The area was in operation.

Pools, hot tubs and water play areas1 finding

  • Item 09Deck 9 - Main Pool

    The inspector and crew measured the pH above 7.8 in the open recreational water facility (RWF). The inspector and technician took samples from various locations around the pool. The inspector's pH readings were 8.04, 7.99, and 7.99. The technician's pH readings were 7.83, 8.01, and 7.76. A technician was dispatched to the RWF equipment room to check the analyzer reading. The technician discovered a pump had broken and the equipment room was flooded by water from the main pool, so an accurate reading could not be taken at that time. The pool was closed, the pump was fixed, then the pool was reopened after a comparison test was taken.
