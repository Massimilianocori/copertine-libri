    The halogen analyzer reading was more than 0.2 ppm from the manual comparison test. The analyzer recorded the chlorine level as 4.55 ppm; however, manual samples measured 3.55 ppm and 3.32 ppm by the crew and 4.20 ppm and 3.82 ppm by the inspector.
