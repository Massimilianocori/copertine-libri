Food storage (provisions)4 findings

  • Item 13Loading Dock

    There was not adequate oversight of the loading operation to ensure foods requiring temperature control were promptly loaded and refrigerated. At 0800, several pallets of raw shell eggs and packaged dairy products were on the dock awaiting loading. At 1000, the products remained on the dock while the outdoor ambient air temperature was 83°F. Using a calibrated thermometer, crew and the inspector measured the internal temperatures of raw shell eggs representatively sampled from two pallets (20 boxes per pallet). Temperatures ranged from 48°F to 59°F. Crew and the inspector also measured the internal temperatures of pasteurized chocolate milk with temperatures ranging from 52°F to 54°F. The arrival time and original receiving temperatures of the products were not recorded, preventing management from determining how long the foods had been outside temperature control or whether temperatures had increased while awaiting loading. Despite the refrigerated foods remaining on the dock, shoreside workers continued loading dry-storage goods. There was no intervention to prioritize the loading of the raw shell eggs and dairy products and minimize the time these foods remained outside temperature control.

  • Item 16Loading Dock

    At 0800, several pallets of raw shell eggs and packaged dairy products were on the dock awaiting loading. At 1000, the outdoor ambient air temperature was 83°F, and the products remained on the dock. Using a calibrated thermometer, crew and the inspector measured the internal temperatures of raw shell eggs representatively sampled from two pallets (20 boxes per pallet). Temperatures ranged from 48°F to 59°F. Crew and the inspector also measured the internal temperatures of pasteurized chocolate milk representatively sampled from two pallets (20 containers per pallet). Temperatures ranged from 52°F to 54°F. The arrival time and original receiving temperatures of the products were not recorded. Shoreside workers were loading dry-storage goods while the pallets of TCS food remained on the dock awaiting loading.
