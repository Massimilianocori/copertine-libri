Galleys (kitchens)10 findings

  • Item 16Deck A- Preparation Countertop

When the inspection team entered the area, crew said they were following the time control plan for the Main Galley Cold Pantry. The Main Galley Cold Pantry time control plan did not list refrigerator #1 on it and only had red and yellow control labels indicated on it. Two trays of various TCS/PHF sandwiches were stored in refrigerator #1 and labeled with green time control labels. The time control plan for the Living Room Bar Cold Display, where the sandwiches would be sent, was posted above the Main Galley Cold Pantry time control plan and included set up and discard times for green time control labels.

  • Item 17Deck 2- Restaurant Pantry

Drinking water5 findings

  • Item 08Cross Connection-Control Log

    The differential pressure values for the water misting system's reduced pressure zone assemblies (RPZ) were not listed in the testable cross connection -control log. The inspector and staff noted that this type of RPZ has a different valve testing protocol. The test does not list a differential pressure value, but this value can be derived by calculating actual gate pressures and comparing them to the test value. The protocols state that the differential pressure must be no more than 2 pressure per square inch (psi) greater than the actual gate pressure. Staff and the inspector added a comment section explaining the differential pressure procedure and the testing kit's standardized psi value for reference.

  • Item 08Incinerator Room

    The handwashing sink's potable water lines were not striped, blue/green/blue, or blue for potable water.
