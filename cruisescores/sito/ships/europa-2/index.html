Pools, hot tubs and water play areas2 findings

  • Item 10Whirlpool Shock Halogenation

    The Deck 11 crew whirlpool was open but did not have records of shock halogenation on the following dates: 1 January 2026, 18 January 2026, and 19 January 2026. During 19 January, a note stated they were unable to complete shock halogenation due to paint issues. Additionally, the Deck 11 passenger whirlpool was open but did not have records of shock halogenation on the following dates: 13 December 2025, 24 December 2025, 1 January 2026, and 19 January 2026.

  • Item 10Records

    During rough weather, shock chlorination did not occur either prior to draining or prior to opening the recreational water facility. Additionally, when the recreational water facilities were drained, multiple days of records were missing. It was not clear how long the facility remained closed, especially prior to installation of the data logger.

Pools, hot tubs and water play areas4 findings

  • Item *Manual Testing Log

    The logs for all four recreational facilities (one swimming pool and three jacuzzi/whirlpools) documented all manual test and calibration times as the same (07:20 daily). Staff stated that this time was an average of when the actual manual test and calibration were performed. Staff explained that the datalogger was not reliable and they used their manual samples for documentation of water chemistry.

  • Item 10Fecal and Vomit Accident Procedures

    The fecal and vomit accident procedures did not specify to maintain the pH of 7.5 or less and the temperature at 77'F (25'C) or higher during the disinfection. The plan was updated and replaced by the end of the inspection.

  • Item 10Water Chemistry Monitoring

    Crew used a photometer system which was capable of measuring combined chlorine and alkalinity; however, reagents for the alkalinity test were not available and therefor crew lacked the means to test for alkalinity. Crew did have the reagents to measure combined chlorine onboard. Crew reported they did not monitor combined chlorine or total alkalinity. On the September 2016 inspection report, it was noted that staff had no means to measure combined chlorine levels or total alkalinity.

Medical center2 findings

  • Item 02Acute Gastrointestinal Questionnaire

    The self-administered questionnaires used for crew members did not contain all the data elements that appear in the questionnaire found in Annex 13.2. Specifically, only the food and shipboard activities history was used. The self-administered questionnaires used for passengers did contain all the data elements.
