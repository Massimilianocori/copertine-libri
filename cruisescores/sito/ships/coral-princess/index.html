Medical center2 findings

  • Item 11Acute Gastroenteritis (AGE) Log

    Four crew members experiencing AGE symptoms did not properly isolate and report to medical after symptom onset. A nonfood employee had an onset of symptoms on 17 June at 0100 and did not report to medical until 1100. The employee did not have any close contacts and did not report to work. The employee was retrained. A nonfood employee had an onset of symptoms on 16 June at 1100 and did not report to medical until 1405. According to documentation provided by the medical center, the employee stated they vomited in their cabin around 1100 and then called the medical center around 1200. The employee stated no one answered or called back; the medical team had no documentation of the phone call in the phone log. The employee then reported to work some time before 1400, when they vomited again and then physically reported to the medical center. The employee was retrained. A food employee had an onset of symptoms on 4 June at 1130 and did not report to medical until 1500. The employee reported to work at 1200 and stopped working at 1500. A food risk assessment was conducted, and the employee received retraining. A nonfood employee had an onset of symptoms on 2 June at 1200 and did not report to medical until 1730. The employee was conducting handover duties in their cabin but did not report to work or visit crew mess after experiencing symptoms. The employee disembarked the vessel via separate transport between 0930-1000 on 3 June.

    Every deck with cabins is equipped with one or two garbage rooms.
