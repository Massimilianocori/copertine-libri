Food storage (provisions)4 findings

  • Item 19Deck 3 - Ice Cream Freezer

    More than a day's accumulation of ice was on a cardboard box of food stored below the deckhead hatch next to the evaporator condenser. The product was discarded immediately.

  • Item 33Deck 3 - Frozen Vegetable

    More than a day's accumulation of ice was on the deck below the evaporator condenser. No product was affected by the ice.

  • Item 33Deck 3 - Fish Freezer and Expensive Items Freezer

    Ice collected on the deckhead and bulkhead around the back evaporator condenser units. This was a known issue and under warranty with the shipyard. The crew stated that the shipyard will be sending technicians to install dehumidifiers in those areas when the ship is in Europe. Meanwhile, the ship is placing protective tarps over the food until the issue is resolved.

  • Item 33Deck 3 - Ice Cream Freezer

  • Item 21Deck 11 - Pool Bar

Housekeeping2 findings

  • Item 10Regent Suite - Heated Jetted Tub - Draining, Cleaning, and Disinfection

    Records of the heated jetted tub from the previous inspection to 31MAR2026 showed that the unit was drained, cleaned, and disinfected every three days rather than once per day per the VSP 2025 Environmental Public Health Standards. Once per day draining, cleaning, and disinfecting procedure went into effect on 01APR2026 due to a policy change. All records from 01APR2026 were in compliance.

  • Item 10Regent Suite - Heated Jetted Tub - Draining, Cleaning, and Disinfection

    The heated jetted tub did not have a safety sign at the tub entrance advising users that using the unit without the use of automated halogen and pH control may increase the risk of respiratory infections.
