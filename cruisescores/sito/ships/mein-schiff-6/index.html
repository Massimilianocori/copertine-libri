The white pipes were cut, creating an air gap.

Drinking water4 findings

  • Item 07Pipe Disinfection Records

    On 18 March, the potable water pipes were disinfected with less than 50 ppm, concentrations ranged from 48-49 ppm, for 5 hours.

  • Item 08Engine Room- AWP

    A potable water line, protected with a backflow preventer, was connected to the pre-treatment filter for the black water/gray advanced wastewater treatment system (AWP). This line was removed, as the only allowable connection between potable water and black water/gray water systems is an air gap.

  • Item 08Tank Maintenance Records

    Painting and disinfection of the eight potable water tanks was conducted between 18 November and 20 December 2016. For the tank interior coating application records, no information was provided about the curing procedure or if this was done according to manufacturer's recommendations; the manufacturer's curing time for the paint was 9-10 days at 16°C. Additionally, no free halogen residual was recorded after disinfection of the tanks to verify the halogen was less than 5.0 ppm before the tanks were placed back into service.

  • Item 08Pipe Disinfection Records

    No free halogen residual was recorded after pipe disinfection to verify the halogen level was less than 5.0 ppm before the pipes were placed back into service. On 27 March, documentation was provided for different areas throughout the ship showing the free halogen residuals were recorded as less than 5.0 ppm.
