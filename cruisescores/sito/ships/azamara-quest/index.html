Housekeeping1 finding

  • Item 41Outbreak Prevention and Response Plan (OPRP)

    The OPRP was last updated in November 2023 and does not reflect information from the 2025 Environmental Public Health Standards. Specifically: 1. The OPRP referenced a previously used disinfectant instead of the current product, including its appropriate contact time; and 2. The daily attack rate was listed as 0.50% instead of the updated 0.45%. Additionally, the OPRP specified that contaminated linens should be washed at 71°C (160°F) for 3 minutes or 65°C (149°F) for 10 minutes. However, contaminated linens should be washed at a minimum temperature of 71°C (160°F) for at least 25 minutes. (VSP 2025 Environmental Public Health Standards)
