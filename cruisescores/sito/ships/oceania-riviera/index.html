Food safety, general3 findings

  • Item 33Condition of Deck Tile Grout

    The deck tile grout was missing or recessed throughout the following food areas: *Deck 12 Waves Grill near the grooved grill. *Deck 4 Crew Galley. *Deck 5 Main Galley throughout, but especially near the pastry section ice cream machines.

  • Item 13Knowledge

    Lack of proper managerial controls and knowledge resulted in several time/temperature control for safety (TCS) / potentially hazardous food (PHF) violations: (1) No time control discard labels were provided for any of the TCS / PHF out for service on the center buffet island. According to the time control plan, breakfast was from 0630-1030 and lunch was from 1030-1430 with no break in service. The posted time control plans stated, 'For areas open 4hrs or less - the total setup and service time does not exceed 4hrs, therefore individual containers with PHF do not need to be labeled.' Staff stated the area was open for four hours; however, per the time control plan, the area was open for eight continuous hours. (2) Bowls of salmon, bacon, and cut tomatoes stored in the time-controlled reach-in refrigerator #1 had incorrect time control discard labels. The time control discard labels stated 1030 - 1430; however, it was 1000 when the inspector was in the area and the containers had been in use that morning. (3) Two pans of lasagna prepared on 20 September 2026 did not retain the date of the first ingredients, bolognese and bechamel, that were prepared on 19 September 2026.

  • Item 13Variance - Culinary Kitchen

    Multiple approved procedures required for the implementation of the culinary kitchen variance were not understood or correctly followed: (1) Participant lists were not available for review. (2) Medical clearance for participants was not completed no more than one hour prior to the start of the class. Emails were typically sent the evening on the day prior to the class. (3) The records were not kept for 12 months. Staff stated on two different occasions that they only needed to be kept for 30 days.
