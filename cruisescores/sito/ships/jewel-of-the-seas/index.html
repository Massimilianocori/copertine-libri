  • Item 24Deck 4 - Warewashing

    The final sanitizing rinse plate surface temperature of the flight type warewashing machine was unable to achieve the required 160°F and above when tested using the crew and inspector's temperature measuring device. The following plate surface temperatures were recorded by the crew's tip sensitive thermometer: 156°F, 154°F, & 153°F. The inspector's temperature measuring device recorded a plate surface temperature of 158°F. The manifold reading was 176°F, which did not meet the minimum requirement of 180°F as per the data plate posted on the machine. Crew began immediate corrective actions and were finally able to achieve a plate surface temperature of 163°F. Food equipment was actively being washed and sanitized prior to arriving in this area. Crew returned all previously cleaned and sanitized food equipment to be reprocessed.

  • Item 10Jetted Tub Cleaning and Disinfection

    The jetted tub disinfection procedures and process in cabins1560 and 1562 had disinfection occur prior to cleaning. Staff powered the jets with a solution of water and 100ppm chlorine before cleaning and scrubbing resulting in a dark scum line from the turbulent water. Upon review of the written procedure in the Public Health Manual, revised in December 2025, it was clear staff followed the procedure that called for cleaning and scrubbing after disinfecting. It was discussed during the inspection that cleaning and scrubbing should precede disinfection and the procedure should be revised.
