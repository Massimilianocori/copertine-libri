(2) On 24 April, there was no notation on the chart record of a PVI. Sea-to-sea mode started at 7pm. The Response Log stated a PVI at occurred 4:50pm. (3) On 10 April, there was no notation on the chart record of a PVI. Sea-to-sea mode started at 7pm. The Response Log stated a PVI occurred at 6:20pm. (4) On 13 March, there was no notation on the chart record of a PVI. The Response Log stated a PVI occurred at 6:10pm. The chart record stated an increase in chlorine concentration to 4.3 ppm from 6:10pm - 6:45pm. The Response Log stated chlorine concentration 2.5-2.9 ppm during that time. (5) On 1 June, the chart record stated the pool was closed at 9:45pm due to a PVI. The Response Log stated the time 8:57pm with the disinfection occurring 9:02pm - 9:30pm. For the Waterpark: -On 22 April, the chart record stated the facility was closed at 2pm due to a fecal incident. The Response Log stated this was a vomit incident at 3:54pm.
