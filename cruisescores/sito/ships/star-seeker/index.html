Ventilation1 finding

  • Item 43HVAC Units

    The air handling units on the vessel did not comply with the VSP 2025 Environmental Public Health Standards or the VSP 2018 Construction Guidelines. This issue was documented in the November 2025 Construction Inspection Report provided to the shipyard and previous owner. Windstar took ownership of the vessel in December 2025. This issue was also noted on the previous March 2026 operational inspection report. The construction inspection report noted: – Make air handling unit condensate collection pans readily available for inspection, cleaning, and maintenance per section 33.1.6 of the VSP 2018 Construction Guidelines. – AC Station 01.03 was inspected and did not comply with the 2018 Construction Guidelines. Shipyard personnel reported all HVAC units are constructed the same and therefore no condensate collection pans are accessible. This is not compliant and must be corrected. The March 2026 Operational Inspection Report noted that the deck 5 - Air Handling Units 02.05.01 and 02.05.02 condensate drain pans and collection systems were not accessible for inspection, maintenance, and cleaning. Staff provided documentation showing the manufacturer had been contacted for corrective action and the response was pending. Since the March 2026 report, the vessel purchased and began using a borescope for inspections. This interim solution also did not allow for cleaning of the unit to take place. A corrective action plan for the March 2026 inspection was not on file with VSP. Crew reported a sister ship was currently undergoing a refit in the yard and the HVAC units on that vessel would be assessed. There was no plan presented for corrections on the Star Seeker.
