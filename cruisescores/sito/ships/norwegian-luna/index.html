What the inspectors found

The full findings of the latest inspection, as written in the CDC reports. Each finding refers to an item of the CDC’s Vessel Sanitation Program standards; points are deducted by item, for a single significant violation or for a pattern of similar ones (CDC FAQ).

10 findings · 0 points deducted

No points deducted.

All 10 findings the inspectors wrote down, grouped by area of the ship. Findings with a red edge belong to an item that lost points; the others were noted without a deduction.

Galleys (kitchens)4 findings

  • Item 20Deck 8 - The Local - Salad Station

    A cutting board in active use was heavily scored. This was corrected immediately.

  • Item 26Deck 8 - The Local - Pastry

    A previously cleaned and sanitized plate was stored soiled over the serving line. This was corrected immediately.

  • Item 26Deck 6 - Ice Machine

    The ice machine bin was soiled with a brown debris on the interior right side. Corrective action began immediately and was corrected prior to the end of the inspection.

  • Item 27Deck 8 - Anda

    More than a day's worth of grease residue was in the exhaust hood overhang above the stacked combination ovens. This was cleaned immediately.

Buffets2 findings

  • Item 30Deck 4

    A paper towel dispenser at the crew handwashing station was empty. This was corrected immediately.

  • Item 33Deck 17 - Surf Side Cafe - Pizza Station

    A gap was in between the deckhead panels above the pizza preparation countertop. No food was impacted. This was corrected before the end of the inspection.

Pools, hot tubs and water play areas2 findings

  • Item 10Deck 17 - Main Pool

    The portside shepherd’s hook was obstructed by three umbrella stands. The umbrella stands were promptly removed, and a square was painted to clearly mark the area and prevent any items from blocking access to the shepherd’s hook.

  • Item 10Deck 18 - Aqua Park

    The mechanism to operate the shower was more than one meter above the deck. The bottom of the circular mechanism is one meter above the deck, and the actual sensor is an additional 2 centimeters above.

Housekeeping1 finding

  • Item 10Jetted Tub Disinfection

    The disinfection records for staterooms 12226, 12224, and 12222 documented they were cleaned and disinfected twice on 30 March 2026 and not between a change of occupancy on the 31 March 2026. This was identified as a clerical error.

Food storage (provisions)1 finding

  • Item 39Deck 4 - Flour Dry Store Walk-In

    One fly was near the dry store walk-in door along the provision corridor.
