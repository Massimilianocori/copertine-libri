Lindblad Expeditions norovirus and GI outbreaks: the CDC list

Every gastrointestinal illness outbreak on Lindblad Expeditions ships posted by the CDC Vessel Sanitation Program since 2023: ship, sailing dates, people ill and cause.

The CDC has posted 5 gastrointestinal illness outbreaks on Lindblad Expeditions ships from 2023 to 2026 (list read on November 27, 2026): 3 unknown cause, 2 norovirus. In 2026 so far: 4. Most recent: National Geographic Sea Bird, sailing 7/15/2026 – 7/20/2026 (cause as stated by the CDC: Unknown; 8 of 57 passengers (14.0%) and 5 of 26 crew (19.0%) reported being ill).

What this list counts, and what it leaves out. The CDC says it posts an outbreak when the ship “is under VSP jurisdiction (on voyages including both U.S. and foreign ports)” and the voyage “has 3% or more of passengers or crew reporting symptoms of GI illness to the ship’s medical staff”. It “may also post other outbreaks of public health significance” (CDC). Ships under VSP jurisdiction “carry 13 or more passengers and have a foreign itinerary with U.S. ports” (CDC, about VSP). So a sailing that never calls at a U.S. port, or one where fewer than 3% reported symptoms, is normally not here. The figures are totals for the whole voyage, not people sick at the same time. The CDC can update the counts of a recent outbreak: the figures here are the ones on the CDC pages on November 27, 2026. The number of outbreaks is not a rate: a line with more ships and more voyages that call at U.S. ports has more voyages that can appear. It is not a safety rating.
