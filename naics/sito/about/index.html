About NAICS Code Lookup

An independent site that makes the NAICS classification readable.

NAICS Code Lookup is an independent, free information site. It puts the North American Industry Classification System of the United States into readable pages, with the official descriptions, the activities of the official index, the old 2017 codes and the 1987 SIC codes.

Why it exists

The official files are spreadsheets with thousands of rows: structure, descriptions, index, cross-references and concordances are in separate files. Here they are joined code by code, with the source next to each block.

What we are not

  • We are not the Census Bureau, OMB, the IRS or the SBA, and we are not affiliated with them.
  • We give no legal, tax or business advice: check with the agency or form that asks for the code.
  • We do not collect data about readers, apart from the optional form to report an error.

How the site is paid for

The site may show Google AdSense ads on the pages with the most content. Ads never change a code or a text, and nobody pays to appear.

NAICS Code Lookup

NAICS Code Lookup turns the official NAICS 2022 files of the U.S. Census Bureau into readable pages: titles, descriptions, the activities of the official index, the 2017 codes and the 1987 SIC codes. Independent site, not affiliated with the Census Bureau, OMB, the IRS or the SBA.

Codes, titles, descriptions, index entries and concordances: U.S. Census Bureau, North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) 2022 files and the official concordances back to the 1987 SIC (public domain). Official texts are shown without changes; layout, links, counts and the chaining of concordances from 1997 to 2022 are ours.
