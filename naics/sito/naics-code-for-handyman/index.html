NAICS code for handyman, remodeling and painting

Handyman construction service on homes is NAICS 236118, on commercial buildings 236220; painting contractors 238320; drywall 238310.

The official index places «handyman construction service» with building contractors, by type of building: residential is 236118 «Residential Remodelers». A trade done on its own, such as painting or drywall, has its own specialty code. The index lists 67 matching entries in 7 codes.[1],[2]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for a handyman?

The official index lists «Handyman construction service, residential building» under 236118 «Residential Remodelers» and «Handyman construction service, commercial and institutional building» under 236220 «Commercial and Institutional Building Construction».[1]

What is the NAICS code for remodeling?

Remodeling houses and other residential buildings is 236118 «Residential Remodelers», which includes remodeling general contractors, for-sale remodelers and remodeling design-build firms according to its official description.[3]

What is the NAICS code for painting?

House painting and painting contractors (except roofs) are 238320 «Painting and Wall Covering Contractors». Roof painting is 238160 «Roofing Contractors» and line painting on highways and parking lots is 237310 «Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction».[1],[2]
