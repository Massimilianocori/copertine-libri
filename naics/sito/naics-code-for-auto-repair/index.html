NAICS code for auto repair, car wash and towing

General auto repair 811111, specialized repair 811114, body shops 811121, car washes and detailing 811192, towing 488410.

Automotive repair is industry group 8111 «Automotive Repair and Maintenance», split by type of work. The official index lists 88 matching entries in 9 codes; towing is a transportation support activity.[1],[2]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for auto repair?

General automotive repair shops are 811111 «General Automotive Repair»; brake, transmission, exhaust and other specialized repair shops are 811114 «Specialized Automotive Repair»; body and paint shops are 811121 «Automotive Body, Paint, and Interior Repair and Maintenance».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a car wash or detailing?

811192 «Car Washes», with car washes, self-service and mobile car washes and automotive detailing services in the official index.[1]

What is the NAICS code for towing?

488410 «Motor Vehicle Towing», with tow truck services, wrecker services and emergency road services in the official index.[1]
