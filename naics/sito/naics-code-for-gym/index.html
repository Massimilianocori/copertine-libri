NAICS code for a gym or personal trainer

Gyms and fitness centers are NAICS 713940, personal fitness training 812990, exercise and yoga instruction 611620/611699.

Running a gym and training clients are different codes in the official index. Fitness centers and health clubs are 713940 «Fitness and Recreational Sports Centers»; personal fitness training services are 812990 «All Other Personal Services». The index lists 28 matching entries in 5 codes.[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

The codes to look at first

713940Fitness and Recreational Sports Centers

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating fitness and recreational sports facilities featuring exercise and other active physical fitness conditioning or recreational sports activities, such as swimming, skating, or racquet sports.

In the official index: Fitness centers; Exercise centers; Body building studios, physical fitness; Aerobic dance and exercise centers; Athletic club facilities, physical fitness; Dance centers, aerobic…

SIC 1987: 7991, 7997, 7999

812990All Other Personal Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing personal services (except personal care services, death care services, drycleaning and laundry services, pet care services, photofinishing services, or parking space and/or valet parking services).

In the official index: Personal fitness training services

SIC 1987: 7251, 7299, 7389

611620Sports and Recreation Instruction

This industry comprises establishments, such as camps and schools, primarily engaged in offering instruction in athletic activities. Overnight and day sports instruction camps are included in this industry.

In the official index: Aerobic dance and exercise instruction; Gymnastics instruction, camps or schools; Martial arts instruction, camps or schools

SIC 1987: 7999

611699All Other Miscellaneous Schools and Instruction

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in offering instruction (except academic schools, colleges, and universities; business, computer, and management instruction; technical and trade instruction; and fine arts, sports, recreation, language, exam preparation, tutoring, and automobile driving instruction).

In the official index: Yoga instruction, camps or schools; Pilates instruction, camps or schools; Self defense (except martial arts) instruction

SIC 1987: 7999, 8299

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for a gym?

713940 «Fitness and Recreational Sports Centers». The official index lists fitness centers, exercise centers, health clubs and body building studios there.[1]

What is the NAICS code for a personal trainer?

The official index lists «Personal fitness training services» under 812990 «All Other Personal Services». Aerobic dance and exercise instruction is 611620 «Sports and Recreation Instruction», and yoga or pilates instruction is 611699 «All Other Miscellaneous Schools and Instruction».[1]
