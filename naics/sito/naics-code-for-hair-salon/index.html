NAICS code for a hair salon, beauty salon, nail salon or barber

Beauty and women's hair salons 812112, barber shops 812111, nail salons 812113, day spas 812199, beauty schools 611511.

Personal care services are industry group 8121 «Personal Care Services», and the official index separates hair and beauty salons from barber shops and nail salons. It lists 41 matching entries in 5 codes, including beauty and barber schools.[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

The codes to look at first

812112Beauty Salons

This U.S. industry comprises establishments (except those known as barber shops or men's hair stylist shops) primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) cutting, trimming, shampooing, coloring, waving, or styling hair; (2) providing facials; and (3) applying makeup (except permanent makeup).

In the official index: Beauty salons; Hair stylist salons or shops, unisex or women's; Esthetician (i.e., skin care) services; Beauty and barber shops, combined; Beauty parlors; Beauty shops…

SIC 1987: 7231

812111Barber Shops

This U.S. industry comprises establishments known as barber shops or men's hair stylist shops primarily engaged in cutting, trimming, and styling men's and boys' hair; and/or shaving and trimming men's beards.

In the official index: Barber shops; Hair stylist services, men's; Hair stylist shops, men's

SIC 1987: 7241

812113Nail Salons

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing nail care services, such as manicures, pedicures, and nail extensions.

In the official index: Nail salons; Manicure and pedicure salons; Manicurist services; Pedicure and manicure salons; Pedicurist services

SIC 1987: 7231

812199Other Personal Care Services

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing personal care services (except hair, nail, facial, nonpermanent makeup, or non-medical diet and weight reducing services).

In the official index: Day spas; Hair removal (i.e., depilatory, electrolysis, laser, waxing) services; Depilatory (i.e., hair removal) salons; Electrolysis (i.e., hair removal) salons; Eyebrow threading services; Hair replacement services (except by offices of physicians)…

SIC 1987: 7299

611511Cosmetology and Barber Schools

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in offering training in barbering, hair styling, or the cosmetic arts, such as makeup or skin care. These schools provide job-specific certification.

In the official index: Cosmetology schools; Beauty schools; Barber colleges; Colleges, barber and beauty; Cosmetic art schools (e.g., makeup, skin care); Manicure and pedicure schools…

SIC 1987: 7231, 7241

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for a hair salon?

Unisex or women's hair stylist salons, hairdressers and beauty salons are 812112 «Beauty Salons». Men's hair stylist shops and barber shops are 812111 «Barber Shops».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a nail salon?

812113 «Nail Salons», with nail salons, manicure and pedicure salons and manicurist services in the official index.[1]

What is the NAICS code for a barber shop?

812111 «Barber Shops». Shops combining beauty and barber services are listed under 812112 «Beauty Salons» as «Beauty and barber shops, combined».[1]
