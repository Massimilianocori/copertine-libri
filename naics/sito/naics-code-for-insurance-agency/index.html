NAICS code for an insurance agency or broker

Insurance agencies and brokerages are NAICS 524210; claims adjusting 524291, third party administration 524292, other services 524298.

Selling insurance for insurers is industry group 5242 «Agencies, Brokerages, and Other Insurance Related Activities», separate from the carriers that underwrite it. The official index lists 26 matching entries in 4 codes.[1],[2]

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

The codes to look at first

524210Insurance Agencies and Brokerages

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as agents (i.e., brokers) in selling annuities and insurance policies.

In the official index: Insurance agencies; Insurance brokerages; Life insurance agencies; Agencies, insurance; Brokerages, insurance; Brokers' offices, insurance

SIC 1987: 6411

524291Claims Adjusting

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in investigating, appraising, and settling insurance claims.

In the official index: Insurance claims adjusting; Insurance claims investigation services; Cause-of-loss investigators, insurance; Claims adjusting, insurance; Insurance settlement offices

SIC 1987: 6411

524292Pharmacy Benefit Management and Other Third Party Administration of Insurance and Pension Funds

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing pharmacy benefit management (PBM) services and other third party administration services of insurance and pension funds, such as claims processing and other administrative services to insurance carriers, employee benefit plans, and self-insurance funds.

In the official index: Insurance claims processing services, third party; Claims processing services, insurance, third party; Insurance fund, third party administrative services (except claims adjusting only); Insurance plan administrative services (except claims adjusting only), third party

SIC 1987: 6371, 6411

524298All Other Insurance Related Activities

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing insurance services on a contract or fee basis (except insurance agencies and brokerages, claims adjusting, and third party administration). Insurance advisory services, insurance actuarial services, and insurance ratemaking services are included in this industry.

In the official index: Insurance advisory services; Insurance actuarial services; Actuarial services, insurance; Insurance coverage consulting services; Insurance exchanges; Insurance investigation services (except claims investigation)…

SIC 1987: 6411

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for an insurance agency?

524210 «Insurance Agencies and Brokerages», with insurance agencies, insurance brokerages and life insurance agencies in the official index.[1]

Do insurance brokers have a different code from agents?

No. The official index lists insurance brokerages in the same code as insurance agencies, 524210 «Insurance Agencies and Brokerages». Claims adjusting is 524291 «Claims Adjusting».[1]
