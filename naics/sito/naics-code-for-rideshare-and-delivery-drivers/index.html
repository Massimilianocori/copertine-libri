NAICS code for rideshare and delivery drivers

Ridesharing and ride hailing are NAICS 485310; restaurant meal and grocery delivery 492210: the official index entries for drivers.

No entry of the official index names Uber, Lyft or DoorDash: the index classifies the service. Carrying passengers by car without regular routes, including ridesharing and ride hailing, is 485310 «Taxi and Ridesharing Services»; delivering meals, groceries and small items locally is 492210 «Local Messengers and Local Delivery». The index lists 28 matching entries in 5 codes.[1],[2]

492210Local Messengers and Local Delivery

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing local messenger and delivery services of small items within a single metropolitan area or within an urban center. These establishments generally provide point-to-point pick-up and delivery and do not operate as part of an intercity courier network.

In the official index: Restaurant meals order and delivery services (i.e., independent order and delivery services); Grocery delivery services (i.e., independent service from grocery store); Delivery service (except as part of intercity courier network, U.S. Postal Service); Alcoholic beverage delivery service; Bicycle courier; Local letter and parcel delivery services (except as part of intercity courier network, U.S. Postal Service)…

SIC 1987: 4215

492110Couriers and Express Delivery Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing air, surface, or combined mode courier and express delivery services of parcels, but not operating under a universal service obligation. These parcels can include goods and documents, but the express delivery services are not part of the normal mail service. These services are generally between metropolitan areas, urban centers, or international, but the establishments of this industry form a network that includes local pick-up and delivery to serve their customers' needs.

In the official index: Express delivery services (except establishments operating under a universal service obligation); Air courier services (except establishments operating under a universal service obligation); Courier services (i.e., intercity network) (except establishments operating under a universal service obligation); Local letter and parcel delivery services as part of intercity courier network

SIC 1987: 4215, 4513

485320Limousine Service

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing an array of specialty and luxury passenger transportation services via limousine or luxury sedan generally on a reserved basis. These establishments do not operate over regular routes and on regular schedules.

In the official index: Limousine services (except shuttle services); Luxury automobiles for hire with driver (except taxis); Automobile rental with driver (except shuttle service, taxis); Limousines for hire with driver (except taxis); Passenger limousine rental with driver (except shuttle service, taxi); Passenger van rental with driver (except shuttle service, taxi)

SIC 1987: 4119

488490Other Support Activities for Road Transportation

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing services (except motor vehicle towing) to road network users.

In the official index: Driving services, independent (e.g., automobile, truck delivery)

SIC 1987: 4173, 4231, 4785

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for an Uber or Lyft driver?

No entry of the official index names Uber or Lyft. Ridesharing services and ride hailing services are listed under 485310 «Taxi and Ridesharing Services», whose official description includes ridesharing and ride hailing services, including arrangement services.[1],[3]

What is the NAICS code for a DoorDash driver?

No entry of the official index names DoorDash. It lists «Restaurant meals order and delivery services (i.e., independent order and delivery services)» and grocery delivery services under 492210 «Local Messengers and Local Delivery».[1]
