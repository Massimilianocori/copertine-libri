NAICS code 332510: Hardware Manufacturing

Official description

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing metal hardware, such as metal hinges, metal handles, keys, and locks (except coin- or card-operated, time locks).

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

  • Manufacturing time locks--are classified in U.S. Industry 334519, Other Measuring and Controlling Device Manufacturing;
