NAICS code for HVAC and plumbing

HVAC and plumbing contractors share NAICS 238220; equipment wholesalers and manufacturers have other codes in the official index.

Installing and servicing plumbing, heating and air-conditioning is one code: 238220 «Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors». The official index lists 106 matching entries in 8 codes; selling or making the equipment is classified in wholesale trade or manufacturing.[1],[2]

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for HVAC?

HVAC contractors are 238220 «Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors», the same code as plumbers. Wholesalers of warm air heating and air-conditioning equipment are 423730 «Warm Air Heating and Air-Conditioning Equipment and Supplies Merchant Wholesalers» and manufacturers of that equipment are 333415 «Air-Conditioning and Warm Air Heating Equipment and Commercial and Industrial Refrigeration Equipment Manufacturing».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for plumbing?

Plumbing contractors are 238220 «Plumbing, Heating, and Air-Conditioning Contractors». Wholesalers of plumbing and heating equipment and supplies are 423720 «Plumbing and Heating Equipment and Supplies (Hydronics) Merchant Wholesalers».[1],[2]
