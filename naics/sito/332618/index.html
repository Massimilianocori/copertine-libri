NAICS code 332618: Other Fabricated Wire Product Manufacturing

Official 2022 title and description, 53 activities from the Census Bureau index, the NAICS 2017 code and the SIC equivalent.

Official description

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing fabricated wire products (except springs) made from purchased wire.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]
