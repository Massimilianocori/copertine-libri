Official description

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of clothing and clothing accessories.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

There is no official SIC line for 424350: the only official Census concordance is between 1987 SIC and 1997 NAICS, and this industry changed after 1997. The SIC codes below are our derivation: we followed the official concordances 2022 → 2017 → 2012 → 2007 → 2002 → 1997 back to 2 NAICS 1997 industries and took their official SIC lines.[5],[6],[7]

Only a piece of each of those 1997 industries is in 424350 (the rest went to other 2022 codes), so each of the 2 SIC codes below matches this industry only in part: read the part descriptions.
