NAICS code for a food truck or catering business

Food trucks and carts are NAICS 722330, caterers 722320, food service contractors 722310: the official index entries for each one.

Food trucks and caterers share industry group 7223 «Special Food Services». The official index lists 23 entries in its 3 codes: food served from vehicles and carts is 722330 «Mobile Food Services», catering for events is 722320 «Caterers» and food services run under contract for an institution are 722310 «Food Service Contractors».[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

The codes to look at first

722330Mobile Food Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in preparing and serving meals and snacks for immediate consumption from motorized vehicles or nonmotorized carts. The establishment is the central location from which the caterer route is serviced, not each vehicle or cart. Included in this industry are establishments primarily engaged in providing food services from vehicles, such as hot dog carts and ice cream trucks.

In the official index: Food carts and trucks, mobile; Lunch wagons; Ice cream truck vendors; Beverage stands, nonalcoholic, mobile; Canteens, mobile; Coffee carts, mobile…

SIC 1987: 5963

722320Caterers

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing single event-based food services. These establishments generally have equipment and vehicles to transport meals and snacks to events and/or prepare food at an off-premise site. Banquet halls with catering staff are included in this industry. Examples of events catered by establishments in this industry are graduation parties, wedding receptions, business or retirement luncheons, and trade shows.

In the official index: Caterers; Catering services, social; Banquet halls with catering staff; Bartender services including supply of equipment and alcohol; Bartender services on own account (serving only)

SIC 1987: 5812

722310Food Service Contractors

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing food services at institutional, governmental, commercial, or industrial locations of others based on contractual arrangements with these types of organizations for a specified period of time. The establishments of this industry provide food services for the convenience of the contracting organization or the contracting organization's customers. The contractual arrangement of these establishments with contracting organizations may vary by type of facility operated (e.g., cafeteria, restaurant, fast-food eating place), revenue sharing, cost structure, and personnel provided. Management staff is always provided by food service contractors.

In the official index: Food service contractors, institutional; Cafeteria food services contractors (e.g., government office cafeterias, hospital cafeterias, school cafeterias); Food concession contractors (e.g., convention facilities, entertainment facilities, sporting facilities); Food service contractors, airline; Food service contractors, cafeteria; Food service contractors, concession operators (e.g., convention facilities, entertainment facilities, sporting facilities)…

SIC 1987: 4789, 5812

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for a food truck?

722330 «Mobile Food Services». The official index lists food carts and trucks, lunch wagons, mobile food stands and ice cream truck vendors there, and the official description says the establishment is the central location from which the route is served, not each vehicle.[1],[3]

What is the NAICS code for catering?

Caterers and social catering services are 722320 «Caterers», which also includes banquet halls with catering staff. Food services provided on a contract basis, for example for hospital or school cafeterias, are 722310 «Food Service Contractors».[1],[2]
