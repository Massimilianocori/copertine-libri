Official description

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing new floor coverings, such as rugs and carpets, laminate and vinyl floor coverings, linoleum flooring, and floor tile (except ceramic tile or hardwood floor coverings only); or retailing new floor coverings in combination with installation and repair services.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

3 activities in the official index

Each line is an entry of the Census Bureau's 2022 NAICS index that is classified in 449121.[2]

  • Carpet stores
  • Floor covering stores (except hardwood or ceramic tile only)
  • Rug stores

Classified elsewhere (cross-references)

Establishments primarily engaged in:

  • Installing floor coverings without retailing new floor coverings--are classified in Industry 238330, Flooring Contractors;
  • Retailing new ceramic floor tile or hardwood floor coverings only--are classified in Industry 444180, Other Building Material Dealers; and
  • Retailing used rugs and carpets--are classified in Industry 459510, Used Merchandise Retailers.

Cross-references from the official 2022 file.[3]
