NAICS code 312111: Soft Drink Manufacturing

Official description

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing soft drinks and artificially carbonated waters.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

Each line is an entry of the Census Bureau's 2022 NAICS index that is classified in 312111.[2]

Classified elsewhere (cross-references)

Establishments primarily engaged in:

  • Canning fruit and vegetable juices--are classified in U.S. Industry 311421, Fruit and Vegetable Canning;
  • Manufacturing fruit syrups for flavoring--are classified in Industry 311930, Flavoring Syrup and Concentrate Manufacturing;
  • Manufacturing nonalcoholic cider--are classified in U.S. Industry 311941, Mayonnaise, Dressing, and Other Prepared Sauce Manufacturing;
  • Purifying and bottling water (except artificially carbonated and flavored water)--are classified in U.S. Industry 312112, Bottled Water Manufacturing;
  • Manufacturing milk-based drinks--are classified in U.S. Industry 311511, Fluid Milk Manufacturing;
  • Manufacturing nonalcoholic beers--are classified in Industry 312120, Breweries; and
  • Manufacturing nonalcoholic wines--are classified in Industry 312130, Wineries.

Cross-references from the official 2022 file.[3]

What other industries send to 312111

Cross-references written in the official descriptions of other codes that name 312111: activities those industries exclude and classify here.[3]

  • In 311421 Fruit and Vegetable Canning: Manufacturing canned fruit and vegetable drinks, cocktails, and ades (except juices)--are classified in U.S. Industry 312111, Soft Drink Manufacturing.
  • In 311920 Coffee and Tea Manufacturing: Establishments primarily engaged in bottling and canning iced coffee or tea are classified in U.S. Industry 312111, Soft Drink Manufacturing.
  • In 312112 Bottled Water Manufacturing: Manufacturing artificially carbonated or flavored waters--are classified in U.S. Industry 312111, Soft Drink Manufacturing; and
  • In 424450 Confectionery Merchant Wholesalers: Manufacturing and bottling soft drinks--are classified in U.S. Industry 312111, Soft Drink Manufacturing.
  • In 424490 Other Grocery and Related Products Merchant Wholesalers: Manufacturing and bottling soft drinks--are classified in U.S. Industry 312111, Soft Drink Manufacturing;
