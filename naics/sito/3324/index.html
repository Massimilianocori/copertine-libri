NAICS 3324: Boiler, Tank, and Shipping Container Manufacturing

4 six-digit codes with official titles and descriptions; subsector 332 «Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing».

Official description

This industry group comprises establishments primarily engaged in one of the following: (1) manufacturing power boilers and heat exchangers; (2) cutting, forming, and joining heavy gauge metal to manufacture tanks, vessels, and other containers; or (3) forming light gauge metal containers.

[1]

Classified elsewhere (cross-references)

Establishments primarily engaged in:

  • Manufacturing foil containers--are classified in Industry 33299, All Other Fabricated Metal Product Manufacturing;
  • Reconditioning barrels and drums--are classified in Industry 81131, Commercial and Industrial Machinery and Equipment (except Automotive and Electronic) Repair and Maintenance; and
  • Manufacturing heavy gauge metal containers--are classified in Industry 33242, Metal Tank (Heavy Gauge) Manufacturing.
