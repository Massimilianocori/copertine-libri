Articles of Organization for a North Carolina LLC cost $125 at the Secretary of State. The LLC annual report is due on April 15th of each year after the year of creation and costs $200 on paper or $203 online.

Registered agentRequired: a registered agent and registered office in North Carolina

The agent must agree to act and keep a physical registered office in the state.

Each domestic and foreign Business Corporation, Nonprofit Corporation, Limited Liability Company, Limited Partnership and Limited Liability Partnership registered on the NC Business Registry is required to have a current Registered Agent and Registered Agent Office of record with the North Carolina Secretary of State’s Office.North Carolina Secretary of State, Registered Office and Registered Agent Information, read on November 14, 2026[3]

The NAICS code of the business

A NAICS code describes what the business does, not its legal form. The NAICS manual says an establishment is classified in an industry when its primary activity meets the definition for that industry.[4] To find it, use the code search or the guides by business activity; before using a code on a form, check with the agency or form that asks for the code.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

How do you start an LLC in North Carolina?

A North Carolina LLC is formed by filing Articles of Organization with the Secretary of State; the fee is $125. The LLC must have a registered agent and registered office of record in North Carolina. This page lists what the state publishes; it is not a step-by-step legal guide.

When is the NC LLC annual report due and how much is it?

The Secretary of State writes that the report of a limited liability company is due on April 15th of each year after the year of creation. The fee is $200 on paper and $203 online; the online amount includes a $3 electronic filing fee for credit cards.

Does a North Carolina LLC need a registered agent?

Yes. Each limited liability company registered on the NC Business Registry is required to have a current registered agent and registered agent office of record with the Secretary of State. The agent must continuously maintain a physical registered office in North Carolina.

What is the North Carolina LLC filing fee?

$125 for the Articles of Organization. Expedited filing costs extra: $100 for filing within 24 hours after receipt, or $200 for filing by the end of the same business day when the document is received by 12:00 noon.
