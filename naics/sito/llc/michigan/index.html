The NAICS code of the business

A NAICS code describes what the business does, not its legal form. The NAICS manual says an establishment is classified in an industry when its primary activity meets the definition for that industry.[4] To find it, use the code search or the guides by business activity; before using a code on a form, check with the agency or form that asks for the code.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

How much does an LLC cost in Michigan?

The Corporations Division fee schedule lists $50 for the Articles of Organization (Form 700). Every LLC then files an Annual Statement each year, due February 15, for which the schedule lists $25.

How do you start an LLC in Michigan?

A Michigan LLC is formed by filing Articles of Organization (Form 700) with the Corporations Division of LARA; the fee is $50. A resident agent and registered office must be included in the articles. This page lists what the state publishes; it is not a step-by-step legal guide.

Does a Michigan LLC need a registered agent?

Yes, under the name resident agent: a resident agent and registered office must be included in the Articles of Organization. The resident agent must be a Michigan resident, a Michigan corporation or LLC, or a foreign corporation or LLC authorized to transact business in Michigan.

Does a Michigan LLC file an annual report?

It files an Annual Statement: every limited liability company is required to file one each year, online, due February 15. An LLC formed or qualified after September 30 is not required to file the statement due on the February 15 immediately following.
