Florida LLC: filing fee, annual report and registered agent

What the Florida Division of Corporations and the other state offices publish, with the official sentence next to each figure.

Filing fee$125
Annual report$138.75, January 1 to May 1
Processing timeIn the order received
Registered agentRequired, Florida street address

Read on the official pages of the state on November 14, 2026. Each figure below is followed by the sentence it comes from.

A new Florida LLC costs $125 to file with the Division of Corporations: a $100 filing fee plus a required $25 registered agent fee. Every year the LLC files an annual report between January 1 and May 1; the fee is $138.75 and a $400 late fee applies after May 1.

Processing timeIn the order received

The Division gives no fixed number of days for new LLCs. It publishes the receipt date it is currently working on, on its Document Processing Dates page.

Filed online with a credit card: Your document will be processed in the order it was received.Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations, Florida Limited Liability Company, read on November 14, 2026[4]

Registered agentRequired: a registered agent with a street address in Florida

The LLC cannot be its own agent, but a person associated with the business can serve. The $25 registered agent fee is part of the filing total.

The registered agent must have a physical street address in Florida. (Do not list a P.O. Box address.)Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations, Instructions for Articles of Organization (FL LLC), read on November 14, 2026[2]
An entity cannot serve as its own registered agent. However, an individual or principal associated with the business may serve as the registered agent.Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations, Instructions for Articles of Organization (FL LLC), read on November 14, 2026[2]

The NAICS code of the business

A NAICS code describes what the business does, not its legal form. The NAICS manual says an establishment is classified in an industry when its primary activity meets the definition for that industry.[5] To find it, use the code search or the guides by business activity; before using a code on a form, check with the agency or form that asks for the code.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

How much does an LLC cost in Florida?

The state total for a new Florida LLC is $125: a $100 filing fee plus a required $25 registered agent fee. Each following year the annual report costs $138.75 if it is filed between January 1 and May 1; after May 1 the fee table shows $538.75.

How do you start an LLC in Florida?

A Florida LLC is formed by filing Articles of Organization with the Division of Corporations; the state total is $125. The articles name a registered agent with a physical street address in Florida, who must sign. This page lists what the state publishes; it is not a step-by-step legal guide.

When is the Florida LLC annual report due?

The filing period is January 1 to May 1 of the calendar year after the LLC was filed. The fee is $138.75. A $400 late fee is imposed after May 1, and an LLC that does not file the report is administratively dissolved.

Does a Florida LLC need a registered agent?

Yes. The registered agent fee is marked as required in the state fee table ($25), and the instructions say the registered agent must have a physical street address in Florida and must sign the application. An entity cannot serve as its own registered agent, but an individual or principal associated with the business may.
