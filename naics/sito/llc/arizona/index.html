Read on the official pages of the state on November 14, 2026. Each figure below is followed by the sentence it comes from.

Articles of Organization for an Arizona LLC cost $50 with regular processing, or $85 expedited, at the Arizona Corporation Commission. Arizona LLCs are not required to file annual reports. Every LLC must appoint and maintain a statutory agent.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

How much does an LLC cost in Arizona?

The Arizona Corporation Commission’s schedule of fees lists $50 for Articles of Organization with regular processing and $85 with expedited processing. LLCs are not required to file annual reports in Arizona.

How do you start an LLC in Arizona?

An Arizona LLC is formed by filing Articles of Organization with the Arizona Corporation Commission; the fee is $50, and regular processing typically takes 14-16 days. Every LLC must appoint a statutory agent, who accepts the designation. This page lists what the state publishes; it is not a step-by-step legal guide.

What is an Arizona statutory agent?

The Commission describes a statutory agent as a person or business that has a physical address in Arizona and accepts service of process or legal documents on behalf of a corporation or LLC. Every corporation and limited liability company is required to appoint and maintain a statutory agent at all times.

Does an Arizona LLC file an annual report?

No. The Arizona Corporation Commission writes that LLCs are not required to file annual reports; only corporations are.
