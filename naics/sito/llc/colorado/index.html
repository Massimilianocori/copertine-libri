Colorado LLC: filing fee, annual report and registered agent

What the Colorado Secretary of State and the other state offices publish, with the official sentence next to each figure.

Read on the official pages of the state on November 14, 2026. Each figure below is followed by the sentence it comes from.

Articles of Organization for a Colorado LLC cost $50 and are filed online with the Secretary of State, in real time. Each year the LLC submits a Periodic Report ($25); the late filing penalty is $50.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

How much does an LLC cost in Colorado?

The Secretary of State’s fee schedule lists $50 for the Articles of Organization of a limited liability company, filed online. The Periodic Report due each year costs $25, and the late filing penalty is $50.

How do you start an LLC in Colorado?

A Colorado LLC is formed by filing Articles of Organization online with the Secretary of State; the fee is $50 and documents submitted online are filed in real time. The entity must list a registered agent with a Colorado address. This page lists what the state publishes; it is not a step-by-step legal guide.

What is the Colorado periodic report?

Reporting entities such as limited liability companies are required by law to submit a Periodic Report each year to the Secretary of State. The fee is $25. It can be filed two months before the entity’s Periodic Report month or two months after without any penalty; the late filing penalty is $50.

Does a Colorado LLC need a registered agent?

Yes. The Secretary of State writes that almost all entities on its record must have a registered agent listed with a Colorado address, even if the business is located in a different state, and that the registered agent must consent to be the registered agent.
