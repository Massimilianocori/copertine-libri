Read on the official pages of the state on November 14, 2026. Each figure below is followed by the sentence it comes from.

The fee for filing the Articles of Organization of a New York LLC is $200. Most LLCs must then publish a notice in two newspapers for six consecutive weeks and file a Certificate of Publication ($50) within 120 days. A Biennial Statement ($9) is due every two years.

The NAICS code of the business

A NAICS code describes what the business does, not its legal form. The NAICS manual says an establishment is classified in an industry when its primary activity meets the definition for that industry.[5] To find it, use the code search or the guides by business activity; before using a code on a form, check with the agency or form that asks for the code.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the New York LLC filing fee?

The Department of State writes that the fee for filing the Articles of Organization is $200. The Certificate of Publication that follows has a $50 filing fee, and the Biennial Statement costs $9 every two years.

What is the New York LLC publication requirement?

Section 206 of the Limited Liability Company Law requires a copy of the Articles of Organization, or a notice about the formation, to be published in two newspapers for six consecutive weeks. A Certificate of Publication is then filed with the Department of State with a $50 fee. Failure to publish and file within 120 days suspends the LLC’s authority to carry on business.

How do you start an LLC in New York?

A New York LLC is formed by filing Articles of Organization with the Department of State; the fee is $200. The articles designate the Secretary of State as agent for service of process, and most LLCs then have to meet the publication requirement within 120 days. This page lists what the state publishes; it is not a step-by-step legal guide.

Does a New York LLC need a registered agent?

What the Department of State requires in the Articles of Organization is that the LLC designate the Secretary of State as its agent for service of process. The Biennial Statement, every two years, gives the address to which the Secretary of State mails a copy of any process.

What is the New York biennial statement?

Domestic and foreign LLCs are required to file a Biennial Statement every two years with the New York Department of State, setting out the address to which the Secretary of State mails a copy of any process accepted on their behalf. The filing fee is $9.
