NAICS code for manufacturing

Manufacturing is NAICS sector 31-33: 21 subsectors by product, from food and apparel to chemicals and machinery, with index examples.

Manufacturing is sector 31-33 «Manufacturing», with 21 subsectors and 346 six-digit industries. The code follows the product made; the table below gives, for each subsector, two industries with an entry of the official index.[1],[2]

The Manufacturing sector comprises establishments engaged in the mechanical, physical, or chemical transformation of materials, substances, or components into new products. The assembling of component parts of manufactured products is considered manufacturing, except in cases where the activity is appropriately classified in Sector 23, Construction.Official description of 31-33 «Manufacturing», 2022 NAICS[3]
Establishments in the Manufacturing sector are often described as plants, factories, or mills and characteristically use power-driven machines and material handling equipment. However, establishments that transform materials or substances into new products by hand or in the worker's home and those engaged in selling to the general public products made on the same premises from which they are sold, such as bakeries, candy stores, and custom tailors, may also be included in this sector. Manufacturing establishments may process materials or may contract with other establishments to process their materials for them. Both types of establishments are included in manufacturing. Selected industries in the Manufacturing sector are comprised solely of establishments that process materials for other establishments on a contract or fee basis. Beyond these dedicated contract manufacturing industries, establishments that process materials for other establishments are generally classified in the Manufacturing industry of the processed materials.Official description of 31-33 «Manufacturing», 2022 NAICS[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for manufacturing?

Manufacturing is sector 31-33 «Manufacturing». The six-digit code depends on the product: for example petroleum refineries are 324110 «Petroleum Refineries» and wood household furniture other than upholstered is 337122 «Nonupholstered Wood Household Furniture Manufacturing». The NAICS manual says the code is assigned on the principal product or group of products produced.[2],[4]
