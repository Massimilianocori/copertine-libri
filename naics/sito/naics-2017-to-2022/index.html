Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What changed in NAICS from 2017 to 2022?

According to the change indicators of the official 2022 structure, 94 six-digit codes are new in 2022, 2 reuse a 2017 code with different content and 15 changed only the title. The sector with the most new six-digit codes is 44-45 «Retail Trade» (47 new codes). Before the revision, the government asked for comments on the treatment of electronic shopping in Retail Trade.[2],[3]

Is a 2017 NAICS code still valid in 2022?

Often yes: 916 of the 1,057 six-digit 2017 codes match exactly one 2022 code with the same number, according to the official concordance. The other 141 were renumbered, split or merged: the table on this page gives the 2022 code or codes for each of them.[1]
