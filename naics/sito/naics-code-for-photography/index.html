NAICS code for photography and video production

Portrait and wedding photography 541921, commercial photography 541922, independent artistic photographers 711510, video 512110.

Photography has two main codes in the official index: portrait work for consumers is 541921 «Photography Studios, Portrait», work for businesses is 541922 «Commercial Photography». The index lists 90 matching entries in 9 codes, including film and video production.[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for photography?

Portrait, wedding and school photography services are 541921 «Photography Studios, Portrait»; commercial, medical and aerial photography services are 541922 «Commercial Photography»; independent artistic photographers are 711510 «Independent Artists, Writers, and Performers».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for video production?

Motion picture and video production, including television commercials, is 512110 «Motion Picture and Video Production». Video taping of special events such as weddings is 541921 «Photography Studios, Portrait», and commercial event videography is 541922 «Commercial Photography».[1]
