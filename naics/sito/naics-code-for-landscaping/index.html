NAICS code for landscaping and lawn care

Landscaping, lawn care and tree services are NAICS 561730; landscape architects 541320; mower repair 811411: the official entries.

Lawn care, landscape maintenance and tree services are 561730 «Landscaping Services». Designing landscapes is a professional service, 541320 «Landscape Architectural Services». The official index lists 52 matching entries in 5 codes.[1],[2]

The codes to look at first

561730Landscaping Services

This industry comprises (1) establishments primarily engaged in providing landscape care and maintenance services and/or installing trees, shrubs, plants, lawns, or gardens and (2) establishments primarily engaged in providing these services along with the design of landscape plans and/or the construction (i.e., installation) of walkways, retaining walls, decks, fences, ponds, and similar structures.

In the official index: Landscape care and maintenance services; Fertilizing lawns; Tree trimming services; Arborist services; Garden maintenance services; Hydroseeding services (e.g., decorative, erosion control purposes)…

SIC 1987: 0782, 0783

541320Landscape Architectural Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in planning and designing the development of land areas for projects, such as parks and other recreational areas; airports; highways; hospitals; schools; land subdivisions; and commercial, industrial, and residential areas, by applying knowledge of land characteristics, location of buildings and structures, use of land areas, and design of landscape projects.

In the official index: Landscape design services; Landscape architects' offices; Architects' offices, landscape; Architects' private practices, landscape; Architectural services, landscape; Garden planning services…

SIC 1987: 0781, 8748

811411Home and Garden Equipment Repair and Maintenance

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in repairing and servicing home and garden equipment without retailing new home and garden equipment, such as lawnmowers, handheld power tools, edgers, snowblowers, leaf blowers, and trimmers.

In the official index: Lawnmower repair and maintenance shops without retailing new lawnmowers; Garden equipment repair and maintenance services without retailing new garden equipment; Garden tool sharpening and repair services; Lawn and garden equipment repair and maintenance services without retailing new lawn and garden equipment; Tool, home and garden, sharpening and repair services without retailing new home and garden tools; Tractor, lawn and garden, repair and maintenance services without retailing new lawn and garden tractors

SIC 1987: 7699

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for landscaping?

561730 «Landscaping Services». The official index lists landscape care and maintenance services, landscape installation services and landscape contractors (except construction) there. Landscape design and landscape architects are 541320 «Landscape Architectural Services».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for lawn care?

Lawn care is in 561730 «Landscaping Services», with entries such as fertilizing lawns. Repair of lawn and garden equipment without retailing new equipment is 811411 «Home and Garden Equipment Repair and Maintenance».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a tree service?

The official index lists tree services (bracing, planting, pruning, removal, spraying, surgery, trimming), arborist services and tree removal services under 561730 «Landscaping Services».[1]
