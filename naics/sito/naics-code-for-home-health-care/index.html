NAICS code for home health care, home care and assisted living

Medical home health care 621610, non-medical home care 624120, assisted living 623312, nursing homes 623110: official NAICS entries.

The official index separates home care by whether it is medical. Home health agencies and home nursing are 621610 «Home Health Care Services»; non-medical home care of the elderly and homemaker services are 624120 «Services for the Elderly and Persons with Disabilities». It lists 26 matching entries in 7 codes, including residential care.[1],[2]

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

623312Assisted Living Facilities for the Elderly

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing residential and personal care services without nursing care for (1) the elderly or other persons who are unable to fully care for themselves and/or (2) the elderly or other persons who do not desire to live independently. The care typically includes room, board, supervision, and assistance in daily living, such as housekeeping services.

In the official index: Assisted living facilities for the elderly without nursing care; Homes for the aged without nursing care; Homes for the elderly without nursing care; Retirement homes without nursing care

SIC 1987: 8361

623110Nursing Care Facilities (Skilled Nursing Facilities)

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing inpatient nursing and rehabilitative services. The care is generally provided for an extended period of time to individuals requiring nursing care. These establishments have a permanent core staff of registered or licensed practical nurses who, along with other staff, provide nursing and continuous personal care services.

In the official index: Nursing homes; Assisted living facilities (without nursing facilities) for the elderly with nursing care; Homes for the aged with nursing care; Homes for the elderly with nursing care; Hospices, inpatient care; Retirement homes with nursing care

SIC 1987: 8051, 8052, 8059

623311Continuing Care Retirement Communities

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing a range of residential and personal care services with on-site nursing care facilities for (1) the elderly and other persons who are unable to fully care for themselves and/or (2) the elderly and other persons who do not desire to live independently. Individuals live in a variety of residential settings with meals, housekeeping, social, leisure, and other services available to assist residents in daily living. Assisted living facilities with on-site nursing care facilities are included in this industry.

In the official index: Assisted living facilities with on-site nursing facilities; Continuing care retirement communities; Retirement communities, continuing care

SIC 1987: 8051, 8052, 8059

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for home health care?

621610 «Home Health Care Services». The official index lists home health agencies, home health care agencies, medical home care of elderly and home nursing services (except private practices) there.[1]

What is the NAICS code for non-medical home care?

The official index lists «Home care of elderly, non-medical» and homemaker's service for elderly or disabled persons under 624120 «Services for the Elderly and Persons with Disabilities», in social assistance rather than health care.[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for assisted living?

It depends on nursing care: assisted living facilities for the elderly without nursing care are 623312 «Assisted Living Facilities for the Elderly»; with nursing care they are 623110 «Nursing Care Facilities (Skilled Nursing Facilities)»; with on-site nursing facilities they are 623311 «Continuing Care Retirement Communities».[1],[2]
