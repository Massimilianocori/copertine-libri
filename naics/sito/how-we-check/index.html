How we check

How we read and check the Census files

Which files we use, when we read them, what we derive, which pages we index and how often we update.

We use only the official files of the U.S. Census Bureau, with their date and fingerprint. Titles, descriptions, index entries and cross-references are shown as published. We do not tell you which code to choose: check with the agency or form that asks for the code.

What we do with the data

  1. Tree. 2,125 codes from the 2–6 digit file, each with its parent (2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 digits); the build stops if a code is missing, doubled or without the right parent, and if the counts differ from the official summary table (20 sectors, 96 subsectors, 308 industry groups, 689 NAICS industries, 1012 six-digit industries).
  2. Descriptions and index. Descriptions, illustrative examples, cross-references and the 20,373 index entries are shown word for word; codes cited in the texts become links.
  3. 2017 codes. From the official 2022 ↔ 2017 concordance, with the change indicators of the 2022 structure.
  4. SIC codes. The Census Bureau publishes one official SIC ↔ NAICS concordance, between 1987 SIC and 1997 NAICS. To reach 2022 we follow the official concordances 1997 → 2002 → 2007 → 2012 → 2017 → 2022. When the 2022 industry has the same code and content since 1997 the official line applies as it is; otherwise the page says the link is our chaining and that a SIC code may cover only a piece of the industry.

Which pages we publish

One page for each sector (20), subsector (96), industry group (308) and six-digit industry (1,012). Five-digit NAICS industries do not get their own page: when they have a single six-digit industry (code ending in 0, same title, same description) the six-digit page covers both; when they have several, they are a section of the industry group page, with their own address (#n- and the code). We ask search engines to index 982 code pages, those with at least 120 groups of words of their own and less than 55% of text in common with the most similar page, plus the shorter six-digit pages that people search for (the code or its activity appears in the search suggestions we saved); the other 454 stay available but are not indexed and carry no ads. «NAICS code for …» pages exist only for activities with measured Google searches.

How often we update

Every month we download the Census files again: if a fingerprint changes we run all the checks. NAICS is revised every five years; when a new edition is published, the site moves to it.

NAICS Code Lookup

NAICS Code Lookup turns the official NAICS 2022 files of the U.S. Census Bureau into readable pages: titles, descriptions, the activities of the official index, the 2017 codes and the 1987 SIC codes. Independent site, not affiliated with the Census Bureau, OMB, the IRS or the SBA.

Codes, titles, descriptions, index entries and concordances: U.S. Census Bureau, North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) 2022 files and the official concordances back to the 1987 SIC (public domain). Official texts are shown without changes; layout, links, counts and the chaining of concordances from 1997 to 2022 are ours.
