Official description

This industry comprises establishments commonly referred to as contractors primarily engaged in (1) cutting materials owned by others for apparel and accessories and/or (2) sewing materials owned by others for apparel and accessories.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

Classified elsewhere (cross-references)

Establishments primarily engaged in:

  • Manufacturing cut and sew apparel from purchased fabric--are classified in Industry 315250, Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing (except Contractors);
  • Manufacturing apparel accessories from purchased fabric--are classified in Industry 315990, Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing; and
  • Embroidering apparel on a contract or fee basis for the trade--are classified in U.S. Industry 314999, All Other Miscellaneous Textile Product Mills.

Cross-references from the official 2022 file.[3]

What other industries send to 315210

Cross-references written in the official descriptions of other codes that name 315210: activities those industries exclude and classify here.[3]

  • In 315250 Cut and Sew Apparel Manufacturing (except Contractors): Cutting and/or sewing materials owned by others for apparel--are classified in Industry 315210, Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors;
  • In 315990 Apparel Accessories and Other Apparel Manufacturing: Cutting and/or sewing materials owned by others for apparel and apparel accessories--are classified in Industry 315210, Cut and Sew Apparel Contractors;

SIC codes: no single equivalent

There is no official SIC line for 315210: the only official Census concordance is between 1987 SIC and 1997 NAICS, and this industry changed after 1997. The SIC codes below are our derivation: we followed the official concordances 2022 → 2017 → 2012 → 2007 → 2002 → 1997 back to 2 NAICS 1997 industries and took their official SIC lines.[4],[5],[6]

Only a piece of each of those 1997 industries is in 315210 (the rest went to other 2022 codes), so each of the 25 SIC codes below matches this industry only in part: read the part descriptions.
