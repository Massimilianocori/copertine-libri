NAICS code 333120: Construction Machinery Manufacturing

Official 2022 title and description, 60 activities from the Census Bureau index, the NAICS 2017 code and the SIC equivalent.

Official description

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in manufacturing construction machinery, surface mining machinery, and logging equipment.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

Classified elsewhere (cross-references)

Establishments primarily engaged in:

  • Manufacturing drilling and underground mining machinery and equipment--are classified in Industry 33313, Mining and Oil and Gas Field Machinery Manufacturing;
  • Manufacturing industrial plant overhead traveling cranes and hoists, truck-type cranes and hoists, winches, aerial work platforms, and automotive wrecker hoists--are classified in Industry 33392, Material Handling Equipment Manufacturing; and
  • Manufacturing rail layers, ballast distributors, and other railroad track-laying equipment--are classified in Industry 336510, Railroad Rolling Stock Manufacturing.

Cross-references from the official 2022 file.[3]

What other industries send to 333120

Cross-references written in the official descriptions of other codes that name 333120: activities those industries exclude and classify here.[3]

  • In 33311 Agricultural Implement Manufacturing: Manufacturing forestry machinery and equipment, such as brush, limb, and log chippers; log splitters; and construction equipment--are classified in Industry 33312, Construction Machinery Manufacturing.
  • In 333111 Farm Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing: Manufacturing construction-type tractors--are classified in Industry 333120, Construction Machinery Manufacturing.
  • In 33313 Mining and Oil and Gas Field Machinery Manufacturing: Manufacturing surface mining machinery and equipment--are classified in Industry 33312, Construction Machinery Manufacturing;
  • In 333131 Mining Machinery and Equipment Manufacturing: Manufacturing surface mining machinery and equipment--are classified in Industry 333120, Construction Machinery Manufacturing;
  • In 33392 Material Handling Equipment Manufacturing: Manufacturing construction-type tractors and cranes--are classified in Industry 33312, Construction Machinery Manufacturing; and
  • In 333923 Overhead Traveling Crane, Hoist, and Monorail System Manufacturing: Manufacturing construction-type cranes--are classified in Industry 333120, Construction Machinery Manufacturing;
  • In 333924 Industrial Truck, Tractor, Trailer, and Stacker Machinery Manufacturing: Manufacturing construction-type tractors--are classified in Industry 333120, Construction Machinery Manufacturing.
  • In 33399 All Other General Purpose Machinery Manufacturing: Manufacturing power-driven heavy construction or mining hand operated tools, such as tampers, jackhammers, and augers--are classified in Industry 33312, Construction Machinery Manufacturing, or Industry 33313, Mining and Oil and Gas Field Machinery Manufacturing;
  • In 333991 Power-Driven Handtool Manufacturing: Manufacturing power-driven heavy construction or mining hand operated tools, such as tampers, jackhammers, and augers--are classified in Industry 333120, Construction Machinery Manufacturing, or Industry 33313, Mining and Oil and Gas Field Machinery Manufacturing; and
  • In 336120 Heavy Duty Truck Manufacturing: Manufacturing off-highway construction equipment--are classified in Industry 333120, Construction Machinery Manufacturing.
