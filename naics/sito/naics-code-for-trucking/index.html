NAICS code for trucking

Trucking is NAICS subsector 484: general freight local or long-distance (TL, LTL), specialized freight, moving, independent drivers.

Trucking is subsector 484 «Truck Transportation». The code depends on three things in the official titles: general or specialized freight, local or long-distance, and for long-distance general freight whether loads are truckload (TL) or less-than-truckload (LTL). The index lists 73 trucking entries in 8 codes.[1],[2]

The codes to look at first

484110General Freight Trucking, Local

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing local general freight trucking. General freight trucking establishments handle a wide variety of commodities, generally palletized and transported in a container or van trailer. Local general freight trucking establishments usually provide trucking within a metropolitan area which may cross state lines. Generally the trips are same-day return.

In the official index: General freight trucking, local; Container trucking services, local; Bulk mail truck transportation, contract, local; Motor freight carrier, general, local; Transfer (trucking) services, general freight, local; Trucking, general freight, local

SIC 1987: 4212, 4214

484121General Freight Trucking, Long-Distance, Truckload

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing long-distance general freight truckload (TL) trucking. These long-distance general freight truckload carrier establishments provide full truck movement of freight from origin to destination. The shipment of freight on a truck is characterized as a full single load not combined with other shipments.

In the official index: General freight trucking, long-distance, truckload (TL); Bulk mail truck transportation, contract, long-distance (TL); Container trucking services, long-distance (TL); Motor freight carrier, general, long-distance, truckload (TL); Trucking, general freight, long-distance, truckload (TL)

SIC 1987: 4213

484122General Freight Trucking, Long-Distance, Less Than Truckload

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing long-distance, general freight, less than truckload (LTL) trucking. LTL carriage is characterized as multiple shipments combined onto a single truck for multiple deliveries within a network. These establishments are generally characterized by the following network activities: local pick-up, local sorting and terminal operations, line-haul, destination sorting and terminal operations, and local delivery.

In the official index: General freight trucking, long-distance, less-than-truckload (LTL); LTL (less-than-truckload) long-distance freight trucking; Motor freight carrier, general, long-distance, less-than-truckload (LTL); Trucking, general freight, long-distance, less-than-truckload (LTL)

SIC 1987: 4213

484210Used Household and Office Goods Moving

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing local or long-distance trucking of used household, used institutional, or used commercial furniture and equipment. Incidental packing and storage activities are often provided by these establishments.

In the official index: Used household and office goods moving; Furniture moving, used; Van lines, moving and storage services; Motor freight carrier, used household goods; Trucking used household, office, or institutional furniture and equipment

SIC 1987: 4212, 4213, 4214

484230Specialized Freight (except Used Goods) Trucking, Long-Distance

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing long-distance specialized trucking. These establishments provide trucking between metropolitan areas that may cross North American country borders.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for trucking?

General freight trucking is 484110 «General Freight Trucking, Local» when local; long-distance it is 484121 «General Freight Trucking, Long-Distance, Truckload» or 484122 «General Freight Trucking, Long-Distance, Less Than Truckload». Specialized freight such as dump, flatbed, bulk liquids or livestock trucking is 484220 «Specialized Freight (except Used Goods) Trucking, Local» (local) or 484230 «Specialized Freight (except Used Goods) Trucking, Long-Distance» (long-distance).[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for an independent truck driver?

The official index lists «Independent truck driver (except owner-operators)» under 488490 «Other Support Activities for Road Transportation». Owner-operators are excluded from that entry; the official description of subsector 484 «Truck Transportation» covers over-the-road transportation of cargo using motor vehicles, such as trucks and tractor trailers.[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a moving company?

484210 «Used Household and Office Goods Moving». The official index lists used household and office goods moving, used furniture moving and van lines offering moving and storage services there.[1]
