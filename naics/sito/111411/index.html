NAICS code 111411: Mushroom Production

Official description

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in growing mushrooms under cover in mines underground, or in other controlled environments.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

3 activities in the official index

Each line is an entry of the Census Bureau's 2022 NAICS index that is classified in 111411.[2]

  • Mushroom spawn farming
  • Shitake mushroom farming
