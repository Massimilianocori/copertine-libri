NAICS code for real estate

Real estate is NAICS subsector 531: agents and brokers 531210, property managers 531311/531312, appraisers 531320, lessors 5311.

Real estate is subsector 531 «Real Estate». The official 2022 index lists 69 real estate entries in 9 of its codes. The code follows what you do: sell or buy property for others, manage it, appraise it, or own it and rent it out.[1],[2]

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

531210Offices of Real Estate Agents and Brokers

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as agents and/or brokers in one or more of the following: (1) selling real estate for others; (2) buying real estate for others; and (3) renting real estate for others.

531390Other Activities Related to Real Estate

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in performing real estate related services (except lessors of real estate, offices of real estate agents and brokers, real estate property managers, and offices of real estate appraisers).

In the official index: Real estate escrow agencies; Real estate listing services; Real estate consultants' (except agents, appraisers) offices; Agencies, real estate escrow; Agents' offices, real estate escrow; Consultants', real estate (except appraisers), offices…

SIC 1987: 6531

531110Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of buildings used as residences or dwellings, such as single-family homes, apartment buildings, and town homes. Included in this industry are owner-lessors and establishments renting real estate and then acting as lessors in subleasing it to others. The establishments in this industry may manage the property themselves or have another establishment manage it for them.

In the official index: Real estate rental or leasing of residential building; Equity real estate investment trusts (REITs), primarily leasing residential buildings and dwellings; Lessors of residential buildings and dwellings

SIC 1987: 6513, 6514

531120Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings (except Miniwarehouses)

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of buildings (except miniwarehouses and self-storage units) that are not used as residences or dwellings. Included in this industry are: (1) owner-lessors of nonresidential buildings; (2) establishments renting real estate and then acting as lessors in subleasing it to others; and (3) establishments providing full service office space, whether on a lease or service contract basis. The establishments in this industry may manage the property themselves or have another establishment manage it for them.

In the official index: Real estate rental or leasing of nonresidential building (except miniwarehouse); Equity real estate investment trusts (REITs), primarily leasing nonresidential buildings (except miniwarehouses); Lessors of nonresidential buildings (except miniwarehouses)

SIC 1987: 6512

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for real estate?

Real estate is subsector 531 «Real Estate». Real estate agents and brokers are 531210 «Offices of Real Estate Agents and Brokers»; property managers are 531311 «Residential Property Managers» or 531312 «Nonresidential Property Managers»; appraisers are 531320 «Offices of Real Estate Appraisers»; owners renting out buildings are in industry group 5311 «Lessors of Real Estate».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a real estate agent?

531210 «Offices of Real Estate Agents and Brokers». The official index lists real estate agents' offices, real estate agencies and brokerages, buyers' agents and commercial real estate agents there. The IRS instructions for Schedule C use 531210 as their example of a code.[1],[4]
