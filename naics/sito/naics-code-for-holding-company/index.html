NAICS code for a holding company

Bank holding companies are NAICS 551111, other holding companies 551112, holding companies that manage 551114: official index and texts.

Holding companies are in subsector 551 «Management of Companies and Enterprises», split into 3 codes. The official index lists 16 entries; the difference between 551112 «Offices of Other Holding Companies» and 551114 «Corporate, Subsidiary, and Regional Managing Offices» is whether the company manages the businesses whose securities it holds.[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

This U.S. industry comprises establishments (except government establishments) primarily engaged in administering, overseeing, and managing other establishments of the company or enterprise. These establishments normally undertake the strategic or organizational planning and decision-making role of the company or enterprise. Establishments in this industry may hold the securities of the company or enterprise.Official description of 551114 «Corporate, Subsidiary, and Regional Managing Offices», 2022 NAICS[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for a holding company?

Bank holding companies that do not manage are 551111 «Offices of Bank Holding Companies»; other holding companies that do not manage, including personal, financial and insurance holding companies, are 551112 «Offices of Other Holding Companies»; holding companies that manage are 551114 «Corporate, Subsidiary, and Regional Managing Offices».[1],[2]

What does NAICS code 551112 mean?

551112 is «Offices of Other Holding Companies». Its official description covers legal entities that hold the securities of other companies to own a controlling interest or influence management decisions, without administering, overseeing and managing the other establishments.[3]
