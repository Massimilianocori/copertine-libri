NAICS code for cleaning services

Which NAICS code fits a cleaning business: janitorial, maid, carpet, window, chimney and laundry services, from the official 2022 index.

Cleaning services are spread over several NAICS codes, depending on what is cleaned. The official 2022 index lists 119 cleaning activities in 36 codes; cleaning of buildings and homes is in industry group 5617 «Services to Buildings and Dwellings».[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

561720Janitorial Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in cleaning building interiors, interiors of transportation equipment (e.g., aircraft, rail cars, ships), and/or windows.

In the official index: Building cleaning services, interior; Building cleaning services, janitorial; Cleaning homes; Cleaning offices; Cleaning shopping centers; Housekeeping services (i.e., cleaning services)…

561740Carpet and Upholstery Cleaning Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in cleaning and dyeing used rugs, carpets, and upholstery.

561790Other Services to Buildings and Dwellings

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing services to buildings and dwellings (except exterminating and pest control; janitorial; landscaping care and maintenance; and carpet and upholstery cleaning).

In the official index: Building exterior cleaning services (except sandblasting, window cleaning); Chimney cleaning services; Chimney sweep (i.e., cleaning) services; Cleaning (e.g., power sweeping, washing) driveways and parking lots; Cleaning building exteriors (except sandblasting, window cleaning); Cleaning swimming pools…

SIC 1987: 7349, 7389, 7699

812320Drycleaning and Laundry Services (except Coin-Operated)

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in one or more of the following: (1) providing drycleaning services (except coin- or card-operated); (2) providing laundering services (except linen and uniform supply or coin- or card-operated); (3) providing drop-off and pick-up sites for laundries and/or drycleaners; and (4) providing specialty cleaning services for specific types of garments and other textile items (except carpets and upholstery), such as fur, leather, or suede garments; wedding gowns; hats; draperies; and pillows. These establishments may provide all, a combination of, or none of the cleaning services on the premises.

In the official index: Cleaning and dyeing plants (except rug cleaning plants); Curtain cleaning services; Drapery cleaning services; Drycleaning plants (except rug cleaning plants); Fur garment cleaning services; Garment cleaning (e.g., fur, leather, suede) services…

SIC 1987: 7211, 7212, 7216, 7219, 7389

562991Septic Tank and Related Services

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) pumping (i.e., cleaning) septic tanks and cesspools and/or (2) renting and/or servicing portable toilets.

In the official index: Cesspool cleaning services; Portable toilet pumping (i.e., cleaning) services; Pumping (i.e., cleaning) cesspools and septic tanks; Pumping (i.e., cleaning) portable toilets; Septic tank cleaning services; Septic tank pumping (i.e., cleaning) services…

562998All Other Miscellaneous Waste Management Services

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing waste management services (except waste collection, waste treatment and disposal, remediation, operation of materials recovery facilities, septic tank pumping and related services, and waste management consulting services).

In the official index: Beach maintenance and cleaning services; Catch basin cleaning services; Grease trap cleaning; Lake maintenance and cleaning services; Pond maintenance and cleaning services; Reservoir maintenance and cleaning services…

SIC 1987: 4959, 7699

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for cleaning services?

It depends on what is cleaned. Building interiors and homes (janitorial, maid and housekeeping services) are 561720 «Janitorial Services»; carpets and upholstery 561740 «Carpet and Upholstery Cleaning Services»; building exteriors, chimneys and other services to dwellings 561790 «Other Services to Buildings and Dwellings»; clothes and linens 812320 «Drycleaning and Laundry Services (except Coin-Operated)».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for janitorial services?

561720 «Janitorial Services». The official index lists janitorial services, office cleaning services and housekeeping services there.[1]

What is the NAICS code for pressure washing?

The official index lists «Pressure washing (e.g., buildings, decks, fences)», power washing and steam cleaning of building exteriors under 561790 «Other Services to Buildings and Dwellings».[1]
