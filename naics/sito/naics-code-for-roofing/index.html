NAICS code for roofing

Roofing contractors are NAICS 238160; gutters 238170, roofing material wholesalers 423330, shingle makers 324122: official entries.

Roof installation and repair is 238160 «Roofing Contractors». The official index lists 52 roof-related entries in 13 codes; gutters, materials and roof consulting each have their own code.[1],[2]

The codes to look at first

238160Roofing Contractors

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in roofing. This industry also includes establishments treating roofs (i.e., spraying, painting, or coating) and installing skylights. The work performed may include new work, additions, alterations, maintenance, and repairs.

In the official index: Asphalt roof shingle installation; Roof painting, spraying, or coating; Copper roofing installation; Corrugated metal roofing installation; Galvanized iron roofing installation; Low slope roofing installation…

SIC 1987 (partial match): 1761

238170Siding Contractors

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in installing siding of wood, aluminum, vinyl, or other exterior finish material (except brick, stone, stucco, or curtain wall). This industry also includes establishments installing gutters and downspouts. The work performed may include new work, additions, alterations, maintenance, and repairs.

In the official index: Gutters, seamless roof, formed and installed on site; Gutter and downspout contractors; Downspout, gutter, and gutter guard installation; Ice apron, roof, installation

SIC 1987 (partial match): 1761

423330Roofing, Siding, and Insulation Material Merchant Wholesalers

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in the merchant wholesale distribution of nonwood roofing and nonwood siding and insulation materials.

In the official index: Roofing materials (except wood) merchant wholesalers; Asphalt roofing shingles merchant wholesalers; Ceramic and clay roofing materials merchant wholesalers; Gutters and down spouts (except wood) merchant wholesalers; Sheet metal roofing materials merchant wholesalers; Shingles (except wood) merchant wholesalers

SIC 1987 (partial match): 5033

324122Asphalt Shingle and Coating Materials Manufacturing

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) saturating purchased mats and felts with asphalt or tar from purchased asphaltic materials and (2) manufacturing asphalt and tar and roofing cements and coatings from purchased asphaltic materials.

In the official index: Asphalt shingles made from purchased asphaltic materials; Asphalt roofing cements made from purchased asphaltic materials; Asphalt roofing coatings made from purchased asphaltic materials; Cements, asphalt roofing, made from purchased asphaltic materials; Pitch, roofing, made from purchased asphaltic materials; Roofing cements, asphalt, made from purchased asphaltic materials…

SIC 1987: 2952

541690Other Scientific and Technical Consulting Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing advice and assistance to businesses and other organizations on scientific and technical issues (except environmental).

In the official index: Roof consulting services

SIC 1987: 0781, 8748, 8999

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for roofing?

Roofing contractors are 238160 «Roofing Contractors», including roof shingle installation and roof painting, spraying or coating. Gutter and downspout contractors are 238170 «Siding Contractors».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a roofing contractor?

Roofing contractors are 238160 «Roofing Contractors». Selling roofing materials is a different industry: merchant wholesalers of roofing materials (except wood) and asphalt roofing shingles are 423330 «Roofing, Siding, and Insulation Material Merchant Wholesalers».[1]
