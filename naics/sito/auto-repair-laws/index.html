Repair shops and NAICS codes

A NAICS code describes the activity of the business and has nothing to do with these state rules. The official index puts most car repair work under 811111 (General Automotive Repair) and 811198 (All Other Automotive Repair and Maintenance); the guide NAICS code for auto repair has the index entries. Before using a code on a form, check with the agency or form that asks for the code.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

Can a mechanic charge more than the estimate?

It depends on the state, and each state sets its own margin. In the official texts read on November 19, 2026, the customer’s authorization is needed, state by state. Florida: to go past the estimate by more than $10 or 10 percent, whichever is greater (at most $50); California: before any repair and before any additional repair; Michigan: to exceed the estimate by more than 10% or $50.00, whichever is lesser; New York: for every service (work order) and to exceed the estimate; Washington: to go more than ten percent over the written estimate; Minnesota: to charge more than 110 percent of the estimate. Each state page quotes the sentence.

Do you need a license to be a mechanic or to open a repair shop?

The rules read for these pages are about the shop, not the individual mechanic. In the official texts read on November 19, 2026. Florida: state registration, $50 to $300 a year per location; California: bAR registration, $200 and $200 a year to renew; Michigan: state registration, number on every document; New York: dMV repair shop registration; Washington: none specific to repair shops in the chapter read; Minnesota: not in the sections read. Requirements for individual mechanics, local business licenses and tax registrations are outside the pages we read.

Is there an auto repair invoice template that follows state law?

No single template fits every state, because each state lists different items: the checklists on the state pages show the items word for word, with the section they come from, and can be printed and compared with the form a shop already uses. A form is not checked or approved by this site.

What has to be on an auto repair estimate?

Each state has its own list. In the states covered here the lists share a core: who the shop and the customer are, the vehicle, the problem or the work requested, and the price of parts and labor. Some states add statements that must be printed word for word. The state checklists give every item with its section.
