Florida auto repair laws: estimate, invoice and shop rules

Read in the official texts on November 19, 2026. Each rule below is followed by the sentence it comes from.

In Florida the rules for repair shops are in the Florida Motor Vehicle Repair Act, part of chapter 559 of the Florida Statutes. A shop prepares a written estimate when the repair will cost the customer more than $150, gives an invoice when any repair is completed, and registers with the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services before doing business.

What Florida requires from a repair shop, in the official words

Written estimateWritten estimate when the repair work will cost more than $150

The estimate is a form with a list of required items, prepared before any diagnostic work or repair. The customer can decline it only by checking and signing the statement the statute prints in capital letters.

When any customer requests a motor vehicle repair shop to perform repair work on a motor vehicle, the cost of which repair work will exceed $150 to the customer, the shop shall prepare a written repair estimate, which is a form setting forth the estimated cost of repair work, including diagnostic work, before effecting any diagnostic work or repair.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.905, Written motor vehicle repair estimate and disclosure statement required, read on November 19, 2026[1]
Except as provided in subsection (5), a copy of the written repair estimate required by subsection (1) and the disclosure statement required by subsection (2) shall be given to the customer before repair work is begun.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.905, Written motor vehicle repair estimate and disclosure statement required, read on November 19, 2026[1]

Customer authorizationCustomer approval before charging more than the estimate plus $10 or 10 percent, whichever is greater, up to $50

The shop notifies the customer of the additional work and its estimated cost; the customer authorizes, modifies or cancels the order, orally or in writing.

It shall be unlawful for a motor vehicle repair shop to charge more than the written estimate plus $10 or 10 percent, whichever is greater, but not to exceed $50, unless the motor vehicle repair shop has obtained authorization to exceed the written estimate in accordance with subsection (1).The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.909, Notification of charges in excess of repair estimate; inspection of parts, read on November 19, 2026[2]
… the customer shall be promptly notified by telephone, telegraph, mail, or other means of the additional repair work and estimated cost thereof. A customer so notified shall, orally or in writing, authorize, modify, or cancel the order for repair.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.909, Notification of charges in excess of repair estimate; inspection of parts, read on November 19, 2026[2]

InvoiceA legible invoice when any repair is completed

The invoice can be on the same form as the written estimate. Its required contents are in the checklist below, including the shop registration number.

The motor vehicle repair shop shall provide each customer, upon completion of any repair, with a legible copy of an invoice for such repair. The invoice may be provided on the same form as the written repair estimate and shall include the following information:The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.911, Invoice required of motor vehicle repair shop, read on November 19, 2026[3]

Replaced partsInspection or return of removed parts, if the customer asks when the work is authorized

The estimate form carries a statement that lets the customer say whether replaced parts are to be saved. Return does not apply where the shop has a warranty arrangement or an exchange parts program.

Upon request made at the time the repair work is authorized by the customer, the customer is entitled to inspect parts removed from her or his vehicle or, if the shop has no warranty arrangement or exchange parts program with a manufacturer, supplier, or distributor, have them returned to her or him.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.909, Notification of charges in excess of repair estimate; inspection of parts, read on November 19, 2026[2]

Shop registrationRegistration with the department before doing business: $50, $150 or $300 a year for each location

The fee depends on how many employees perform repairs at the place of business (1 to 5, 6 to 10, 11 or more) and the registration is renewed biennially. The statute exempts an individual with no employees and no established place of business.

Each motor vehicle repair shop engaged or attempting to engage in the business of motor vehicle repair work must register with the department prior to doing business in this state.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.904, Motor vehicle repair shop registration; application; exemption, read on November 19, 2026[4]
If the place of business has 1 to 5 employees who perform repairs: $50.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.904, Motor vehicle repair shop registration; application; exemption, read on November 19, 2026[4]
If the place of business has 6 to 10 employees who perform repairs: $150.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.904, Motor vehicle repair shop registration; application; exemption, read on November 19, 2026[4]
If the place of business has 11 or more employees who perform repairs: $300.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.904, Motor vehicle repair shop registration; application; exemption, read on November 19, 2026[4]
Each registration must be renewed biennially on or before the expiration date of the current registration.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.904, Motor vehicle repair shop registration; application; exemption, read on November 19, 2026[4]
No registration application or fee is required for an individual with no employees and no established place of business.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.904, Motor vehicle repair shop registration; application; exemption, read on November 19, 2026[4]

RecordsEstimates and invoices kept and open to the customer for at least 12 months

The statute speaks of the customer’s right to inspect and copy the records; department staff can inspect them during business hours.

Each motor vehicle repair shop shall maintain repair records which shall include written repair estimates and repair invoices. A customer’s records shall be available to the customer for inspection and copying for a period of at least 12 months.The Florida Legislature, Florida Statutes § 559.915, Motor vehicle repair shop records, read on November 19, 2026[5]

On the written estimate

  • The name, address, and telephone number of the motor vehicle repair shop.§ 559.905(1)(a)[1]
  • The name, address, and telephone number of the customer.§ 559.905(1)(b)[1]
  • The date and time of the written repair estimate.§ 559.905(1)(c)[1]
  • The year, make, model, odometer reading, and license tag number of the motor vehicle.§ 559.905(1)(d)[1]
  • The proposed work completion date.§ 559.905(1)(e)[1]
  • A general description of the customer’s problem or request for repair work or service relating to the motor vehicle.§ 559.905(1)(f)[1]
  • A statement as to whether the customer is being charged according to a flat rate or an hourly rate, or both.§ 559.905(1)(g)[1]
  • The estimated cost of repair which must include any charge for shop supplies or for hazardous or other waste removal and, if a charge is included, the estimate must include the following statement: “This charge represents costs and profits to the motor vehicle repair facility for miscellaneous shop supplies or waste disposal.”§ 559.905(1)(h)[1]
  • If a charge is mandated by state or federal law, the estimate must contain a statement identifying the law and the specific amount charged under the law.§ 559.905(1)(h)[1]
  • The charge for making a repair price estimate or, if the charge cannot be predetermined, the basis on which the charge will be calculated.§ 559.905(1)(i)[1]
  • The customer’s intended method of payment.§ 559.905(1)(j)[1]
  • The name and telephone number of another person who may authorize repair work, if the customer desires to designate such person.§ 559.905(1)(k)[1]
  • A statement indicating what, if anything, is guaranteed in connection with the repair work and the time and mileage period for which the guarantee is effective.§ 559.905(1)(l)[1]
  • A statement allowing the customer to indicate whether replaced parts should be saved for inspection or return.§ 559.905(1)(m)[1]
  • A statement indicating the daily charge for storing the customer’s motor vehicle after the customer has been notified that the repair work has been completed.§ 559.905(1)(n)[1]
  • If the cost of repair work will exceed $150, the shop must present to the customer a written notice conspicuously disclosing, in a separate, blocked section, only the following statement, in capital letters of at least 12-point type:§ 559.905(2)[1]

On the invoice

  • The current date and odometer reading of the motor vehicle.§ 559.911(1)[3]
  • A statement indicating what was done to correct the problem or a description of the service provided.§ 559.911(2)[3]

The shop itself

  • Each motor vehicle repair shop engaged or attempting to engage in the business of motor vehicle repair work must register with the department prior to doing business in this state.§ 559.904(1)[4]
  • Each initial application for registration must be accompanied by copies of the applicant’s estimate and invoice forms.§ 559.904(4)[4]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What does the Florida Motor Vehicle Repair Act require from a repair shop?

The sections read for this page require a shop to register with the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services before doing business, to prepare a written estimate when repair work will cost the customer more than $150, to get the customer’s authorization before charging more than the estimate plus $10 or 10 percent, whichever is greater, but not more than $50, and to give a legible invoice when any repair is completed.

How much is the Florida motor vehicle repair shop registration?

The statute sets the registration fee for each location on a per-year basis: $50 if the place of business has 1 to 5 employees who perform repairs, $150 with 6 to 10, and $300 with 11 or more. Each registration is renewed biennially. The statute calls it a registration, not a license.

Can a mechanic charge more than the estimate in Florida?

The statute makes it unlawful for a motor vehicle repair shop to charge more than the written estimate plus $10 or 10 percent, whichever is greater, but not to exceed $50, unless the shop has obtained authorization to exceed the written estimate. The customer is notified of the additional repair work and its estimated cost and authorizes, modifies or cancels the order.
