Michigan’s rules are in the Motor Vehicle Service and Repair Act. A repair facility registers with the state, gives the customer a written, itemized estimate before beginning repair work, returns replaced parts, and hands over a written statement when the repaired vehicle goes back to the customer.

What Michigan requires from a repair shop, in the official words

Written estimateWritten, itemized estimate before beginning repair work, unless the customer signs the waiver printed in the act

The waiver is a fixed text in 14 point or larger bold capital type, with the amount the customer agrees to pay without an estimate. The cost of a diagnosis goes in the written estimate before the diagnosis is made.

Before beginning repair work, a motor vehicle repair facility shall give to the customer a written estimate that itemizes as closely as possible the price for labor and parts necessary for the work.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1332, Written estimate required; waiver; registration number on documents, read on November 19, 2026[1]
The waiver must be in 14 point or larger bold capital type face and executed with 1 copy to the customer who is requesting the repairs.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1332, Written estimate required; waiver; registration number on documents, read on November 19, 2026[1]

Customer authorizationWritten or oral consent before charging above the estimate; none needed for an excess of not more than 10% or $50.00, whichever is lesser

The small-excess rule applies when no waiver was signed and unless the customer specifically asked to be consulted. With a waiver, the limit is the amount the customer stated.

A facility shall not charge for work done or parts supplied in excess of the estimated price, or in excess of the limit stated by the customer in the waiver described in subsection (3), without the knowing written or oral consent of the customerMichigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1332, Written estimate required; waiver; registration number on documents, read on November 19, 2026[1]
If a waiver is not signed under subsection (3) and the estimated price is exceeded by not more than 10% or $50.00, whichever is lesser, the facility is not required to obtain the written or oral consent of the customer for the excess charge unless specifically requested by the customer.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1332, Written estimate required; waiver; registration number on documents, read on November 19, 2026[1]

InvoiceA written statement to each customer when the repaired vehicle is returned

The act does not call it an invoice. It is signed for the facility and names the mechanic or mechanics who did the diagnosis and the repair.

… shall give to each customer a written statement upon return of the repaired vehicle to the customerMichigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1334, Written statement required upon return of repaired vehicle, read on November 19, 2026[2]
The statement shall be signed by the owner of the facility or by a person designated by the owner to represent the facility. The name of the mechanic or mechanics who performed the diagnosis and the repair shall also appear on the statement.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1334, Written statement required upon return of repaired vehicle, read on November 19, 2026[2]

Replaced partsReplaced parts returned to the customer when the repair work is completed, with a printed notice of that right

The exceptions are parts exempted for size or weight, warranty or exchange parts, and containers that held flammable fuels. The notice goes on the work order or on a sign with lettering not less than 1 inch high.

A motor vehicle repair facility shall return replaced parts to the customer at the time the repair work is completed.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1333, Right to receive or inspect replaced parts, read on November 19, 2026[3]
A facility that displays the notice described in subsection (2) on a clearly legible sign with lettering not less than 1 inch high, conspicuously displayed in the part of the facility where customers routinely contract for repairs, is not required to provide the notice to a customer in the form of a document described in subsection (2).Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1333, Right to receive or inspect replaced parts, read on November 19, 2026[3]

Shop registrationRegistration of the facility with the state administrator; registration number on every document used with the public

In the act the administrator is the secretary of state. The certificate of registration is displayed where customers can see it. Fees are not in the sections we read.

Unless the act or practice is otherwise exempt by this act, a person shall not engage in the business or activity of a motor vehicle repair facility unless the person registers the facility with the administrator pursuant to this act.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1306, Motor vehicle repair facility; registration required, read on November 19, 2026[4]
"Administrator" means the secretary of state or any individual designated by the secretary of state to act on behalf of the secretary of state.Michigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1302, Definitions, read on November 19, 2026[5]
A motor vehicle repair facility shall include the facility's registration number, as assigned by the administrator, on each copy of any instrument, form, contract, or other document used by the applicant in dealing with the public in the repair of motor vehiclesMichigan Legislature, Michigan Compiled Laws § 257.1332, Written estimate required; waiver; registration number on documents, read on November 19, 2026[1]

Not in the sections we read: how long records are kept. That means we did not find it there, not that none applies: ask the state.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What does the Michigan Motor Vehicle Service and Repair Act require from a repair facility?

In the sections read for this page, the act requires a facility to be registered, to give a written, itemized estimate before beginning repair work, to obtain the customer’s written or oral consent before charging more than the estimate (with a small-excess rule of not more than 10% or $50.00, whichever is lesser), to return replaced parts, and to give a written statement of the repairs when the vehicle is returned.

Does a repair shop in Michigan need a repair facility license?

The act speaks of registration: a person shall not engage in the business or activity of a motor vehicle repair facility unless the person registers the facility with the administrator, unless the act exempts the activity. The facility displays its certificate of registration and puts its registration number on every document used with the public. The fee is not in the sections we read.

Can a mechanic charge more than the estimate in Michigan?

Not without the knowing written or oral consent of the customer, obtained after the facility finds the estimate insufficient and before the extra work is done. The act allows one case without consent: when no waiver was signed and the estimated price is exceeded by not more than 10% or $50.00, whichever is lesser, unless the customer specifically asked to be consulted.
