Official description

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in publishing directories, mailing lists, and collections or compilations of fact. The products are typically protected in their selection, arrangement and/or presentation. Examples are lists of mailing addresses, telephone directories, directories of businesses, collections or compilations of proprietary drugs or legal case results, compilations of public records, etc. These establishments may publish directories and mailing lists in print or electronic form, including exclusively on the Internet.

2022 NAICS description, U.S. Census Bureau.[1]

Classified elsewhere (cross-references)

Establishments primarily engaged in:

  • Operating Web search portals--are classified in Industry 519290, Web Search Portals and All Other Information Services;
  • Compiling mailing lists in conjunction with providing advertising and public relations services--are classified in Industry Group 5418, Advertising, Public Relations, and Related Services;
  • Printing without publishing directories and mailing lists--are classified in Industry 32311, Printing;
  • Publishing computer software--are classified in Industry 513210, Software Publishers;
  • Creating and publishing encyclopedias and similar collections of creative works in print and/or electronic media--are classified in Industry 513130, Book Publishers; and
  • Creating and publishing collections of creative works that are periodically updated--are classified in Industry 513120, Periodical Publishers.

SIC codes: no single equivalent

There is no official SIC line for 513140: the only official Census concordance is between 1987 SIC and 1997 NAICS, and this industry changed after 1997. The SIC codes below are our derivation: we followed the official concordances 2022 → 2017 → 2012 → 2007 → 2002 → 1997 back to 7 NAICS 1997 industries and took their official SIC lines.[5],[6],[7]

Only a piece of each of those 1997 industries is in 513140 (the rest went to other 2022 codes), so each of the 8 SIC codes below matches this industry only in part: read the part descriptions.
