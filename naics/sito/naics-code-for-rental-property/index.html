NAICS code for rental property, Airbnb and short-term rentals

Long-term rental, short-term rental, guest house or bed and breakfast: the NAICS 2022 codes of the official index for each case.

Renting out a home and running short-term lodging are different industries. Leasing residential buildings is 531110 «Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings»; short-term lodging other than hotels, motels and bed-and-breakfast inns is 721199 «All Other Traveler Accommodation». The official index lists 84 matching entries in 9 codes.[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

The codes to look at first

531110Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of buildings used as residences or dwellings, such as single-family homes, apartment buildings, and town homes. Included in this industry are owner-lessors and establishments renting real estate and then acting as lessors in subleasing it to others. The establishments in this industry may manage the property themselves or have another establishment manage it for them.

In the official index: Apartment rental or leasing; Residential building rental or leasing; Cottage rental or leasing; Apartment building rental or leasing; Apartment hotel rental or leasing; Building, apartment, rental or leasing…

SIC 1987: 6513, 6514

531120Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings (except Miniwarehouses)

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in acting as lessors of buildings (except miniwarehouses and self-storage units) that are not used as residences or dwellings. Included in this industry are: (1) owner-lessors of nonresidential buildings; (2) establishments renting real estate and then acting as lessors in subleasing it to others; and (3) establishments providing full service office space, whether on a lease or service contract basis. The establishments in this industry may manage the property themselves or have another establishment manage it for them.

In the official index: Commercial building rental or leasing; Banquet hall rental or leasing; Amusement facility rental or leasing; Arena, no promotion of events, rental or leasing; Auditorium rental or leasing; Bank building rental or leasing…

SIC 1987: 6512

721199All Other Traveler Accommodation

Official description quoted above.

In the official index: Guest houses; Tourist homes; Housekeeping cottages; Cottages, housekeeping

SIC 1987: 7011

721191Bed-and-Breakfast Inns

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing short-term lodging in facilities known as bed-and-breakfast inns. These establishments provide short-term lodging in private homes or small buildings converted for this purpose. Bed-and-breakfast inns are characterized by a highly personalized service and inclusion of a full breakfast in the room rate.

In the official index: Bed and breakfast inns; Inns, bed and breakfast

SIC 1987: 7011

721310Rooming and Boarding Houses, Dormitories, and Workers' Camps

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in operating rooming and boarding houses and similar facilities, such as fraternity houses, sorority houses, off campus dormitories, residential clubs, and workers' camps. These establishments provide temporary or longer-term accommodations, which, for the period of occupancy, may serve as a principal residence. These establishments also may provide complementary services, such as housekeeping, meals, and laundry services.

In the official index: Rooming and boarding houses; Boarding houses; Corporate rooming and boarding houses

SIC 1987: 7021, 7041

531311Residential Property Managers

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in managing residential real estate for others.

In the official index: Resort or vacation property managers' offices; Apartment managers' offices; Cooperative apartment managers' offices; Managing cooperative apartments

SIC 1987: 6531

561599All Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services

This U.S. industry comprises establishments (except travel agencies, tour operators, and convention and visitors bureaus) primarily engaged in providing travel arrangement and reservation services.

In the official index: Online accommodations reservation services (through a website or mobile application)

SIC 1987: 4729, 7389, 7922, 7999, 8699

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for rental property?

Residential rentals such as apartments, houses and cottages are 531110 «Lessors of Residential Buildings and Dwellings»; commercial buildings are 531120 «Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings (except Miniwarehouses)»; land is 531190 «Lessors of Other Real Estate Property». The official description of 531110 includes owner-lessors and says they may manage the property themselves or have another establishment manage it.[1],[3]

What is the NAICS code for an Airbnb or short-term rental?

The official index has no entry for a booking platform or for «short-term rental». Short-term lodging (except hotels, motels, casino hotels and bed-and-breakfast inns) is 721199 «All Other Traveler Accommodation», with guest houses, tourist homes and housekeeping cottages; bed and breakfast inns are 721191 «Bed-and-Breakfast Inns». Online accommodations reservation services are 561599 «All Other Travel Arrangement and Reservation Services».[1],[3]
