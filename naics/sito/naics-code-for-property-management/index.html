NAICS code for property management

Residential property managers are NAICS 531311, commercial ones 531312; homeowners' associations and facilities support have other codes.

The official 2022 index splits property management by the kind of property: residential is 531311 «Residential Property Managers», commercial and other nonresidential is 531312 «Nonresidential Property Managers». It lists 36 matching entries in 5 codes, including owners' associations and facilities support services.[1],[2]

The codes to look at first

531311Residential Property Managers

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in managing residential real estate for others.

In the official index: Property managers' offices, residential real estate; Apartment managers' offices; Condominium managers' offices, residential; Cooperative apartment managers' offices; Managers' offices, residential condominium; Managers' offices, residential real estate…

SIC 1987: 6531

531312Nonresidential Property Managers

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in managing nonresidential real estate for others.

In the official index: Property managers' offices, commercial real estate; Nonresidential property managing; Condominium managers' offices, commercial; Commercial property managing; Commercial real estate property managers' offices; Managers' offices, commercial condominium…

SIC 1987: 6531

813990Other Similar Organizations (except Business, Professional, Labor, and Political Organizations)

This industry comprises establishments (except religious organizations, social advocacy organizations, civic and social organizations, business associations, professional organizations, labor unions, and political organizations) primarily engaged in promoting the interests of their members.

In the official index: Homeowners' associations; Condominium owners' associations; Property owners' associations; Condominium corporations; Cooperative owners' associations; Homeowners' associations, condominium

SIC 1987: 6531, 8641, 8699

561210Facilities Support Services

This industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing operating staff to perform a combination of support services within a client's facilities. Establishments in this industry typically provide a combination of services, such as janitorial, maintenance, trash disposal, guard and security, mail routing, reception, laundry, and related services to support operations within facilities. These establishments provide operating staff to carry out these support activities, but are not involved with or responsible for the core business or activities of the client. Establishments providing facilities (except computer and/or data processing) operation support services and establishments providing private jail services or operating correctional facilities (i.e., jails) on a contract or fee basis are included in this industry.

In the official index: Facilities (except computer operation) support services

SIC 1987: 8744

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for property management?

531311 «Residential Property Managers» for homes, apartments and residential condominiums, and 531312 «Nonresidential Property Managers» for commercial and other nonresidential real estate. Homeowners' and condominium owners' associations are 813990 «Other Similar Organizations (except Business, Professional, Labor, and Political Organizations)».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for residential property management?

531311 «Residential Property Managers». The official index also places resort or vacation property managers' offices and managers of cooperative apartments in this code.[1]
