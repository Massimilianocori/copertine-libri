NAICS code for a restaurant, bar or coffee shop

Full-service or limited-service restaurant, bar, coffee shop or bakery: the NAICS 2022 codes the official Census index gives to each one.

There is no single restaurant code: NAICS 2022 separates eating places by how they serve. The official index lists 81 entries for restaurants, bars, coffee shops and bakeries in 15 codes, bakery and coffee manufacturing and retailing included; eating places are in industry group 7225 «Restaurants and Other Eating Places», while drinking places are 722410 «Drinking Places (Alcoholic Beverages)».[1],[2]

Which code: what the official index says

If the main activity of the establishment is one of the entries in the first column, the official 2022 index points to the code next to it. Entries are quoted word for word.[1]

The codes to look at first

722511Full-Service Restaurants

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing food services to patrons who order and are served while seated (i.e., waiter/waitress service) and pay after eating. These establishments may provide this type of food service to patrons in combination with selling alcoholic beverages, providing carryout services, or presenting live nontheatrical entertainment.

In the official index: Full service restaurants; Family restaurants, full service; Fine dining restaurants, full service; Bagel shops, full service; Brew pub restaurants, primarily serving meals, full service; Diners, full service…

SIC 1987: 5812

722513Limited-Service Restaurants

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in providing food services (except snack and nonalcoholic beverage bars) where patrons generally order or select items and pay before eating. Food and drink may be consumed on premises, taken out, or delivered to the customer's location. Some establishments in this industry may provide these food services in combination with selling alcoholic beverages.

In the official index: Fast-food restaurants; Carryout restaurants; Drive-in restaurants; Cafes, limited-service; Delicatessen restaurants; Diners, limited-service…

SIC 1987: 5812

722514Cafeterias, Grill Buffets, and Buffets

This U.S. industry comprises establishments, known as cafeterias, grill buffets, or buffets, primarily engaged in preparing and serving meals for immediate consumption using cafeteria-style or buffet serving equipment, such as steam tables, refrigerated areas, display grills, and self-service nonalcoholic beverage dispensing equipment. Patrons select from food and drink items on display in a continuous cafeteria line or from buffet stations.

In the official index: Cafeterias; Buffet eating places

SIC 1987: 5812

722515Snack and Nonalcoholic Beverage Bars

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in (1) preparing and/or serving a specialty snack, such as ice cream, frozen yogurt, cookies, or popcorn, or (2) serving nonalcoholic beverages, such as coffee, juices, or sodas for consumption on or near the premises. These establishments may carry and sell a combination of snack, nonalcoholic beverage, and other related products (e.g., coffee beans, mugs, coffee makers) but generally promote and sell a unique snack or nonalcoholic beverage.

In the official index: Coffee shops, on premise brewing; Juice bars, nonalcoholic, fixed location; Bakery cafes, on premise baking and selling for immediate consumption; Bagel shops, on premise baking and carryout service; Beverage (e.g., coffee, juice, soft drink) bars, nonalcoholic, fixed location; Confectionery snack shops, made on premises with carryout services…

SIC 1987: 5461, 5812

722410Drinking Places (Alcoholic Beverages)

This industry comprises establishments known as bars, taverns, nightclubs, or drinking places primarily engaged in preparing and serving alcoholic beverages for immediate consumption. These establishments may also provide limited food services.

In the official index: Bars (i.e., drinking places), alcoholic beverage; Cocktail lounges; Drinking places (i.e., bars, lounges, taverns), alcoholic; Alcoholic beverage drinking places; Brew pubs, primarily serving alcoholic beverages for immediate consumption; Lounges, cocktail…

SIC 1987: 5813

311811Retail Bakeries

This U.S. industry comprises establishments primarily engaged in retailing bread and other bakery products not for immediate consumption made on the premises from flour, not from prepared dough.

In the official index: Bakeries with baking from flour on the premises, retailing not for immediate consumption

SIC 1987: 5461

Descriptions: 2022 NAICS, U.S. Census Bureau.[3]

Frequently asked questions

Questions people really search on Google, answered from the official sources cited.

What is the NAICS code for a restaurant?

It depends on the service. In the official 2022 index, full service restaurants, family restaurants with full service and fine dining restaurants are 722511 «Full-Service Restaurants»; fast-food, carryout and drive-in restaurants are 722513 «Limited-Service Restaurants»; cafeterias and buffet eating places are 722514 «Cafeterias, Grill Buffets, and Buffets».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a bar?

Bars, cocktail lounges and taverns serving alcoholic beverages are 722410 «Drinking Places (Alcoholic Beverages)». Nonalcoholic beverage bars at a fixed location, such as juice bars, are 722515 «Snack and Nonalcoholic Beverage Bars».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a coffee shop?

The official index lists «Coffee shops, on premise brewing» under 722515 «Snack and Nonalcoholic Beverage Bars». Mobile coffee carts are 722330 «Mobile Food Services», and coffee roasting is 311920 «Coffee and Tea Manufacturing».[1],[2]

What is the NAICS code for a bakery?

Bakeries that bake from flour on the premises and sell for consumption elsewhere are 311811 «Retail Bakeries»; bakery cafes that sell for immediate consumption are 722515 «Snack and Nonalcoholic Beverage Bars»; stores selling baked goods not made on the premises are 445291 «Baked Goods Retailers»; commercial bakeries are 311812 «Commercial Bakeries».[1],[2]
