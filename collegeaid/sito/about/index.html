About US College Aid

What this site is, what it publishes and how it is paid for.

US College Aid is an independent publication, part of a small network of websites that turn official data into plain reference pages. It shows FAFSA deadlines by state, federal student loan interest rates and limits, and the official repayment formulas, each copied from the document it comes from.

We are not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Education, Federal Student Aid, any state agency, college, loan servicer or lender. The site gives general information, not financial, tax or legal advice.

What we publish

  • Dates, rates, limits and formulas printed in official documents, each linked to its source and quoted word for word where it matters.
  • Calculations made from those figures, always labelled as ours.
  • Where an official source does not say something, we say so instead of filling the gap.

How the site is paid for

The site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers never decide what we publish.

Corrections

If a figure does not match the official document, use the corrections form. For questions about your own aid or loans, ask the financial aid office of your college or your loan servicer: they have your records.
