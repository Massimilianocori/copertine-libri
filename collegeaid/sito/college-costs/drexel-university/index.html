Drexel University tuition and total cost

The College Scorecard file lists the same tuition and required fees for in-state and out-of-state students at Drexel University: $64,212 for the 2025–26 academic year. Average net price by family income

Net price is the cost of attendance minus the average grant and scholarship aid. It is what families paid on average for one year, before loans. The file gives it for first-time, full-time undergraduates who received federal student aid, in 2024–25, by the family income used to work out aid.[1],[2]

The gap after federal loans at Drexel University

For students starting now, federal loans have a ceiling. A dependent undergraduate can borrow $5,500 in the first year, $6,500 in the second and $7,500 a year after that, up to $31,000 in all.[3] Since July 1, 2026 parents can borrow at most $20,000 a year and $65,000 in total for each student with Parent PLUS loans.[4] Over four years that is at most $27,000 for the student plus $65,000 for the parents: $92,000 (our addition).

At the average net price of $37,529 a year, four years at Drexel University come to $150,116; the federal limits leave $58,116 to find elsewhere. The table does the same sum for each income band, holding the 2024–25 price flat for four years.

This is arithmetic, not a forecast and not advice to borrow the maximum. Prices usually rise each year, a family can borrow less than the limit, a loan fee is taken from each disbursement, and a Parent PLUS loan needs a credit check. “Left uncovered” has to come from savings, income, work, more scholarships or private loans. Students who were already enrolled and borrowing before July 1, 2026 may keep the old Parent PLUS rules for a time: see federal loan limits. Independent students have higher limits of their own and no Parent PLUS: see federal loan limits.

How to read this page

  • Every figure is copied from one federal file, the College Scorecard institution file the Department of Education updated on November 2, 2026. Nothing is estimated or taken from other websites.
  • The file runs behind the college’s own website. Tuition and fees are for 2025–26; cost of attendance and net price are for 2024–25. For next year’s price, check Drexel University directly.
  • Net price is an average, not an offer. It covers first-time, full-time undergraduates who received federal student aid. Your own price depends on your aid package.
  • Family income is the income the college used to work out financial aid: for a dependent student, the student’s and the parents’ income together.
  • The graduation rate follows the 2019–20 entering class; the admission rate is for fall 2024.
  • The gap is our own arithmetic on the file’s net price and the official loan limits. It is not a figure published by the college or the Department.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

How much is tuition at Drexel University?

Tuition and required fees at Drexel University are $64,212 for the 2025–26 academic year, the same for in-state and out-of-state students. The figure comes from the U.S. Department of Education’s College Scorecard file updated on November 2, 2026.[1],[2]

What does Drexel University cost per year after grants and scholarships?

The average net price at Drexel University was $37,529 a year in 2024–25, for first-time, full-time undergraduates who received federal student aid. By family income it went from $28,843 (income of $0 to $30,000) to $46,936 (income of $110,000 or more). The average cost of attendance before aid was $78,335.[1],[2]
