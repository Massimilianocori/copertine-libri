The two federal dates

The FAFSA form for 2027–28 has one federal window. It is the same in every state, and it is much longer than most state deadlines.

  • FAFSA form openedEarliest date to submit the 2027–28 form, for federal aid in every state.
    October 1, 2026
  • Federal deadlineLast day the 2027–28 form can be received for federal aid. Your college also needs your correct, complete information by your last day of enrollment in 2027–28.
    June 30, 2028

“For federal aid, submit your application as early as possible, but no earlier than October 1, 2026. We must receive your application no later than June 30, 2028.”

Federal Student Aid, U.S. Department of Education, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028 (paper form, deadlines on pages 1 and 2)[1]

“Your college must have your correct, complete information by your last day of enrollment in the 2027–28 school year.”

Federal Student Aid, U.S. Department of Education, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028 (paper form, deadlines on pages 1 and 2)[1]

How to read a state entry

  • A plain date is the day the form must be received, unless the entry says otherwise. California writes “date postmarked” and Florida “date processed”.
  • “For priority consideration” (14 entries) is not written as a final cutoff. The form does not say what happens to later applications, so ask the state agency or your financial aid office.
  • “As soon as possible after Oct. 1, 2026” (14 entries), often with “Awards made while funds exist” (11 entries): there may be no closing date, but the money can run out.
  • “Check with your financial aid office” with nothing else (18 entries, 12 of them states): the form gives no state date at all. The college sets it.
  • “More forms may be required” (25 entries): the FAFSA alone may not be enough for state aid.

“For state or college aid, the deadline may be as early as October 1, 2026, or the aid may be awarded on a first-come, first-served basis.”

Federal Student Aid, U.S. Department of Education, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028 (paper form, deadlines on pages 1 and 2)[1]

Colleges have their own dates too. A college can set a financial aid deadline that is earlier than both the state and the federal one, and the FAFSA form does not list them. The college’s financial aid office is the only source for that date.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the FAFSA deadline for 2027–28?

For federal aid, the 2027–28 FAFSA form must be received no later than June 30, 2028. State and college deadlines are usually earlier: the first fixed state date on the official form is January 15, 2027 (Texas, for priority consideration).[1]

When is the FAFSA deadline in my state?

It depends on the state. On the official 2027–28 form, 31 of 59 states and territories have at least one fixed date, 10 say only to apply as soon as possible after October 1, 2026, and 18 say to check with your financial aid office. The table above has the exact wording for each.[1]

When does the 2027–28 FAFSA close?

The federal form for 2027–28 closes on June 30, 2028, the last day of that award year. The form also says your college must have your correct, complete information by your last day of enrollment in the 2027–28 school year.[1]

What is a FAFSA priority deadline?

Some state entries on the form begin with “For priority consideration, submit by” a date. The form does not define the term or say what happens to applications received later, so treat the priority date as the date to meet and ask the state agency or your financial aid office about anything after it.[1]

Where do these state deadlines come from?

From page 2 of the official 2027–28 FAFSA form published by Federal Student Aid, read on November 18, 2026. Each entry in the table is the form’s own text. States can change their dates after the form is printed, so confirm with the state agency or your college.[1]

Is the federal FAFSA deadline different from the state one?

Yes. The federal deadline is one date for everyone, June 30, 2028 for 2027–28. State aid has its own deadlines, and the form warns that a state or college deadline may be as early as October 1, 2026 or that aid may be awarded first come, first served.[1]
