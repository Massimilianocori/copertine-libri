Ohio · FAFSA 2027–28

FAFSA deadline in Ohio for 2027–28

State aid: October 1, 2027 on the official form. Federal aid: June 30, 2028.

  • State aid
    October 1, 2027
  • FAFSA form openedEarliest date to submit the 2027–28 form, for federal aid in every state.
    October 1, 2026
  • Federal deadlineLast day the 2027–28 form can be received for federal aid. Your college also needs your correct, complete information by your last day of enrollment in 2027–28.
    June 30, 2028

What the official form says for Ohio

This is the complete Ohio entry on page 2 of the 2027–28 FAFSA form, word for word.

“Oct. 1, 2027”

Ohio (OH), 2027–28 FAFSA form, page 2[1]

“Unless otherwise noted, the dates below refer to when the form must be received.”

Federal Student Aid, U.S. Department of Education, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028 (paper form, deadlines on pages 1 and 2)[1]

What that means

  • Ohio’s entry is a single date with no conditions: October 1, 2027, three months into the 2027–28 award year.
  • The form’s general rule applies: it is the date by which the form must be received.

State aid and federal aid have different dates

The Ohio date above is for state aid. Federal aid (federal grants, work-study and loans) follows the federal window: the form opened on October 1, 2026 and must be received by June 30, 2028.[1] Missing a state date does not close the federal form, but it can cost you the state aid that date is for.

“For federal aid, submit your application as early as possible, but no earlier than October 1, 2026. We must receive your application no later than June 30, 2028.”

Federal Student Aid, U.S. Department of Education, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028 (paper form, deadlines on pages 1 and 2)[1]

“For state or college aid, the deadline may be as early as October 1, 2026, or the aid may be awarded on a first-come, first-served basis.”

Federal Student Aid, U.S. Department of Education, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028 (paper form, deadlines on pages 1 and 2)[1]

Your college may want the form earlier. The FAFSA form does not list college deadlines. Ask the financial aid office of each college you are applying to; the form calls it “the best place for information about student aid”.[1]

If aid does not cover the bill: federal loan numbers

Federal student loans are requested through the same FAFSA form. For loans first disbursed from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027, the fixed rate for undergraduates is 6.52%[2], and a first-year dependent student can borrow up to $5,500 for the year[3].

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the FAFSA deadline in Ohio for 2027–28?

For state aid in Ohio, the first date on the official 2027–28 FAFSA form is October 1, 2027: state aid. The federal deadline is June 30, 2028.[1]

When is the FAFSA due for federal aid if I live in Ohio?

The federal dates are the same in every state: the 2027–28 form opened on October 1, 2026 and must be received by June 30, 2028. Only state and college aid follow the earlier Ohio and college dates.[1]

When did the 2027–28 FAFSA open?

The form says to submit it no earlier than October 1, 2026. That is the first day the 2027–28 form could be filed, in Ohio and everywhere else.[1]
