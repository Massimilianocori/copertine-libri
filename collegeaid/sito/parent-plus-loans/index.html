The new Parent PLUS limits

A Direct PLUS Loan for parents used to cover whatever was left of the college’s cost of attendance after other aid. For periods of enrollment beginning on or after July 1, 2026 there are two caps, counted per student and for all of that student’s parents together.

“For periods of enrollment beginning on or after July 1, 2026, the total amount of all Direct PLUS Loans that all parents may borrow on behalf of each dependent student for any academic year of study may not exceed $20,000.”

U.S. Department of Education (Federal Register), Reimagining and Improving Student Education—Federal Student Loan Program Final Regulations; Final Rule (91 FR 23768, May 1, 2026)[1]

“For periods of enrollment beginning on or after July 1, 2026, the total amount of all Direct PLUS Loans that all parents may borrow on behalf of each dependent student may not exceed $65,000, without regard to any amounts repaid, forgiven, canceled, or otherwise discharged on any such loan.”

U.S. Department of Education (Federal Register), Reimagining and Improving Student Education—Federal Student Loan Program Final Regulations; Final Rule (91 FR 23768, May 1, 2026)[1]
  • Two parents borrowing for the same child share the same $20,000 and $65,000; a family with two children in college has the limits twice.
  • The $65,000 is counted “without regard to any amounts repaid”: repaying part of the loan does not make room for new borrowing.
  • The loan still cannot exceed the cost of attendance minus the student’s other financial assistance.[1]
  • If the student was enrolled on June 30, 2026 and a Direct Loan had already been made to the parent or the student for that program, the old rule (up to the cost of attendance) continues for the time left in the program, up to 3 academic years.[1],[3]

The gap after federal loans

With the new caps, federal loans can cover at most $27,000 (student, four years at the dependent limits) plus $65,000 (parents): $92,000 for a four-year degree (our addition).[1],[4] If the cost left after grants and scholarships is higher, the difference has to come from savings, income, work, more scholarships, a cheaper college or private loans. For example, at $32,000 a year for four years the limits leave $36,000 uncovered.

How much do the federal limits cover?

Assumes a dependent student starting the first year who borrows the full federal limit every year, and parents who borrow PLUS loans for the rest up to their limit. Both can borrow less.

Interest rate: 9.07% for 2026–27

Direct PLUS Loans first disbursed from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 have a fixed rate of 9.07%: the 10-year Treasury yield of 4.47% plus 4.60 points. The law caps the PLUS rate at 10.50%.[2] Each year’s loan keeps the rate of its own year. PLUS rates for every year since 2013.

Repaying a Parent PLUS loan

Parent PLUS loans made on or after July 1, 2026 must be repaid under the standard plan: a fixed monthly payment over 10 to 25 years, depending on the principal owed. They cannot go into the Repayment Assistance Plan. Paying faster is allowed without penalty.[3]

“the Secretary shall require a borrower who has received an excepted loan made on or after such date (including such a borrower who also has an excepted loan made before such date) to repay each excepted loan, including principal and interest on those excepted loans, under the standard repayment plan under subparagraph (A)(i).”

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is a Parent PLUS loan?

A federal Direct PLUS Loan that a parent takes out to pay for a dependent undergraduate’s education. For 2026–27 the fixed rate is 9.07%, and from July 1, 2026 parents can borrow up to $20,000 a year and $65,000 in total for each student.[1],[2]

What are the Parent PLUS loan limits?

For periods of enrollment beginning on or after July 1, 2026: $20,000 per academic year and $65,000 in total, for each dependent student and for all of the student’s parents together.[1]

Is the Parent PLUS limit per parent or per child?

Per child. The regulation counts the total of all Direct PLUS Loans that all parents borrow on behalf of each dependent student: $20,000 a year and $65,000 overall.[1]

Do the new limits apply if my child was already in college?

Not right away. If the student was enrolled on June 30, 2026 and a Direct Loan had already been made for that program to the parent or the student, the limits do not apply for the student’s expected time to finish, up to 3 academic years.[1],[3]

What is the monthly payment on a Parent PLUS loan?

Under the standard plan at 9.07%, a balance of $20,000 is about $254.11 a month over 10 years, and $65,000 about $587.75 a month over 20 years (our calculation).[2],[3]

Can a Parent PLUS loan be repaid based on income?

A Parent PLUS loan made on or after July 1, 2026 has to be repaid under the standard plan; the Repayment Assistance Plan is not available for it.[3]
