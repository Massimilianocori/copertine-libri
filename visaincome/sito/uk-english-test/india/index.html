UK visa English test in India: 145 approved centres (SELT)

There are 145 approved centres for UK visa English tests (SELT) in India, in New Delhi (12), Hyderabad (11), Mumbai (9), Ahmedabad (7), Chandigarh (7), Chennai (6), Jalandhar (5), Amritsar (4) and 53 more, run by IELTS SELT Consortium, LanguageCert, Pearson (PTE) and PSI Services (Skills for English), according to the Home Office list of approved test centres outside the UK dated 23 October 2026 (GOV.UK, downloaded on 30 October 2026). The first partner (spouse) visa needs A1 in speaking and listening.

B2 for settlement from 26 March 2027

For settlement applications made on or after 26 March 2027, the UK Statement of Changes HC 1691 raises the English level from B1 to B2 in speaking and listening on the routes it lists (Skilled Worker, Settlement Family Life, Long Residence, Global Talent and others). Applications made before that date still need B1. On the approved lists we read on 30 October 2026, there is no speaking-and-listening-only test at B2: every B2 test listed assesses all four skills. How to book

  • Book on the provider's own website: IELTS SELT Consortium, LanguageCert, Pearson (PTE) and PSI Services (Skills for English). GOV.UK says to check the provider's website for dates and to make sure the centre is open: "Do not contact the test centre directly."
  • Book the SELT version of the test (for example IELTS for UKVI or IELTS Life Skills, not ordinary IELTS). The approved names are: IELTS SELT Consortium: IELTS for UKVI, IELTS Life Skills; LanguageCert: LanguageCert International ESOL SELT, LanguageCert Academic SELT, LanguageCert General SELT; Pearson (PTE): PTE Academic UKVI, PTE Home; PSI Services (Skills for English): Skills for English UKVI.
  • "You should be able to take a test within 28 days of booking, but it may not always be at the location nearest to you." Use the name exactly as it is on your passport or identity document.
  • Test results are valid for 2 years from the date the test is awarded. After you pass, you get a SELT reference number to use in your application.
  • Fees are set by each provider and are not on the Home Office lists, so we do not show them. If you book through an agent, GOV.UK says its fees should be "the same as, or lower than, the price on the test provider's website".

Source: GOV.UK, Prove your English language abilities with a secure English language test (SELT) (updated 26 October 2026, read on 30 October 2026).

Questions people ask

Where can I take the A1 English test for a UK spouse visa in India?

At an approved SELT centre: the Home Office list dated 23 October 2026 has 145 approved centres in India, in New Delhi (12), Hyderabad (11), Mumbai (9), Ahmedabad (7), Chandigarh (7), Chennai (6), Jalandhar (5), Amritsar (4) and 53 more. The A1 speaking and listening tests approved outside the UK from the providers with centres in India are: IELTS Life Skills – A1 Speaking & Listening from IELTS SELT Consortium; LanguageCert International ESOL SELT A1 (Speaking & Listening) from LanguageCert; PTE Home A1 from Pearson (PTE); Skills for English UKVI A1 (2 component) from PSI Services (Skills for English).

How many SELT test centres are there in India?

145 approved centres in 61 cities, from IELTS SELT Consortium, LanguageCert, Pearson (PTE) and PSI Services (Skills for English) (Home Office list dated 23 October 2026).

Does every centre offer every test?

The Home Office list does not say which tests or dates each centre offers. Check the provider's booking page before you travel.

How much does the test cost?

Fees are set by each provider and are not on the Home Office lists, so we do not show them. If you use an agent, GOV.UK says its fees should be the same as, or lower than, the price on the provider's website.

Is the English level for UK settlement going up?

Yes, on many routes: for settlement applications made on or after 26 March 2027, HC 1691 raises the level from B1 to B2 in speaking and listening.
