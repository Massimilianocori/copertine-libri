SELT test centres in Leicester: 2 approved centres for UK visas

There are 2 approved centres for UK visa English tests (SELT) in Leicester, run by Pearson (PTE) and Trinity College London, according to the Home Office list of approved test centres dated 23 October 2026 (GOV.UK, downloaded on 30 October 2026). You book the test on the provider's website, not at the centre.

B2 for settlement from 26 March 2027

For settlement applications made on or after 26 March 2027, the UK Statement of Changes HC 1691 raises the English level from B1 to B2 in speaking and listening on the routes it lists (Skilled Worker, Settlement Family Life, Long Residence, Global Talent and others). Applications made before that date still need B1. On the approved lists we read on 30 October 2026, there is no speaking-and-listening-only test at B2: every B2 test listed assesses all four skills. What changes in 2027.

Source: explanatory memorandum to the Statement of Changes HC 1691, paragraphs 5.60–5.62, and the rule changes (published on GOV.UK on 6 March 2026, read on 30 October 2026).

How to book

  • Book on the provider's own website: IELTS SELT Consortium, LanguageCert, Pearson (PTE) and Trinity College London. GOV.UK says to check the provider's website for dates and to make sure the centre is open: "Do not contact the test centre directly."
  • Book the SELT version of the test (for example IELTS for UKVI or IELTS Life Skills, not ordinary IELTS). The approved names are: IELTS SELT Consortium: IELTS for UKVI, IELTS Life Skills; LanguageCert: LanguageCert International ESOL SELT, LanguageCert Academic SELT, LanguageCert General SELT; Pearson (PTE): PTE Academic UKVI, PTE Home; Trinity College London: Secure English Language Tests for UKVI.
  • "You should be able to take a test within 28 days of booking, but it may not always be at the location nearest to you." Use the name exactly as it is on your passport or identity document.
  • Test results are valid for 2 years from the date the test is awarded. After you pass, you get a SELT reference number to use in your application.
  • Fees are set by each provider and are not on the Home Office lists, so we do not show them. If you book through an agent, GOV.UK says its fees should be "the same as, or lower than, the price on the test provider's website".

Source: GOV.UK, Prove your English language abilities with a secure English language test (SELT) (updated 26 October 2026, read on 30 October 2026).

Questions people ask

Where can I take a SELT in Leicester?

The Home Office list dated 23 October 2026 has 2 approved centres in Leicester: Pearson Professional Centre - Leicester (Pearson (PTE)); Leicester (Trinity College London). Book on the provider's website, not at the centre.

Which English test do I need for a UK visa?

A1 speaking and listening for a first partner or parent visa, A2 to extend after 2.5 years, B1 for settlement and citizenship, and B2 (four skills) for Skilled Worker and most degree-level Student visas. For settlement applications made on or after 26 March 2027 on the routes listed in HC 1691, B2 in speaking and listening.

Does every centre in Leicester offer every test?

The Home Office list does not say which tests or dates each centre offers. Check the provider's booking page: GOV.UK says you should be able to take a test within 28 days of booking, but not always at the nearest location.

How much does the test cost?

Fees are set by each provider and are not on the Home Office lists, so we do not show them. Check the provider's website before you book.
