Schengen visa from the UK: which country refuses least (2025)

In 2025, Schengen consulates in the United Kingdom did not issue 10.5% of the 506,166 visa applications they decided, according to the European Commission's Visa statistics for consulates 2025 (downloaded on 15 October 2026). Among the 25 countries with at least 1,000 applications, the Netherlands had the lowest rate (1.0%) and Iceland the highest (32.0%).

What a Schengen visa costs from the UK

The fee is the same at every Schengen consulate: €90 for adults and €45 for children aged 6 to 12. An additional fee may apply if you go through a visa service centre, which collects applications on behalf of a consulate. The insurance and the money you must show are on top of the fee.

Travel medical insurance: at least €30,000

The Visa Code asks for travel medical insurance covering repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical care, emergency hospital treatment and death, valid in all Schengen countries and for the whole stay, with minimum cover of €30,000 (about £25,014). For a multiple-entry visa the policy must cover the first trip. Applicants should, in principle, take out the insurance in their country of residence (Article 15).

Source: Visa Code, Regulation (EC) No 810/2009 (consolidated text) (read on 31 October 2026).

Who can apply in the United Kingdom, and where

  • You apply where you legally live. "You must apply for a Schengen visa at the consulate with territorial responsibility for the country where you are legally resident" (European Commission). A consulate may accept an application from someone legally present but not resident, if there is a reason to apply there (Visa Code, Article 6).
  • You apply to your main destination: the Schengen country where you will spend the most time or, for stays of equal length, the first one you visit.
  • Processing time: normally 15 days, up to 45 days in some cases.
  • Visa centres. Many consulates take applications through a private visa centre. Where we read it on the embassy's own page:
    • Netherlands (VFS Global): The Dutch government's page says to apply at the VFS Global application centre in Birmingham, Edinburgh, London or Manchester (official page, read on 31 October 2026).
    • Germany (TLScontact): The German missions' page says the visa application centres in London and Manchester (for England, with exceptions, Northern Ireland and Wales) and Edinburgh (for Scotland) are operated by TLScontact (official page, read on 31 October 2026).
    • Italy (VFS Global): The Italian Consulate General in London says appointments are managed only through its service partner, VFS Global, which charges an extra service fee (official page, read on 31 October 2026).
    • Belgium (TLScontact): The Belgian embassy's page sends questions about the application process, appointments and documents to TLScontact (official page, read on 31 October 2026).
    • Portugal (VFS Global): The Portuguese Consulate General in London says its visa application centre in London is operated by VFS Global (official page, read on 31 October 2026).
    For the other countries, check the embassy's official website: we only name a visa centre when we have read it there.

Source: European Commission, Applying for a Schengen visa (read on 31 October 2026); Visa Code, Regulation (EC) No 810/2009 (consolidated text) (read on 31 October 2026).

Questions people ask

Which is the easiest Schengen visa from the UK?

No country is easy for everyone, but in 2025 the Schengen country with the lowest not-issued rate in the United Kingdom (among those with at least 1,000 applications) was the Netherlands (1.0%), and the highest was Iceland (32.0%). You cannot pick a country for its rate: you apply to your main destination.

How much does a Schengen visa cost from the UK?

The visa fee is €90 for adults (about £75.04) and €45 for children aged 6 to 12 (about £37.52), according to the European Commission. If you go through a visa centre, its service fee comes on top.

Can I apply for a Schengen visa from the UK with an Indian or Pakistani passport?

Yes, if you legally live in the United Kingdom: the Visa Code says the consulate responsible for the place where you legally reside examines your application. The figures on this page count every application made in the United Kingdom, whatever the passport: the Commission's file does not split them by nationality.

Can I apply from the UK if I am only visiting or studying there?

The rule is the place where you legally reside. A consulate may also accept an application from someone legally present but not resident in its area, if the applicant gives a reason for applying there (Visa Code, Article 6(2)). Ask the consulate before you book.

How much travel insurance do I need for a Schengen visa?

At least €30,000 (about £25,014), valid in all Schengen countries for the whole stay, covering emergency medical care, hospital treatment and repatriation (Visa Code, Article 15). You should, in principle, buy it in the country where you live.

How long does a Schengen visa take?

The normal processing time is 15 days, and it can be extended up to 45 days when the consulate needs to look at the application in more detail (European Commission). Visa centres and consulates also have their own waiting times for appointments.

How much money do I need to show for a France visa?

France has notified the Commission of €120 a day without proof of accommodation ≈ £100.06; €65 a day for the nights covered by a hotel booking ≈ £54.20; €32.50 a day with proof of accommodation ≈ £27.10. Each Schengen country sets its own reference amount: see the table on this page.
