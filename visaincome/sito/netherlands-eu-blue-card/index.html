Netherlands EU Blue Card income requirement 2026: how much you need (calculator)

Work permit / EU Blue CardNetherlands

Minimum for the main applicant (standard threshold): €5,942 a month (€71,304 a year)

Official source: Required amounts income requirements – highly skilled migrant and European Blue Card (Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND)) · checked on 10 October 2026

The Dutch EU Blue Card for highly qualified employees with a higher education degree. Official name: Europese blauwe kaart.

How the amount is calculated

Rule: €5,942 gross a month (without holiday allowance) in 2026.

Income that counts: Gross monthly salary without the 8% holiday allowance.

Good to know

  • The amounts change every 1 January.
  • The IND amounts exclude the 8% holiday allowance, so the yearly figure (× 12) is lower than a full gross annual salary that includes it.
