How many Indians moved to Europe in 2025?

227,577 Indian citizens received a first residence permit in one of the 27 EU countries in 2025, according to Eurostat (data updated on 22 October 2026). That is 35,396 more than in 2024 (192,181), a change of +18.4%. Part of the change may come from Portugal, where Eurostat marks a break in the time series in 2025. The most went to Germany (49,023), Italy (33,384) and Portugal (25,594). Another 5,665 received one in Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland. Outside the EU and EFTA, in 2025, 98,835 Indian citizens became permanent residents of Canada (IRCC), and the UK granted Indian citizens 153,974 work, study and family visas (Home Office).

What this number is, and what it is not

  • It is the number of Indian citizens who received a residence permit in an EU country for the first time in 2025: a permit valid for at least 3 months, for family, education, work or other reasons. A permit also counts as "first" when the person had none for the same reason in the 6 months before.
  • It is not a count of moves: some people who receive a permit never move, or leave soon after, and someone who received permits in two countries is counted in both.
  • It leaves out Indians who also hold an EU passport (the statistics cover non-EU citizens only), stays shorter than 3 months and renewed permits.
  • It is not the number of Indians living in an EU country, which is much larger.

Questions people ask

How many Indians moved to Europe in 2025?

227,577 Indian citizens received a first residence permit in an EU country in 2025 (Eurostat). That is 35,396 more than in 2024 (192,181), a change of +18.4%. Another 5,665 received one in Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland.

Which European country do most Indians move to?

In 2025 the EU countries that issued the most first permits to Indian citizens were Germany (49,023), Italy (33,384) and Portugal (25,594).

How many Indians moved to the UK in 2025?

Is this the number of Indians living in Europe?

No. These are new permits issued in 2025. Indians who have lived in Europe for years, renewed their permits or hold an EU passport are not counted.
