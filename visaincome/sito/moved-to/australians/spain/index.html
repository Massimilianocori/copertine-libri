How many Australians moved to Spain in 2025?

Spain issued 774 first residence permits to Australian citizens in 2025, the main official indicator of how many moved there (Eurostat, data updated on 22 October 2026). That is 140 fewer than in 2024 (914), a change of −15.3%. By reason: family 290 (37.5%), education 190 (24.5%), work 151 (19.5%), other reasons 143 (18.5%). 612 of them (79.1%) were valid for 12 months or more.

What this number is, and what it is not

  • It is the number of Australian citizens who received a residence permit in Spain for the first time in 2025: a permit valid for at least 3 months, for family, education, work or other reasons. A permit also counts as "first" when the person had none for the same reason in the 6 months before.
  • It is not a count of moves: some people who receive a permit never move, or leave soon after.
  • It leaves out Australians who also hold an EU passport (the statistics cover non-EU citizens only), stays shorter than 3 months and renewed permits.
  • It is not the number of Australians living in Spain, which is much larger.

Questions people ask

How many Australians moved to Spain in 2025?

774 Australian citizens received a first residence permit in Spain in 2025 (Eurostat). That is 140 fewer than in 2024 (914), a change of −15.3%.

How many Australians moved to Spain in 2024?

914 Australian citizens received a first residence permit in Spain in 2024.

Why do Australians move to Spain?

The most common reason in 2025 was family (290 permits). By reason: family 290 (37.5%), education 190 (24.5%), work 151 (19.5%), other reasons 143 (18.5%).

How many Australians moved to Europe in 2025?

The 27 EU countries issued 9,339 first residence permits to Australian citizens in 2025; Spain issued 8.3% of them. All countries.

Is this the number of Australians living in Spain?

No. It counts only new permits issued in 2025. People who have lived there for years, renewed their permits or have an EU passport are not included.
