How many British people moved to Europe in 2025?

44,446 British citizens received a first residence permit in one of the 27 EU countries in 2025, according to Eurostat (data updated on 22 October 2026). That is 20 more than in 2024 (44,426), a change of +0.05%. The most went to Spain (11,949), France (8,235) and Germany (3,732). Another 4,691 received one in Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland. Outside the EU and EFTA, in 2025, 3,345 British citizens became permanent residents of Canada (IRCC).

What this number is, and what it is not

  • It is the number of British citizens who received a residence permit in an EU country for the first time in 2025: a permit valid for at least 3 months, for family, education, work or other reasons. A permit also counts as "first" when the person had none for the same reason in the 6 months before.
  • It is not a count of moves: some people who receive a permit never move, or leave soon after, and someone who received permits in two countries is counted in both.
  • It leaves out British people who also hold an EU passport (the statistics cover non-EU citizens only), stays shorter than 3 months and renewed permits.
  • It is not the number of British people living in an EU country, which is much larger.

Ireland: PPS numbers by month

Ireland also publishes a monthly count by nationality: 9,981 PPS numbers allocated to British citizens from January to September 2026, 12,634 in 2025 (Department of Social Protection, data to September 2026). A PPS number is not a count of arrivals.

Questions people ask

How many Brits moved to Europe in 2025?

44,446 British citizens received a first residence permit in an EU country in 2025 (Eurostat). That is 20 more than in 2024 (44,426), a change of +0.05%. Another 4,691 received one in Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland.

Which European country do most Brits move to?

In 2025 the EU countries that issued the most first permits to British citizens were Spain (11,949), France (8,235) and Germany (3,732).

How many Brits moved to Ireland in 2026?

Is this the number of British people living in Europe?

No. These are new permits issued in 2025. British people who have lived in Europe for years, renewed their permits or hold an EU passport are not counted.
