Canterbury-Bankstown citizenship ceremony wait time: 1,763 people waiting

On 30 September 2026, 1,763 people approved for Australian citizenship were waiting for a ceremony with City of Canterbury-Bankstown (NSW): 72% had been waiting less than 3 months since approval, 28% between 3 and 6 months and 1% more than 6 months (Department of Home Affairs, ceremony wait times published on 16 October 2026).

What the numbers mean

The figures count people whose citizenship application has been approved and who have not yet attended a ceremony, including people already invited, on 30 September 2026. The waiting time is counted from the date of approval to 30 September 2026: it is how long they had been waiting so far, not how long their whole wait will be. Home Affairs masks numbers from 1 to 4 as "<5", and some percentages do not add up to 100% because of rounding.

Ceremony dates and invitations

Home Affairs does not publish ceremony dates for each council: check City of Canterbury-Bankstown's website for its ceremony calendar. From the Home Affairs ceremony wait times page:

  • Citizenship ceremonies are usually organised by local government councils.
  • Which council-hosted ceremony you will attend is determined by your current residential address.
  • You cannot choose where and when to attend your ceremony.
  • You will be invited to attend your ceremony about four weeks before the event.
  • You will receive a letter of invitation from either your local council or the Department of Home Affairs.
  • The wait time for a ceremony depends on the demand in your local government area.
  • Having family members linked to your application may also affect your wait time.

How long the whole application takes

Nationally, 90% of applications for citizenship by conferral are processed within 5 months, 90% of approved applicants waited up to 6 months from approval to ceremony, and 90% up to 11 months from application to ceremony (Home Affairs, updated 16 October 2026). Questions people ask

How long is the citizenship ceremony wait time in Canterbury-Bankstown?

Home Affairs does not give one waiting time per council. On 30 September 2026, 1,763 approved applicants were waiting with City of Canterbury-Bankstown: 72% for less than 3 months since approval, 28% for 3 to 6 months and 1% for more than 6 months. Nationally, Home Affairs says 90% of approved applicants have the opportunity to attend a ceremony within 6 months of approval.

When are the Canterbury-Bankstown citizenship ceremony dates?

Home Affairs does not publish ceremony dates. Councils organise most ceremonies and many list their dates on their own website. Home Affairs says: "You will be invited to attend your ceremony about four weeks before the event."

Can I go to a ceremony outside Canterbury-Bankstown?

Home Affairs says: "Which council-hosted ceremony you will attend is determined by your current residential address. You cannot choose where and when to attend your ceremony."

Why is my ceremony taking longer than other people's?

Home Affairs says: "The wait time for a ceremony depends on the demand in your local government area. Having family members linked to your application may also affect your wait time."
