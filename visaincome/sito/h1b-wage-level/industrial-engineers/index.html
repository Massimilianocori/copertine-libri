Industrial Engineers: H-1B wage levels by area (2026-27)

For Industrial Engineers (SOC 17-2112), the salary that reaches H-1B wage Level II ranges from $73,216 in San Juan, PR to $122,158 in San Jose, CA among the 60 areas with the most jobs in the occupation (Department of Labor wage file for 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, read on 7 November 2026). Level II gives 2 lottery entries, Level IV gives 4.
