Petroleum Engineers: H-1B wage levels by area (2026-27)

For Petroleum Engineers (SOC 17-2171), the salary that reaches H-1B wage Level II ranges from $90,355 in Longview, TX to $174,554 in Anchorage, AK among the 55 areas with the most jobs in the occupation (Department of Labor wage file for 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, read on 7 November 2026). Level II gives 2 lottery entries, Level IV gives 4.

They depend on the area. Among the 55 areas with the most jobs, Level I starts between $59,197 and $146,141 a year, and Level IV between $122,346 and $231,379.

What is the H-1B Level II salary for petroleum engineers?

From $90,355 in Longview, TX to $174,554 in Anchorage, AK among the main areas; in 2 of the 55 areas it is above the US mean wage of the occupation ($157,150).
