Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Massimiliano Cori (Italy), who runs Visa Income Checker as an independent project. You can contact us through the contact form.

What we collect

Only what you type in the contact form: your email, the visa or country and your message. We also record the page or link you came from, to understand which pages need corrections. The calculator runs in your browser: the household, currency and income you type in it are not sent to us.

Exchange rates

To show up-to-date conversions, your browser downloads a small file of exchange rates from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), which receives your IP address as for any web request. No other data is sent.

Why

To answer your messages and correct the site. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes data for us

The site and its form are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which stores form data on our behalf and may process it outside the EU with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses). We do not sell or share your data.

Cookies

This site does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools. If we add advertising or affiliate links in the future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where required before any advertising cookies are used.

How long

Messages are kept for up to 12 months after our last exchange.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection (Articles 15–22 GDPR) and you can complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority in your country.

Visa Income Checker is an independent site, not affiliated with any government, consulate or immigration authority. Every amount is calculated from an official source (law, official gazette, ministry, immigration authority or statistics office) linked under the figure with the date we checked it. Requirements change, and consulates can ask for more: always check the official page or the consulate. Nothing on this site is immigration, legal or tax advice.

Exchange rates: euro foreign exchange reference rates of the European Central Bank (source: ECB), for information only.
