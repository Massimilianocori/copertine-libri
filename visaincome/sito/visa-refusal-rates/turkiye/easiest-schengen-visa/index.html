Questions people ask

Which Schengen country has the lowest rejection rate for Turkish applicants?

In 2025, Portugal had the lowest not-issued rate (3.5%) among the 27 Schengen countries that decided at least 500 applications made in Türkiye. The next lowest were Slovakia (6.5%), Romania (7.4%), Spain (7.4%).

Which Schengen country rejects the most applications from Türkiye?

Lithuania, with 35.5% of 3,287 decisions not issued in 2025, followed by Malta (34.9%), Estonia (30.6%).

Which Schengen visa is easiest to get from Türkiye?

There is no 'easy' Schengen visa: every country applies the same Visa Code, and you must apply to the country of your main destination. The figures show that in 2025 consulates of Portugal in Türkiye issued visas to a larger share of applicants than consulates of Lithuania, but each rate mixes different travellers, purposes and documents. Rates describe past decisions, not your chances.

Can I apply to the country with the lowest refusal rate?

Only if it is your main destination (where you will spend the most time) or, for equal stays, the first Schengen country you enter. Applying to a country you do not plan to visit mainly can itself lead to a refusal.

What "easiest" means here

  • Where you apply, not your passport. The Commission counts applications by the country where they were lodged: the figures for Türkiye include residents of other nationalities, and leave out nationals of Türkiye who applied elsewhere.
  • Not issued = refused plus applications whose examination was discontinued, ÷ (uniform visas issued + visas with limited territorial validity + not issued): the formula in the Commission's file.
  • Minimum 500 decisions. A consulate that decided 40 applications can swing from 5% to 30% in a year without anything changing; we rank only countries and consulates with enough decisions.
  • If you are refused, the consulate must tell you why and how to appeal (European Commission). Full method: how we calculate the rates.
