Netherlands Schengen visa rejection rate 2025: by country where you apply

Short-stay visa applications decided by Netherlands's consulates in 2025: how many were not issued, in each country where people applied. Official figures from the European Commission.

Schengen visa statisticsNetherlands

Netherlands · all consulates · 2025: 17.7% of decisions were visas not issued

746,011applications
607,324uniform visas issued
131,072not issued
20 of 28rank (1 = lowest rate)

In 2024: 15.5% (+2.2 pts). Highest rate among places with at least 500 decisions: Senegal (80.5%); lowest: United States (0.5%). Average for all Schengen countries in 2025: 14.6%.

Source: European Commission, Visa statistics for consulates 2025 (XLSX, downloaded on 15 October 2026); European Commission, Visa statistics for consulates 2024 (XLSX, downloaded on 15 October 2026). Short-stay (Schengen) visas, calendar year. All years on the Commission website.

Statistics, not advice. These are official figures for all applications made in a past period. They cannot predict the decision on your application, and a lower rate does not make a visa easier for you: you must apply to the Schengen country where you will spend the most time (or, for equal stays, the one you enter first), at the consulate responsible for the place where you legally live. Always follow the consulate's official requirements.
