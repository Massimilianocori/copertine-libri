Statistics, not advice. These are official figures for all applications decided in a past period. They cannot predict the decision on your application, and they mix very different applicants, courses and documents. INZ's declined figure also includes withdrawn applications, so it is not the same measure as the UK or Australian refusal rate. Always follow the official requirements of the immigration authority.

How to read these figures

  • By nationality = the country that issued the passport (INZ definition).
  • Declined, for INZ, includes applications that did not meet the criteria and applications withdrawn by the client or replaced by a new one. We divide declined by approved + declined, the applications INZ counts as decided.
  • Applications, not people: one application can include several people (INZ also publishes the people counts).
  • Financial years run from 1 July to 30 June; 2026/27 so far covers July to September 2026. Electronic travel authorities (NZeTA) are not visas and are not counted.
