What this share means, and what it does not. A multiple-entry visa "allows several visits to the Schengen area for as long as the visa is valid"; a single-entry visa allows one entry (European Commission, checked on 30 October 2026). Every visit is still a short stay, of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. A multiple-entry visa is not a long-stay visa, a work permit or a residence permit. The Commission's file only says whether each visa allows multiple entries: it does not say how long the visas were valid for. The share describes visas issued in the past, not your chances: the consulate decides the number of entries and the validity of each visa.

UK visa refusal rate for nationals of the United States: visitor, study, work and family visas

From July 2025 to June 2026, the UK refused 34.6% of visitor visa decisions for nationals of the United States (1,201 refused out of 3,475). 0.5% of study visa decisions were refused (16,938 decisions); 1.4% of work visa decisions were refused (11,487 decisions); 4.7% of family visa decisions were refused (3,659 decisions). For partner (spouse) visas alone the rate was 4.1% (138 refused out of 3,401 decisions; compare all nationalities). Unlike the Schengen figures, the UK counts applicants by nationality, wherever they apply. With only 3,475 visitor visa decisions, read this rate with care: where most nationals can visit the UK without a visa, the people who apply for one are a small and unusual group.

Canada: temporary resident applications from the United States approved (2025)

In 2025, IRCC approved 79.7% of the temporary resident applications from the United States that it processed (15,445 approved out of 19,370 people). This share mixes visitor visas, study permits and work permits, first applications and extensions; "processed" includes applications refused and withdrawn, so the other 20.3% were refused or withdrawn. In 2024: 79.7%. January to August 2026: 78.3% (16,845 processed).

No study permit or visitor visa refusal rate. For each country IRCC publishes only the number of new study permit applications processed (3,975 from the United States in 2025) and the number of visitor visas approved (15), never both for the same visa, so a study permit or visitor visa refusal rate cannot be calculated from the official files.

How to read these figures

  • Where you apply, not your passport. The Schengen figures count applications made in the United States, including residents of other nationalities, and leave out nationals of the United States who applied elsewhere.
  • Not-issued rate = visas not issued ÷ (uniform visas issued + visas with limited territorial validity + visas not issued), the formula in the Commission's file. Applications still pending at the end of the year are not counted.
  • If you are refused, the consulate must tell you why and how to appeal (European Commission).
  • Australia, New Zealand and Canada count applicants by citizenship (Canada: "source country"), like the UK, and each authority defines its rate differently: see the notes under each table.
