Statistics, not advice. These are official figures for all applications decided in a past period. They cannot predict the decision on your application, and they mix very different applicants, courses and documents. A lower rate for your country, sector or month does not make your application more likely to succeed. Always follow the official requirements of the immigration authority.

Country pages put the Australian figures next to the Schengen, UK, New Zealand and Canadian ones.

How to read these figures

  • By citizenship. Home Affairs counts applicants by the country of citizenship (for dual citizens, the citizenship of the travel document or the one nominated in the application).
  • Refusal rate = refused ÷ (granted + refused). Applications withdrawn or still being processed are not decisions. Grant rate = 100% − refusal rate.
  • Primary applicants outside Australia are students applying from abroad; family members (secondary applicants) and students already in Australia are in the country tables.
  • Financial years run from 1 July to 30 June. The current year (July to September 2026) is incomplete. The file is updated monthly and figures can be revised.
  • Minimum 500 decisions in 2025-26 for a nationality to appear in the main table.
