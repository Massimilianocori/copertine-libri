UK family and spouse visa refusal rate by nationality: July 2025 to June 2026

From July 2025 to June 2026 the Home Office refused 7.3% of UK partner (spouse) visa decisions (3,081 of 41,983) and 13.8% of all family visa decisions (9,197 of 66,787), according to its official immigration statistics published on 27 August 2026. Below, nationality by nationality. The partner figure is shown on its own because the family group also includes children, other relatives and refugee family reunion.

Questions people ask

What is the UK spouse visa refusal rate?

From July 2025 to June 2026 the Home Office refused 7.3% of decisions on partner visas, the route for spouses, civil partners and unmarried partners (3,081 refused, 38,902 granted). In the year before it was 7.2%.

What is the UK family visa refusal rate?

13.8% of all family visa decisions were refusals from July 2025 to June 2026 (9,197 of 66,787). The family group also includes children, other relatives and refugee family reunion: without partner visas, 24.7% of those decisions were refusals.

Which nationalities have the highest UK family visa refusal rates?

Among nationalities with at least 500 family visa decisions from July 2025 to June 2026: Nepal (39.6%), Albania (25.9%), Eritrea (25.7%), Somalia (23.8%), Syria (22.7%). The lowest: United States (4.7%), Yemen (5.6%), Thailand (6.2%), Morocco (6.3%), Iran (6.6%).

Does a low refusal rate mean a spouse visa will be approved?

No. Each application is decided on its own evidence: the financial requirement, the relationship, accommodation and English language. These rates describe past decisions for all applicants of a nationality, not anyone's chances.

How to read these figures

  • The Home Office counts applicants by nationality, wherever they applied, and an outcome in a quarter can relate to an application made earlier.
  • The refusal rate is refused ÷ (granted + refused): withdrawn and lapsed applications are left out, as in the Home Office "grant rate".
  • These are entry clearance visas, applied for outside the UK. Partners who switch to a family visa from inside the UK are not counted here.
  • The income needed for a partner visa is on our UK spouse visa income requirement page.
