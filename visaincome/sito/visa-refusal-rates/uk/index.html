Family and partner (spouse) visas

From October 2025 to September 2026 the UK refused 13.0% of family visa decisions (8,339 of 64,026) and 7.1% of partner (spouse) visa decisions (3,137 of 43,896). The family group also includes children, other relatives and refugee family reunion, which is why its rate is higher than the partner rate. How to read these figures

  • The Home Office counts applicants by nationality, wherever they applied. An outcome in a quarter can relate to an application made in an earlier quarter.
  • The refusal rate is the share of decisions (grants and refusals) that were refusals: withdrawn and lapsed applications are left out, as in the Home Office "grant rate".
  • Figures from 2026 Q1 onwards are provisional and can be revised in the next release (25 February 2027).
