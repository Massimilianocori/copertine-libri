What this share means, and what it does not. A multiple-entry visa "allows several visits to the Schengen area for as long as the visa is valid"; a single-entry visa allows one entry (European Commission, checked on 30 October 2026). Every visit is still a short stay, of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. A multiple-entry visa is not a long-stay visa, a work permit or a residence permit. The Commission's file only says whether each visa allows multiple entries: it does not say how long the visas were valid for. The share describes visas issued in the past, not your chances: the consulate decides the number of entries and the validity of each visa.

UK visa refusal rate for nationals of Nigeria: visitor, study, work and family visas

For partner (spouse) visas alone the rate was 10.9% (123 refused out of 1,124 decisions; compare all nationalities). Unlike the Schengen figures, the UK counts applicants by nationality, wherever they apply.

Canada: temporary resident applications from Nigeria approved (2025)

In 2025, IRCC approved 40.6% of the temporary resident applications from Nigeria that it processed (120,290 approved out of 296,495 people). This share mixes visitor visas, study permits and work permits, first applications and extensions; "processed" includes applications refused and withdrawn, so the other 59.4% were refused or withdrawn. In 2024: 34.4%. January to August 2026: 51.3% (160,775 processed).

No study permit or visitor visa refusal rate. For each country IRCC publishes only the number of new study permit applications processed (28,295 from Nigeria in 2025) and the number of visitor visas approved (45,460), never both for the same visa, so a study permit or visitor visa refusal rate cannot be calculated from the official files.

How to read these figures

  • Where you apply, not your passport. The Schengen figures count applications made in Nigeria, including residents of other nationalities, and leave out nationals of Nigeria who applied elsewhere.
  • Not-issued rate = visas not issued ÷ (uniform visas issued + visas with limited territorial validity + visas not issued), the formula in the Commission's file. Applications still pending at the end of the year are not counted.
  • If you are refused, the consulate must tell you why and how to appeal (European Commission).
  • Australia, New Zealand and Canada count applicants by citizenship (Canada: "source country"), like the UK, and each authority defines its rate differently: see the notes under each table.
