Canada: temporary resident applications from Singapore approved (2025)

In 2025, IRCC approved 80.1% of the temporary resident applications from Singapore that it processed (645 approved out of 805 people). This share mixes visitor visas, study permits and work permits, first applications and extensions; "processed" includes applications refused and withdrawn, so the other 19.9% were refused or withdrawn. In 2024: 80.0%. January to August 2026: 82.7% (635 processed).

No study permit or visitor visa refusal rate. For each country IRCC publishes only the number of new study permit applications processed (145 from Singapore in 2025) and the number of visitor visas approved (20), never both for the same visa, so a study permit or visitor visa refusal rate cannot be calculated from the official files.

Travel medical insurance

A Schengen visa application must include medical insurance covering emergency medical care, hospitalisation and repatriation (European Commission, checked on 15 October 2026).

How to read these figures

  • Where you apply, not your passport. The Schengen figures count applications made in Singapore, including residents of other nationalities, and leave out nationals of Singapore who applied elsewhere.
  • Not-issued rate = visas not issued ÷ (uniform visas issued + visas with limited territorial validity + visas not issued), the formula in the Commission's file. Applications still pending at the end of the year are not counted.
  • If you are refused, the consulate must tell you why and how to appeal (European Commission).
  • Australia, New Zealand and Canada count applicants by citizenship (Canada: "source country"), like the UK, and each authority defines its rate differently: see the notes under each table.
  • Full method: how we calculate the rates.
