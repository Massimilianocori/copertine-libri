Questions people ask

Which Schengen country has the lowest rejection rate for Chinese applicants?

In 2025, Luxembourg had the lowest not-issued rate (1.3%) among the 27 Schengen countries that decided at least 500 applications made in China. The next lowest were Croatia (1.9%), Norway (2.3%), Iceland (2.3%).

Which Schengen country rejects the most applications from China?

Malta, with 19.3% of 3,052 decisions not issued in 2025, followed by Slovenia (15.5%), Estonia (13.8%).

Which Schengen visa is easiest to get from China?

There is no 'easy' Schengen visa: every country applies the same Visa Code, and you must apply to the country of your main destination. The figures show that in 2025 consulates of Luxembourg in China issued visas to a larger share of applicants than consulates of Malta, but each rate mixes different travellers, purposes and documents. Rates describe past decisions, not your chances.

Can I apply to the country with the lowest refusal rate?

Only if it is your main destination (where you will spend the most time) or, for equal stays, the first Schengen country you enter. Applying to a country you do not plan to visit mainly can itself lead to a refusal.

Travel medical insurance

A Schengen visa application must include medical insurance covering emergency medical care, hospitalisation and repatriation (European Commission, checked on 15 October 2026).

What "easiest" means here

  • Where you apply, not your passport. The Commission counts applications by the country where they were lodged: the figures for China include residents of other nationalities, and leave out nationals of China who applied elsewhere.
  • Not issued = refused plus applications whose examination was discontinued, ÷ (uniform visas issued + visas with limited territorial validity + not issued): the formula in the Commission's file.
  • Minimum 500 decisions. A consulate that decided 40 applications can swing from 5% to 30% in a year without anything changing; we rank only countries and consulates with enough decisions.
  • If you are refused, the consulate must tell you why and how to appeal (European Commission). Full method: how we calculate the rates.
