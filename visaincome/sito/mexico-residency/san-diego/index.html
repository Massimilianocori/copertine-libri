Mexico residency at the consulate in San Diego: income and savings (2026)

The Mexican consulate in San Diego asks for a net monthly income of US$4,510 or savings of US$75,950 for a temporary resident visa, according to its official PDF (PDF dated 8 April 2026) read on 31 October 2026. For permanent residence it asks for a monthly pension of US$7,560 or savings of US$303,880. The visa application costs US$56.

How the figure compares with the rule in pesos

The guidelines' MXN 79,770.80 (680 × MXN 117.31) divided by US$4,510 gives a rate of about 17.69 pesos per dollar: the consulate converted the peso amount with its own rate when it updated the page (PDF dated 8 April 2026). At ECB rates of 30 October 2026 the peso amount is ≈ US$4,219.

Sources: Diario Oficial de la Federación, Lineamientos generales para la expedición de visas (SEGOB and SRE, DOF, 25 July 2025); INEGI, UMA 2026, press release 1/26 (8 January 2026). Read on 31 October 2026.

After the visa: the resident card

The visa lets you enter Mexico once; within 30 days you apply for the resident card at the National Migration Institute (INM) and pay its fee in pesos: MXN 11,141 for up to one year, MXN 25,058 for four years, MXN 13,579 for a permanent card (2026). All fees.

Source: Diario Oficial de la Federación, Annex 4 of the 2026 Miscellaneous Tax Resolution: updated amounts of the Ley Federal de Derechos (DOF, 28 December 2025). Read on 31 October 2026.

Questions people ask

How much income do I need for Mexican temporary residency in San Diego?

US$4,510 a month after tax over the last six months, or an average balance of US$75,950 over the last 12 months, according to the Mexican consulate in San Diego (PDF dated 8 April 2026).

How much do I need for Mexican permanent residency in San Diego?

A monthly pension of US$7,560 over the last six months, or an average balance of US$303,880 over the last 12 months.

How much is the visa fee at the consulate in San Diego?

US$56 per application, as the consulate's page says. The resident card in Mexico is a separate fee: MXN 11,141 for up to one year (2026).

Why is the figure different from other consulates?

The rule is in pesos (days of UMA). Each consulate converts it with its own exchange rate on the day it updates its page, so the same rule gives slightly different amounts.
