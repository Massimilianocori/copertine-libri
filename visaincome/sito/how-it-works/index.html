How the Visa Income Checker works

How we turn official rules into amounts, where the numbers come from and what can make them change.

1. The official rule

For each visa we read the rule in an official source: a law or decree in the official gazette, a ministry or consulate page, or the immigration authority's page. The rule is either a fixed amount (for example €3,000 net a month for Hungary's White Card, €50,700 a year for Germany's Blue Card) or a percentage of an official index (for example 200% of Spain's minimum wage).

2. The official index

When the rule depends on an index, we take the index from the law that sets it or from the statistics office, and show how we got the monthly figure:

Spanish minimum wage (SMI) 2026: €1,221.00 a month × 14 payments

€1,221 a month paid 14 times a year = €17,094 a year (Royal Decree 126/2026, articles 1 and 3). Over 12 payments this equals €1,424.50 a month. Spain sets a new SMI every year by royal decree (the 2026 decree was published on 19 February 2026, retroactive to 1 January).

  • Real Decreto 126/2026, de 18 de febrero, por el que se fija el salario mínimo interprofesional para 2026 (BOE-A-2026-3815) – Boletín Oficial del Estado, official gazette, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: Article 1: €1,221 a month. Article 3: the annual amount cannot be lower than €17,094.
  • SEPE – Cuantías anuales (SMI and IPREM 2026) – Servicio Público de Empleo Estatal, ministry page, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: SMI 2026: €1,221.00 a month, €17,094.00 a year (14 payments).

Spanish IPREM 2026 (public income indicator), monthly: €600.00 a month

IPREM = €600 a month (€7,200 a year), set by the 2023 State Budget Law and still in force in 2026. The IPREM changes only when a new State Budget Law (or a decree) sets it: check every January.

  • Ley 31/2022, de Presupuestos Generales del Estado para el año 2023, disposición adicional nonagésima (BOE-A-2022-22128) – Boletín Oficial del Estado, official gazette (consolidated text), checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: Monthly IPREM: €600; annual: €7,200.
  • SEPE – Cuantías anuales (SMI and IPREM 2026) – Servicio Público de Empleo Estatal, ministry page, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: IPREM in 2026: €600.00 a month, €7,200.00 a year, set by Law 31/2022.

Portuguese guaranteed minimum monthly wage (RMMG) 2026: €920.00 a month

RMMG 2026 = €920 a month (decree no. 139/2025 of 29 December, as cited by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs). Portugal raises the minimum wage every January.

  • Means of subsistence – National visas (Portal das Comunidades / Vistos) – Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Portugal, ministry page, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: Minimum monthly salary in 2026: €920; first adult 100%, second and further adults 50%, children under 18 and dependent children 30%.

Croatian average monthly net salary for 2025: €1,449.00 a month

Average monthly net salary paid in January–December 2025: €1,449 (Croatian Bureau of Statistics). The Ministry of the Interior states the resulting amount: 2.5 × €1,449 = €3,622.50. The average for the previous year changes each year (DZS publishes it early in the year); the Ministry then updates the amount.

  • Temporary stay of digital nomads – Ministry of the Interior of the Republic of Croatia (MUP), ministry page, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: 2.5 average monthly net salaries of the previous year (Official Gazette 14/21 and 3/26); currently at least €3,622.50 a month; +10% of the average monthly net salary for each family member.
  • Prosječne mjesečne neto i bruto plaće zaposlenih za prosinac 2025. (release RAD-2025-1-1/12, 20 February 2026) – Croatian Bureau of Statistics (DZS), statistics office PDF, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: January–December 2025: average monthly net salary per employee in legal entities €1,449.

Italian income level for exemption from healthcare co-payments: €8,263.31 a year

Law 537/1993, art. 8(16): ITL 16,000,000 a year; at the fixed conversion rate of ITL 1,936.27 per euro = €8,263.31 a year. Fixed in the law: changes only if the law or the decree is amended.

  • Legge 24 dicembre 1993, n. 537, art. 8, comma 16 – Normattiva (Italian State legislation portal), official legislation database, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: Exemption for households with total income below ITL 16 million (raised for a spouse and children).
  • Euro conversion rates of the former national currencies – European Central Bank, central bank page, checked on 10 October 2026
    What it says: €1 = ITL 1,936.27.

3. Family members

Some rules add an amount for each family member (Spain, Portugal's D7, Croatia, the UK partner visa when extending under the pre-2024 rules). Others set no amount for family members, and we say so instead of inventing one. The calculator counts "adults including you" and "children under 18". If you type your income, the calculator compares it with the minimum: monthly rules are compared month by month, yearly thresholds with your yearly total (your monthly income × 12, 13 or 14 payments). For the UK visas it shows the other conditions instead of a yes/no, because the going rate for your occupation also counts.

4. Monthly and yearly amounts

When a rule is monthly, the yearly figure is the monthly amount × 12, except where the official wording counts 14 payments (Spain's digital nomad visa: €2,442 × 14 = €34,188, which equals €2,849 × 12). When a rule is yearly (most salary thresholds), the monthly figure is the yearly amount ÷ 12. In countries that pay 13th and 14th salaries, the monthly payslip is lower than this figure.

5. Currency conversion

We convert with the euro foreign exchange reference rates published by the European Central Bank every working day at around 16:00 CET. The rates are read again by your browser when you open a page, so the conversion follows the latest published rate. For an amount in another currency (for example pounds), we first convert it to euros and then to your currency. Conversions are indicative: consulates usually check the amount in the official currency, sometimes with their own rate, and exchange rates move.

Source: ECB statistics – euro foreign exchange reference rates (daily XML). The reference rates are published for information purposes only.

6. Checking and updating

Every source shows the date we checked it. We re-check all programmes every January, when minimum wages, the Dutch and German salary thresholds, Denmark's pay limit and many other amounts change, and whenever an official page changes. If we cannot read an official source, we do not publish the visa.

Not covered yet

Greece (digital nomad visa and financially independent person visa), Cyprus, Malta, Estonia, Czechia, Romania, Latvia, Iceland, Costa Rica, Brazil, Panama, Colombia, Thailand, the United Arab Emirates and Mexico: the official page could not be read by our checks, gave conflicting figures, or (Mexico) the amount depends on the consulate. Colombia's rule is in Colombian pesos, a currency the ECB does not publish.
