Thailand DTV visa in Madrid: fee and who can apply (2026)

The Royal Thai Embassy in Madrid lists the DTV fee as €350 (fee table of the embassy, in Spanish, fees in euros; fee page last updated on 11 December 2024); on residence, the page says that applicants must be Spanish or Andorran nationals, or permanent residents of Spain or Andorra (our translation from Spanish) (official page updated on 22 September 2026, read on 30 October 2026).

The rule since 31 August 2026

Since 31 August 2026 DTV applicants must submit proof of permanent residence in the country or territory where they apply and a criminal record certificate (Department of Consular Affairs, announcement of 28 August 2026). Applications submitted before that date follow the old rules. What changed.

Sources: announcement of the Department of Consular Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Thailand, dated 28 August 2026 (in Thai), as reported by the Royal Thai Embassy in Moscow and the Royal Thai Consulate-General in Osaka (pages read on 30 October 2026).

Questions people ask

How much is the DTV visa in Madrid?

The Royal Thai Embassy in Madrid lists the fee as €350 (fee table of the embassy, in Spanish, fees in euros), on its page updated on 11 December 2024. Fees are not refunded.

Can I apply for the DTV in Madrid if I do not live in Spain?

Since 31 August 2026 applicants must submit proof of permanent residence in the country or territory where they apply. The Royal Thai Embassy in Madrid writes that applicants must be Spanish or Andorran nationals, or permanent residents of Spain or Andorra (our translation from Spanish): “deberán ser nacionales españoles o andorranos, o residentes permanentes del Reino de España o del Principado de Andorra”.

How much money do I need for the DTV in Madrid?

The Royal Thai Embassy in Madrid asks for financial evidence: a deposit of 500,000 baht or more (our translation from Spanish): “un depósito igual o superior a 500.000 bahts”.

Do I need health insurance for the DTV?

The DTV pages we read, this one included, do not list health insurance among the required documents.
