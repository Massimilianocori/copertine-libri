Health insurance for visas: what each long-stay visa asks (2026)

Spain asks for an insurer authorised in Spain with 100% cover and no co-payments, Germany says foreign insurance is not sufficient, Portugal and Croatia accept travel insurance, Thailand's LTR asks for USD 50,000 of cover, and the UK charges a health surcharge instead. One row per visa, from the official pages read on 7 November 2026; where the source says nothing, the table says so.

Not immigration or insurance advice. These are the words of the official pages on the date we read them. Consulates can ask for more, and each consulate decides whether your policy is acceptable: check with the consulate or visa centre where you apply before you buy.

The official words, visa by visa

Italy digital nomad visa

  • have health insurance for medical care and hospital stays, valid in Italy and for the period of the stay (our translation; official text: dispongano di una assicurazione sanitaria per cure mediche e ricovero ospedaliero valida per il territorio nazionale e per il periodo del soggiorno)
  • Letter or certificate of travel medical insurance showing coverage for medical expenses, hospitalization costs and repatriation for medical reasons, for the days you are outside the United States.
  • Insurance coverage must not be less than 30,000 Euro (or $50,000).

Sources: Decree of 29 February 2024 on digital nomads and remote workers, Gazzetta Ufficiale no. 79 of 4 April 2024 (Gazzetta Ufficiale della Repubblica Italiana, read on 7 November 2026); Consulate General of Italy in Boston, Digital nomad / remote worker visa (Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, read on 7 November 2026).

What the official page does not say: whether the insurer must be local or can be foreign. Ask the consulate or immigration office where you apply.

Italy student visa (university)

The circular of the Ministries of University and Foreign Affairs for 2026-2028 asks international students for Una adeguata copertura assicurativa per cure mediche e ricoveri ospedalieri (“adequate insurance cover for medical care and hospital stays”). Once you hold a study permit you can also register with the National Health Service for at least €700 a year. Health insurance for studying in Italy: the options in detail.

Source: Procedures for the entry, stay and enrolment of international students, academic years 2026-2027 and 2027-2028 (Ministry of University and Research (MUR) and Ministry of Foreign Affairs, read on 31 October 2026).

Croatia digital nomad permit

  • proof of health insurance for the period of time that you plan to be in Croatia (travel or private health insurance must cover the territory of the Republic of Croatia)

Source: Croatian Ministry of the Interior, Temporary stay of digital nomads (Ministarstvo unutarnjih poslova, read on 7 November 2026).

What the official page does not say: whether the insurer must be local or can be foreign; a minimum amount of cover. Ask the consulate or immigration office where you apply.

Hungary White Card

  • You must prove with a document that you are insured for the full range of health services, or that you are able to pay for the cost of health care. (our translation; official text: Önnek okirattal igazolnia kell, hogy az egészségügyi ellátások teljes körére biztosítottnak minősül, vagy az egészségügyi ellátás költségeit biztosítani képes.)
  • on the basis of the documents proving your means of subsistence, you are able to pay for the cost of your health care. (our translation; official text: a megélhetését igazoló okiratok alapján egészségügyi ellátása költségeit biztosítani képes.)

Source: National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing, White Card (digital nomad stay) (Országos Idegenrendészeti Főigazgatóság, read on 7 November 2026).

What the official page does not say: whether the insurer must be local or can be foreign; whether a travel insurance policy is enough; how long the policy must last. Ask the consulate or immigration office where you apply.

Mexico temporary resident visa

None of the 10 Mexican consulate pages we read on 31 October 2026 asks for health insurance for the temporary resident visa. Mexico residency: income and savings by consulate.

UK visas (Skilled Worker, family, Graduate, Ancestry, Student)

  • Most people need to pay the immigration health surcharge
  • You can start using the NHS free of charge from the date your visa starts.
  • £1,035 per year for all other visa and immigration applications
  • £776 per year for students, their dependants, and those on a Youth Mobility Scheme visa
  • £776 per year for visa and immigration applicants who are under the age of 18 at time of application
  • You do not need to pay the surcharge if you’re applying from outside the UK for 6 months or less.

Sources: GOV.UK, Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application (UK Visas and Immigration, read on 7 November 2026); GOV.UK, Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application: how much you have to pay (UK Visas and Immigration, read on 7 November 2026).

Questions people ask

Which visas require health insurance?

11 of the 15 long-stay visas in our table ask for health insurance or an equivalent: Spain, Portugal, Italy, Croatia, Hungary, Germany and the Thailand LTR. The Thailand DTV and Mexico pages we read do not ask for it; the UK asks for the immigration health surcharge instead of insurance.

Is travel insurance enough for a long-stay visa?

It depends on the country. Portugal and Croatia accept travel insurance; the Spanish consulate's study visa page says travel insurance is not valid; the Berlin immigration office says foreign health insurance is not sufficient for the EU Blue Card; the BOI's checklist for the Thailand LTR says travel insurance is not accepted.

Do I need a local insurer?

Spain asks for an insurer authorised to operate in Spain; Germany asks for German statutory or comparable private insurance. The Portuguese, Croatian, Hungarian, Italian and Thai pages do not say the insurer must be local.
