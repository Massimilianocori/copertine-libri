The points test in short

Three of these visas are points-tested and share one points table. The PR points calculator adds up the rows for you.

  • If you do not obtain a score of 65 points you will not be invited to apply for this visa.
  • You must be aged under 45 when we invite you to apply for the visa. You can still apply for the visa if you turn 45 after we invite you to apply.
  • At the time of invitation, you must have at least competent English
  • The maximum number of points that can be awarded is 20, this means that even if you score more than 20 combined points for your employment experience only 20 points will be awarded.

Sources: Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189), Points-tested stream (Australian Government Department of Home Affairs, page last updated on 5 November 2026, read on 19 November 2026); Points table for Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) (Australian Government Department of Home Affairs, page last updated on 4 November 2026, read on 19 November 2026).

Questions people ask

How much does an Australian visa cost?

It depends on the visa. From 1 July 2026 the base application charge in the Migration Regulations is AUD 250 for a Visitor visa applied for outside Australia, AUD 840 for a first working holiday visa, AUD 2,500 for a Student visa, AUD 4,015 for a Skills in Demand visa, AUD 6,135 for a Skilled Independent visa and AUD 11,710 for a partner visa.

Which Australian visas are points-tested?

Among the visas on this page, subclass 189, 190 and 491. Home Affairs says of subclass 189: “If you do not obtain a score of 65 points you will not be invited to apply for this visa.”

What are the requirements for Australian PR through a skilled visa?

For the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189) Home Affairs lists: “have an occupation on the relevant skilled occupation list”; “have a suitable skills assessment for the occupation”; “be invited to apply for this visa”; “satisfy the points test”. It also says: “You must be aged under 45 when we invite you to apply for the visa. You can still apply for the visa if you turn 45 after we invite you to apply.”

What is the difference between the 189, 190 and 491 visas?

All three use the same points table. Subclass 189 has no nomination; subclass 190 needs a state or territory nomination, worth 5 points; subclass 491 needs a nomination or a family sponsor, worth 15 points, and is a provisional visa for regional Australia.

Do I need a migration agent for an Australian visa?

No official source we read says you need one. Home Affairs says: “Only certain people can help you with your visa application. This help is immigration assistance.” “If you appoint someone to give you immigration assistance, they must be:”; “a registered migration agent”; “a legal practitioner, or”; “an exempt person”. Migration agents are registered with the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA). This site gives information, not migration advice.
