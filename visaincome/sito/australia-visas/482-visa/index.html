482 visa Australia (Skills in Demand): official fee, income thresholds and requirements

The Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482) is a temporary visa for a worker sponsored and nominated by an employer; it is not points-tested. Home Affairs says you need at least 1 year of relevant work experience and a salary that is no less than the income threshold of your stream. The visa application charge is AUD 4,015 for the main applicant and the same amount for each additional applicant aged 18 or over, from 1 July 2026. Official sources read on 19 November 2026.

What Home Affairs asks, in its own words

What the visa is

  • This temporary visa lets an employer sponsor a suitably skilled worker to fill a position they can't find a suitably skilled Australian to fill.
  • Have an approved sponsor and be nominated for the visa

Source: Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482) (Australian Government Department of Home Affairs, page last updated on 2 November 2026, read on 19 November 2026).

Core Skills stream: you must

  • be nominated to work in an occupation on the Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL)
  • be paid the Annual Market Salary Rate (AMSR) for the occupation and no less than the Core Skills Income Threshold (CSIT)
  • have at least 1 year relevant work experience in your nominated occupation or a related field
  • have a relevant skills assessment if this is required for your occupation
  • work only for your sponsor or associated entity, unless you are exempt
  • meet minimum standards of English language proficiency unless you are exempt from needing to show this.
  • Up to 4 years. Hong Kong passport holders may stay up to 5 years.

Source: Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482), Core Skills stream (Australian Government Department of Home Affairs, page last updated on 12 October 2026, read on 19 November 2026).

Specialist Skills stream: you must

  • be nominated to work in an Australian and New Zealand Standard Classification of Occupations (ANZSCO) occupation listed in Major Groups 1,2,4,5 or 6. The salary must meet the Specialist Skills Income Threshold
  • have at least 1 year relevant work experience in your nominated occupation or a related field

Source: Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482), Specialist Skills stream (Australian Government Department of Home Affairs, page last updated on 12 October 2026, read on 19 November 2026).

The income thresholds in the Migration Regulations

  • core skills income threshold means $73,150. Note: This amount is indexed under regulation 5.42A.
  • specialist skills income threshold means $135,000. Note: This amount is indexed under regulation 5.42A.
  • indexation day means the first 1 July to occur after the commencement of this regulation and each later 1 July.

The Regulations define both thresholds and add that they are indexed on each 1 July, so the amount in force can be higher than the amount printed in the definition; the Department publishes the indexed amount on its website. We could not read the current figure in the Home Affairs pages we read, so we give only the words of the Regulations.

Source: Migration Regulations 1994, compilation No. 291 (3 November 2026), volume 1: regulations and Schedule 1 (Federal Register of Legislation (Australian Government), read on 19 November 2026).

What these sources do not say: the income thresholds as indexed on 1 July 2026, the occupations on the Core Skills Occupation List, and what the sponsoring employer pays for sponsorship and nomination. The charge on this page is only the visa application charge of the worker and family.

Questions people ask

What is the 482 visa in Australia?

It is the Skills in Demand visa. Home Affairs says: “This temporary visa lets an employer sponsor a suitably skilled worker to fill a position they can't find a suitably skilled Australian to fill.”

What are the 482 visa requirements?

For the Core Skills stream Home Affairs lists: “be nominated to work in an occupation on the Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL)”; “be paid the Annual Market Salary Rate (AMSR) for the occupation and no less than the Core Skills Income Threshold (CSIT)”; “have at least 1 year relevant work experience in your nominated occupation or a related field”; “have a relevant skills assessment if this is required for your occupation”; “work only for your sponsor or associated entity, unless you are exempt”; “meet minimum standards of English language proficiency unless you are exempt from needing to show this.”

How much is the 482 visa fee in 2026–27?

The base application charge is AUD 4,015 for the main applicant, AUD 4,015 for each additional applicant aged 18 or over and AUD 1,005 for each one under 18; the second instalment is nil (Migration Regulations, Schedule 1, item 1240, amounts set from 1 July 2026).

How long can I stay on a Skills in Demand visa?

Home Affairs says: “Up to 4 years. Hong Kong passport holders may stay up to 5 years.”
