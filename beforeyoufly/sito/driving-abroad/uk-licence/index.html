Do I need an international driving permit with a UK licence? Every country (2026)

With a UK photocard driving licence, the official advice of GOV.UK (international driving permit table and FCDO travel advice) says you need an international driving permit in 56 countries and territories, you do not need one in 51, it depends in 82 (usually on how long you stay or on hiring a car), it is recommended in 4, and a local licence or permit is needed in 13; it says nothing about permits for 24 (last checked 28 October 2026).

  • 56IDP needed
  • 51no IDP needed
  • 82it depends
  • 17recommended or local permit

Rules change, and car hire companies can ask for more than the law. We quote the official travel advice word for word, with its date; this is information, not legal advice. If you are unsure, ask the embassy of the country you are visiting, your car hire company or your travel insurer before you travel.

Where to get an international driving permit

GOV.UK says:

You can get an IDP in person from certain shops that have PayPoint.

An IDP costs £5.50.

A 1926 or 1949 permit lasts for 12 months. A 1968 permit lasts for 3 years or until your UK driving licence expires, whichever comes first.

You must always have your driving licence with you, as well as your IDP.

Source: GOV.UK, Get an international driving permit (IDP) and Check if you need an IDP, checked on 28 October 2026.
