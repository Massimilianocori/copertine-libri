Do I need an international driving permit with an Australian licence? Every country (2026)

With an Australian driver's licence, the official advice of Smartraveller (Australian Government) says you need an international driving permit in 93 countries and territories, you do not need one in 7, it depends in 44 (usually on how long you stay or on hiring a car), it is recommended in 4, and a local licence or permit is needed in 6; it says nothing about permits for 25 (last checked 17 October 2026).

  • 93IDP needed
  • 7no IDP needed
  • 44it depends
  • 10recommended or local permit

Rules change, and car hire companies can ask for more than the law. We quote the official travel advice word for word, with its date; this is information, not legal advice. If you are unsure, ask the embassy of the country you are visiting, your car hire company or your travel insurer before you travel.
