Cabin bag size by airline (2026): free under-seat bag and cabin bag

How big can your free bag be, and when do you have to pay for a cabin bag? The official hand luggage sizes of 28 airlines, quoted word for word from each airline's own page with the date we checked it. 13 of them include a cabin bag for the overhead locker in every fare; the others include only a smaller bag that must fit under the seat.

Does my bag fit? Check your bag against all 28 airlines

Airlines change their bag rules and measure bags their own way (usually in a sizer at the airport, with wheels and handles). We quote each airline's official page on the date shown; the official rule and the airline staff decide. Check your booking before you fly.

New EU rule: a free personal item from 23 October 2027

Regulation (EU) 2026/2202 says that airlines “shall permit passengers to carry a personal item into the cabin and at no extra cost”, defined as up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm or a bag that fits under the seat in front. What the regulation says, quoted, and its exact status.
