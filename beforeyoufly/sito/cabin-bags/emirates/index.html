Emirates cabin bag size 2026: free under-seat bag and cabin bag by fare

Cabin bags · EmiratesUnited Arab Emirates

What can I take on board for free? Emirates free carry-on: 55 x 38 x 22 cm in the overhead bin · 7 kg

Official source: Emirates: Cabin baggage rules · checked on 18 October 2026 (read in a browser)

Airlines change their bag rules and measure bags their own way (usually in a sizer at the airport, with wheels and handles). We quote each airline's official page on the date shown; the official rule and the airline staff decide. Check your booking before you fly.

Flights boarding in India have a different rule: one carry-on bag whose length + width + height may not exceed 115 cm (quoted below). Our bag checker uses the 55 x 38 x 22 cm limit and does not check this sum.
