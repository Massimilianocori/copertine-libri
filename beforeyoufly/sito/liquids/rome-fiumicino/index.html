Rome Fiumicino liquids rules 2026: 100 ml or 2 litres?

Answer: 2 litres Up to 2 litres

Up to 2 litres per container; 100 ml for direct US/Israel flights from Terminal 1 and some transfers.

Official source: Aeroporti di Roma – Fiumicino security checks · checked on 10 October 2026 (the site blocks simple automated requests or needs JavaScript, so we read it in a browser)

Rules can differ by terminal, checkpoint or lane and can change at short notice. We quote the airport's official page on the date shown. Check with the airport (and your airline) before you pack, and check the airport you fly home from: it may still have the 100 ml limit.

What the official page says

Starting from 26 July 2025, in compliance with the new provisions of the European Commission, liquids in hand baggage may be carried in containers with a capacity of up to 2 litres.

Official source: Aeroporti di Roma – Fiumicino security checks · checked on 10 October 2026 (the site blocks simple automated requests or needs JavaScript, so we read it in a browser)

Exceptions and lane-specific rules (official)

Traditional rules apply on direct flights to the USA or Israel for airlines that check in at Terminal 1 and for transit passengers from non-Schengen countries not covered by “one stop security” agreements. In these cases, liquids must be separated, carried in containers up to 100 ml and placed in a transparent bag up to a maximum of 1L.

Flying back?

The UK government's advice is to check the rules “at the airport you’re flying from” and “at any other airports you’ll be travelling through, including your return journey” (GOV.UK, hand luggage restrictions). See the liquids rules at 38 airports, the US (TSA) rule and the Canada (CATSA) rule.

Medicines, baby food and duty-free

Exemptions are set out on the official page linked above. For UK airports, GOV.UK lists them on its hand luggage restrictions page; for EU airports, see Your Europe.
