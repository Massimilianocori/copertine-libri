Passport validity rules for UK travellers (2026): every destination

How long a British passport must be valid for 225 destinations, from the UK government's foreign travel advice (FCDO), quoted word for word. For 213 destinations the checker applies the rule to your dates; for the other 12 the official sentence has conditions we do not turn into a calculation, so we show it and you read it.

Source: GOV.UK foreign travel advice (FCDO), read through the GOV.UK Content API · last checked: 10 October 2026.

Schengen rules count your whole trip in the Schengen area: if you visit several Schengen countries, enter the day you first enter the area and the day you leave the last Schengen country.

The check runs in your browser: your dates are not sent anywhere. It applies the FCDO sentence literally (for example “6 months” is counted as the same day of the month 6 months later).

For British passport holders only. These are the rules published by the UK government (FCDO) for British citizens. If you hold another passport, check your own government's advice. The Schengen 10-year issue rule

For the Schengen area the FCDO says your passport must have a ‘date of issue’ less than 10 years before the date you arrive, and an ‘expiry date’ at least 3 months after the day you plan to leave. In the FCDO's words, “if you renewed your passport before 1 October 2018, it might have a date of issue that is more than 10 years ago, making it invalid for entry to the Schengen area”. The checker asks for the issue date only for destinations with this rule.
