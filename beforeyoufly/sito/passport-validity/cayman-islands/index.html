Cayman Islands passport validity rules for UK travellers (2026)

How long your British passport must be valid to enter Cayman Islands, as published by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO), quoted word for word, with a checker for your dates.

FCDO rule for Cayman Islands: Valid for the whole stay (until the day you leave)

Official source: GOV.UK – Cayman Islands travel advice, Entry requirements (FCDO) · page last updated 29 October 2025 · checked on 10 October 2026

Official text: “Passport validity requirements”

To enter the Cayman Islands, your passport must be valid for the duration of your stay.

You will be denied entry if you try to use a passport that has been reported lost or stolen.

You can enter the Cayman Islands for up to 30 days without a UK passport if you have photo identification and a certified copy of a UK birth certificate, or a naturalisation certificate.

Official source: GOV.UK – Cayman Islands travel advice, Entry requirements (FCDO) · page last updated 29 October 2025 · checked on 10 October 2026
