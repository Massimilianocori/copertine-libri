How tickets go on sale: the official rule

Additional tickets: every Wednesday at 12 noon

Additional tickets will go on sale every Wednesday at 12 noon throughout the following week, exclusively on the online purchasing platform.

Source: Cenacolo Vinciano: Info (tickets, hours) (Last Supper Museum (Museo del Cenacolo Vinciano)), checked on 30 October 2026. Quoted word for word.

We calculate these dates from the official rule.

Free first-Sunday tickets: the Wednesday before, at 12 noon

Free admissions related to the first Sundays of the month will be made available exclusively on the online sales platform https://cenacolovinciano.vivaticket.it/ starting at 12 noon on the previous Wednesday morning.

Source: Cenacolo Vinciano: Info (tickets, hours) (Last Supper Museum (Museo del Cenacolo Vinciano)), checked on 30 October 2026. Quoted word for word.

We calculate these dates from the official rule.

Blocks every three months

Reservations will open quarterly on the following schedule:

In September opening for rservations: November, December and January

In December opening for reservations: February, March and April

In March opening for reservations: May, June and July

In June, opening for reservations: August, September and October

*Admissions for September–December 2026 will go on sale Tuesday, June 23 at 12:00 p.m through the usual sales channels

Source: Cenacolo Vinciano: Info (tickets, hours) (Last Supper Museum (Museo del Cenacolo Vinciano)), checked on 30 October 2026. Quoted word for word.

The two official texts do not match: the table puts November and December in the opening held in September, but the notice on the same page put September to December 2026 on sale on Tuesday 23 June. The table also gives the month, not the day. So we do not calculate the date of the next block: check the official page.

Free days, closing days and opening hours

Free days: First Sunday of the month (booking still required)

Free admissions related to the first Sundays of the month will be made available exclusively on the online sales platform https://cenacolovinciano.vivaticket.it/ starting at 12 noon on the previous Wednesday morning.

On some days admission is free or reduced for all: tickets are usually made available on the preceding Wednesday starting at 12 noon.

Advance reservations for entrance to the Last Supper Museum are required at all times, for everyone (even infants) and for all types of admissions (even free ones).

Questions

When do Last Supper tickets go on sale?

Tickets open in blocks every three months, but the official page does not give the exact day of the next block. Extra tickets have a fixed day: “Additional tickets will go on sale every Wednesday at 12 noon throughout the following week, exclusively on the online purchasing platform.” The next releases are on Wednesday 4 November 2026, Wednesday 11 November 2026 and Wednesday 18 November 2026, at 12:00 Milan time.

Are Last Supper tickets released on Wednesdays?

Yes, additional tickets. The official page says: “Additional tickets will go on sale every Wednesday at 12 noon throughout the following week, exclusively on the online purchasing platform.” The next releases are on Wednesday 4 November 2026, Wednesday 11 November 2026 and Wednesday 18 November 2026, at 12:00 Milan time.

How far in advance are Last Supper tickets released?

The official page gives a quarterly table and a notice that do not match: “In September opening for rservations: November, December and January” “In December opening for reservations: February, March and April” “Admissions for September–December 2026 will go on sale Tuesday, June 23 at 12:00 p.m through the usual sales channels” Because of this we do not calculate the next block. Check the official page, and look for extra tickets every Wednesday.

How do you get free Sunday tickets for the Last Supper?

Even free tickets must be booked. The official page says: “Free admissions related to the first Sundays of the month will be made available exclusively on the online sales platform https://cenacolovinciano.vivaticket.it/ starting at 12 noon on the previous Wednesday morning.” The next one is Wednesday 2 December 2026 at 12:00 Milan time, for Sunday 6 December 2026.

Is the Last Supper open on Mondays?

No. The official page says: “Closed every Monday and on January 1 and December 25. (unless otherwise indicated by the superior Ministry of Culture).”

What is the official website for Last Supper tickets?

The official museum website is cenacolovinciano.org, and it sends you to its online sales platform: “Free admissions related to the first Sundays of the month will be made available exclusively on the online sales platform https://cenacolovinciano.vivaticket.it/ starting at 12 noon on the previous Wednesday morning.” “Tickets are named to prevent scalping.” Other websites may resell tickets or sell tours at a higher price.
