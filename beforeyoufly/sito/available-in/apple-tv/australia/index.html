Is Apple TV available in Australia?

Apple TV · Australia2026
Yes

Yes. Australia is on Apple's list of where the Apple TV subscription is available (Apple Support, checked on 28 October 2026).

Lists change: check the official page before you pay for a subscription.

Source: Apple Support: Availability of Apple Media Services, checked on 28 October 2026, page published on 30 September 2026. We copy the list as published and match each name to an ISO country code; we add nothing.
