Every country, every service

“Yes” means the country is on the provider's list; for Starlink, “Coming soon”, “No service shown” and “By region” describe the provider's map. For Google Wallet, “Yes, no tap to pay” means the app is listed but tap to pay is not; for Wise, “Not supported” means Wise lists the country among the places where it does not offer its services, and “Not for holding money” means you can send money there but the country is not on Wise's list for holding money. Uber's and Bolt's pages list cities, not whole countries; Stripe's list is for businesses opening an account; Google Fi's list is of the destinations its Flexible and Unlimited Premium plans cover abroad. How we check

We include a service only if the provider itself publishes a list of countries that we can read without a browser, and we read it again before every update. Services whose list exists only inside an app or a script, or that block automated reading, are left out. Country names are matched to ISO 3166-1 codes; regions inside a country (for example the Canary Islands) are not shown separately. More on the About page. Spotted a change? Tell us.
