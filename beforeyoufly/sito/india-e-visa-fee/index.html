Not in the tables

People also search for the India e-Visa fee for nationals of China, Pakistan, Nigeria, Egypt, Turkey, Afghanistan and Hong Kong. None of these is in either official fee table. The e-Visa page says:

Nationals of countries listed on the e-Visa website https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/ are eligible to apply for e-Visa Services.

International Travellers having Pakistani Passport or Pakistani origin may please apply for regular Visa at Indian Mission.

Afghanistan Nationals may apply online visa on New AFGHAN Portal(https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/avisa)

Source: Indian Visa Online: e-Visa (Bureau of Immigration, Government of India), checked on 4 November 2026. Quoted word for word.

Which e-Tourist Visa: 30 days, one year or five years

For e-Tourist Visa (30 days), the validity would be 30 days from the date of your first arrival in India.

For e-Tourist Visa (01 year / 05 years), the validity would be 365 days / 05 years from the date of grant of ETA with Multiple entries

Maximum stay in India during one Calender Year should not exceed 180 days for 1 year and 5 year Tourist Visa.

For e-Business Visa, the validity would be 365 days from the date of grant of ETA with Multiple entries and continuous stay during each visit which shall not exceed 180 days

Applicant's passport should have at least six months validity at the time of making application for grant of e-Visa.

Source: Indian Visa Online: e-Visa (Bureau of Immigration, Government of India), checked on 4 November 2026. Quoted word for word.

How and when to pay

Asked whether any website other than the government’s can take your application, the official e-Visa page answers:

No, https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/ is only the official website to apply for the e-Visa Services.

Services of e-Visa are completely online and no facilitation is required by any intermediary / travel agents etc.

Source: Indian Visa Online: e-Visa (Bureau of Immigration, Government of India), checked on 4 November 2026. Quoted word for word.

When to apply and pay:

The fee must be paid at least 4 days before the expected date of travel otherwise application will not be processed.

For e-Tourist Visa (30 days), applicants of the eligible countries/territories may apply online minimum 4 days in advance of the date of arrival. Such application can be made 120 days in advance from proposed date of travel.

Bank transaction charges of 3% will be charged additionally on applicable e-Visa fees.

Source: Indian Visa Online: e-Visa (Bureau of Immigration, Government of India), checked on 4 November 2026. Quoted word for word.

If your e-Visa is refused:

e-Visa fee once submitted is non-refundable as the fee is for processing of the application and is not dependent on either Grant or Rejection of Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA).

Source: Indian Visa Online: e-Visa (Bureau of Immigration, Government of India), checked on 4 November 2026. Quoted word for word.

Asked whether you have to pay anything else at the airport, the official e-Visa page answers:

No. You have already paid the required processing fee online. The applicants are advised to beware of unscrupulous elements/ travel agents/ intermediaries demanding extra payment.

Source: Indian Visa Online: e-Visa (Bureau of Immigration, Government of India), checked on 4 November 2026. Quoted word for word.

Questions

How much is the India e-Visa?

It depends on your nationality and on the type of e-Visa. In the e-Tourist table dated 8 September 2026, 138 of the 175 nationalities pay $10 for 30 days (April to June), $25 for 30 days (July to March), $40 for one year and $200 for five years; the others pay different amounts, and some pay nothing. Find your nationality in the table above. A 3% bank charge is added.

How much is the 5-year India e-Visa?

$200 for 142 of the 175 nationalities in the official e-Tourist table. The exceptions: Gibraltar $484, Guernsey $484, Isle of Man $484, Jersey $484, United Kingdom $484, Mozambique $220, United States $160, Russia $120, Japan $25, South Africa $25, Sri Lanka $25; 22 nationalities pay $0 (see the table). A 3% bank charge is added.

How much is the India e-Business Visa?

$120 for 105 of the 175 nationalities with a fee in the official table. The highest: United Arab Emirates $415, Gibraltar $242, Guernsey $242, Isle of Man $242, Jersey $242, United Kingdom $242, Ecuador $240, Philippines $220; some nationalities pay $0 (see the table). A 3% bank charge is added.

How much is the India e-Medical Visa?

$80 for 141 of the 176 nationalities in the official table. The exceptions with a fee: Gibraltar $129, Guernsey $129, Isle of Man $129, Jersey $129, United Kingdom $129, Russia $120, United States $100, Ukraine $85, Japan $25, Sri Lanka $25; some nationalities pay $0 (see the table). For every nationality the table gives the e-Medical Attendant Visa the same fee as the e-Medical Visa. A 3% bank charge is added.

How much is the India e-Visa for EU citizens?

All 27 EU countries are in the official e-Tourist table with the same fees: $10 for 30 days (April to June), $25 for 30 days (July to March), $40 for one year and $200 for five years. The e-Business Visa is $120 for most EU citizens, except Ireland $200, Czechia $190, France $165, Poland $145. A 3% bank charge is added.

How do I pay the Indian e-Visa fee?

Online, on the official website only. Asked whether any other website can take applications, the official e-Visa page answers: “No, https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/ is only the official website to apply for the e-Visa Services.” “The fee must be paid at least 4 days before the expected date of travel otherwise application will not be processed.” “Bank transaction charges of 3% will be charged additionally on applicable e-Visa fees.”

Is the Indian e-Visa fee refunded if my visa is refused?

No. The official e-Visa page says: “e-Visa fee once submitted is non-refundable as the fee is for processing of the application and is not dependent on either Grant or Rejection of Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA).”

How much is the India e-Transit Visa?

The two official fee tables do not give a fee for the e-Transit Visa: they cover only the e-Tourist, e-Business, e-Medical and e-Medical Attendant visas. The e-Visa page says the fee is set by country: “e-Visa fee is country/Territory specific.” Check the fee shown on the official website before you pay.
