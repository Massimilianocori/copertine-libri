What is zone A99 / AR?

High-risk flood areas that will be protected by a flood protection system (such as a levee) still under construction (A99), or behind a system that lost its accreditation and is being restored (AR). Almost all policies here are rated A99. Federal law requires flood insurance for buildings in high-risk zones (A and V) with a government-backed mortgage, in communities that take part in the NFIP (FEMA FloodSmart). Some lenders ask for it elsewhere too.

FEMA's data dictionary label: "A99 - Special Flood with Protection Zone; AR - A Special Flood Hazard Area that results from the decertification of a previously accredited flood protection system that is determined to be in the process of being restored to provide base flood protection". To find the zone of an address, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center; the zone printed on a policy is the one used to rate it.

This is what policyholders paid, not a quote. Two neighbors can pay very different prices: NFIP prices depend on the building (elevation, foundation, number of floors, replacement cost), the coverage and deductible chosen and the distance to water (FEMA FloodSmart: premium factors). For a price for your building, ask an insurance agent. Private flood insurance is not included in these figures.

FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote. Your price depends on your building, its elevation and the coverage you choose. Private flood insurance is not included. Nothing here is insurance, financial or legal advice.
