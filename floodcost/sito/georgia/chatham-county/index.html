Flood claims history

Since 1978, NFIP policyholders in Chatham County, Georgia have filed 5,475 flood claims. 3,962 received a payment, for a total of $82.6 million (an average of $20,856 per paid claim). The year with the most claims was 1999 (1,163 claims). Amounts are in dollars of the time, not adjusted for inflation.

FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote. Your price depends on your building, its elevation and the coverage you choose. Private flood insurance is not included. Nothing here is insurance, financial or legal advice.

This product uses the FEMA OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA. The Federal Government or FEMA cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from the Agency's website(s).
