Open WI-2026-02 · published Jul 13, 2026

Severe storms, tornadoes and flooding: deadlines postponed to November 2, 2026

“These taxpayers now have until Nov. 2, 2026, to file various federal individual and business tax returns and make tax payments.”
“Following the disaster declaration issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), individuals and households that reside in Bayfield, Brown, Buffalo, Iowa, Jackson, Jefferson, Juneau, Kenosha, Kewaunee, Manitowoc, Marathon, Milwaukee, Outagamie, Racine, Rock, Sauk, Vernon, Washington, Waukesha, Waupaca, and Winnebago counties, as well as the Oneida Indian Reservation qualify for tax relief.”

21 counties named in the notice: Bayfield County, Brown County, Buffalo County, Iowa County, Jackson County, Jefferson County, Juneau County, Kenosha County, Kewaunee County, Manitowoc County, Marathon County, Milwaukee County, Outagamie County, Racine County, Rock County, Sauk County, Vernon County, Washington County, Waukesha County, Waupaca County, Winnebago County.

The relief follows a FEMA disaster declaration. The new date has not passed yet (as of Oct 30, 2026). Read the IRS release WI-2026-02.

Open WI-2026-03 · published Oct 14, 2026

Severe storms and tornadoes: deadlines postponed to February 1, 2027

“These taxpayers now have until Feb. 1, 2027, to file various federal individual and business tax returns and make tax payments.”
“Following the disaster declaration issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), individuals and households that reside or have a business in Forest, Outagamie, Vilas, and Winnebago counties qualify for tax relief.”

4 counties named in the notice: Forest County, Outagamie County, Vilas County, Winnebago County.

The relief follows a FEMA disaster declaration. The new date has not passed yet (as of Oct 30, 2026). Read the IRS release WI-2026-03.

FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote. Your price depends on your building, its elevation and the coverage you choose. Private flood insurance is not included. Nothing here is insurance, financial or legal advice.

This product uses the Federal Emergency Management Agency’s OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA. The Federal Government or FEMA cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from the Agency's website(s).
