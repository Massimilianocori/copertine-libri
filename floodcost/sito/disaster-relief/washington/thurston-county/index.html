Deadline passed WA-2025-03

Severe storms, straight-line winds, flooding, landslides, and mudslides: deadlines postponed to May 1, 2026

“These taxpayers now have until May 1, 2026, to file various federal individual and business tax returns and make tax payments.”
“Following the disaster declaration issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), individuals and households that reside or have a business in Benton, Chelan, Clallam, Grays Harbor, Jefferson, King, Kittitas, Lewis, Mason, Pierce, Samish, Skagit, Snohomish, Thurston, Wahkiakum, Whatcom, and Yakima counties qualify for tax relief.”

16 counties named in the notice: Benton County, Chelan County, Clallam County, Grays Harbor County, Jefferson County, King County, Kittitas County, Lewis County, Mason County, Pierce County, Skagit County, Snohomish County, Thurston County, Wahkiakum County, Whatcom County, Yakima County.

The notice also names “Samish”, which we could not match to a county in the Census list (for example an independent city or a name spelled differently): read the official list in the IRS release.

The relief follows a FEMA disaster declaration. The new date has passed (as of Oct 30, 2026). Read the IRS release WA-2025-03.
