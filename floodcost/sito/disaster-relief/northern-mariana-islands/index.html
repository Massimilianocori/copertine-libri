Open NMI-2026-01 · published May 4, 2026

Super Typhoon Sinlaku: deadlines postponed to November 2, 2026

“These taxpayers now have until Nov. 2, 2026, to file various federal individual and business tax returns and make tax payments.”
“Following the disaster declaration issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), individuals and households affected by Super Typhoon Sinlaku that reside or have a business in the Northern Islands, Rota, Saipan and Tinian qualify for tax relief.”

The notice does not name the areas in a form we can match to counties automatically (for example a tribal area, a territory or a name we could not find in the Census list): read the official list in the IRS release.

The relief follows a FEMA disaster declaration. The new date has not passed yet (as of Oct 30, 2026). Read the IRS release NMI-2026-01.
