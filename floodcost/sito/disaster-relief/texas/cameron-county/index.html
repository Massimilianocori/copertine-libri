FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote.
