Open IN-2026-01 · published Sep 2, 2026 · updated Oct 8, 2026

Severe storms, straight-line winds, tornadoes, and flooding: deadlines postponed to February 1, 2027

“These taxpayers now have until Feb. 1, 2027, to file various federal individual and business tax returns and make tax payments.”
“Following the disaster declaration issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), individuals and households that reside or have a business in Carroll, Cass, Dearborn, Decatur, Delaware, Fayette, Franklin, Grant, Hamilton, Hancock, Henry, Lake, LaPorte, Madison, Marion, Morgan, Park, Porter, Pulaski, Randolph, Rush, Starke, Tipton, Union, Vermillion, Warren, and Wayne Counties qualify for tax relief.”

26 counties named in the notice: Carroll County, Cass County, Dearborn County, Decatur County, Delaware County, Fayette County, Franklin County, Grant County, Hamilton County, Hancock County, Henry County, Lake County, LaPorte County, Madison County, Marion County, Morgan County, Porter County, Pulaski County, Randolph County, Rush County, Starke County, Tipton County, Union County, Vermillion County, Warren County, Wayne County.

The notice also names “Park”, which we could not match to a county in the Census list (for example an independent city or a name spelled differently): read the official list in the IRS release.

The relief follows a FEMA disaster declaration. The new date has not passed yet (as of Oct 30, 2026). Read the IRS release IN-2026-01.

FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote. Your price depends on your building, its elevation and the coverage you choose. Private flood insurance is not included. Nothing here is insurance, financial or legal advice.
