Deadline passed MO-2025-01 · published Jun 25, 2025 · updated Jun 25, 2025

Severe storms, straight-line winds, tornadoes, and flooding: deadlines postponed to November 3, 2025

“These taxpayers now have until Nov. 3, 2025, to file various federal individual and business tax returns and make tax payments.”
“Following the disaster declaration issued by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA), individuals and households that reside or have a business in the City of St. Louis and in Scott and St. Louis counties qualify for tax relief.”

2 counties named in the notice: St. Louis County, Scott County.

The notice also names “City of St. Louis”, which we could not match to a county in the Census list (for example an independent city or a name spelled differently): read the official list in the IRS release.

The relief follows a FEMA disaster declaration. The new date has passed (as of Oct 30, 2026). Read the IRS release MO-2025-01.

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data.
