Flood insurance cost in Arkansas

What homeowners with a National Flood Insurance Program policy actually pay, from FEMA's records of 8,500 home policies in force on August 31, 2026.

Median annual cost of NFIP flood insurance for a home

$1,003a year

Half of insured homes paid between $728 and $1,331. That is 7% less than the national median ($1,082). The most common flood zone for insured homes here is zone AE (48%).

This is what policyholders paid, not a quote. Two neighbors can pay very different prices: NFIP prices depend on the building (elevation, foundation, number of floors, replacement cost), the coverage and deductible chosen and the distance to water (FEMA FloodSmart: premium factors). For a price for your building, ask an insurance agent. Private flood insurance is not included in these figures.

Flood claims history

Since 1978, NFIP policyholders in Arkansas have filed 10,260 flood claims. 8,223 received a payment, for a total of $220.6 million (an average of $26,831 per paid claim). The year with the most claims was 2011 (1,158 claims). Amounts are in dollars of the time, not adjusted for inflation.

FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote. Your price depends on your building, its elevation and the coverage you choose. Private flood insurance is not included. Nothing here is insurance, financial or legal advice.

This product uses the FEMA OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA. The Federal Government or FEMA cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from the Agency's website(s).
