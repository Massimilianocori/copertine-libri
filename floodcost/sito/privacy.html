Privacy

Privacy notice

What we collect (very little) and why.

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Massimiliano Cori (Italy), who runs FloodCost as an independent project. Contact: the contact form.

What we collect

Only what you type in the contact form: your email, the page or place and your message, plus the page you came from. The search box runs in your browser: what you type is not sent to us. If you switch the light or dark theme, your choice is stored in your browser only.

The data on this site

The figures come from FEMA's public OpenFEMA data, which FEMA publishes without names or addresses. We publish only totals and medians of at least 10 policies or claims.

Why

To answer your messages and correct the site. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes data for us

The site and its form are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which stores form data on our behalf and may process it outside the EU with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses). Netlify keeps technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. We do not sell or share your data.

Cookies and visit counter

This site does not use profiling cookies. To count visits we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter: it sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, builds no profiles and does not store your IP address, your full browser signature or any identifier. It keeps only aggregate numbers (page, hour or day, browser, country, screen width) on servers in the European Union and shares nothing with third parties; see GoatCounter's privacy policy. If we add advertising or affiliate links, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where required before any advertising cookies are used.

How long

Messages are kept for up to 12 months after our last exchange.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection (Articles 15–22 GDPR) and you can complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority where you live. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information.

FloodCost

FloodCost shows what National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policyholders actually pay, from FEMA's public policy and claims data. It is an independent site, not affiliated with FEMA, the NFIP or any insurer. These are past prices paid by other policyholders, not a quote. Your price depends on your building, its elevation and the coverage you choose. Private flood insurance is not included. Nothing here is insurance, financial or legal advice.

This product uses the FEMA OpenFEMA API, but is not endorsed by FEMA. The Federal Government or FEMA cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from the Agency's website(s).
