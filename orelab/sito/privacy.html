Who we are

orelab is an independent project run by Massimiliano Cori (Italy), who is the data controller for the information described here. You can contact us by replying to any email we send you.

What we collect on the waitlist

Cookies

This page sets no tracking or advertising cookies and uses no analytics tools.

How long we keep it

Until early access opens and for up to 12 months after, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, restrict or object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time (GDPR Articles 15–22). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).
