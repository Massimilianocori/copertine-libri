"For tenancies subject to ORS 90.600 (1) in facilities* with 30 or fewer spaces or for tenancy types subject to ORS 90.323, percentage is the lesser of: Ten percent; or Seven percent plus Consumer Price Index (CPI)." For 2027, the maximum allowable rent increase percentage for tenancies subject to ORS 90.324(b) is 10%. To view the methodology, calculation and Consumer Price Index data, see the Maximum Annual Rent Increase, 2027 (Excel file). The allowable rent increase percentage for the previous year , 2026, was 9.5%. Questions related to the administration of the maximum allowable rent increase and other landlord/tenant concerns should be directed to Oregon Housing and Community Services (OHCS).
