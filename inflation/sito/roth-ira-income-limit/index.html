How the reduced limit is calculated

The calculator follows the IRS worksheet for a reduced Roth IRA contribution (Publication 590-A, Worksheet 2-2) line by line: the part of your MAGI above the start of the range is divided by the width of the range ($15,000 for single filers, $10,000 for joint filers and for married filing separately), that share of the limit is removed, and the result is rounded up to the next $10, with a minimum of $200 when it is above zero. The worksheet asks for the ratio “rounded to at least three places”; we use the exact ratio, so a result can be $10 away from one worked out by hand with the ratio rounded to three places.

Worked example from the IRS worksheet (2025, single, MAGI $151,000, under 50): 1,000 ÷ 15,000 = 0.067; 7,000 − 469 = 6,531, rounded up to $6,540. The calculator gives $6,540.

“Divide line 3 by line 4 and enter the result as a decimal (rounded to at least three places). If the result is 1.000 or more, enter 1.000.”IRS, “Publication 590-A (2025), Contributions to Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs)”
“Subtract line 7 from line 6. Round the result up to the nearest $10. If the result is less than $200, enter $200.”IRS, “Publication 590-A (2025), Contributions to Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs)”
“Enter the lesser of line 8 or line 10. This is your reduced Roth IRA contribution limit.”IRS, “Publication 590-A (2025), Contributions to Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs)”

Common questions

Can I contribute to a Roth IRA?

You can if you have taxable compensation and your modified AGI is below the top of the range for your filing status: for 2026, under $168,000 for single and head of household, under $252,000 for married filing jointly, and under $10,000 for married filing separately if you lived with your spouse. Inside the range the limit is reduced.

How much can you put in a Roth IRA?

Up to $7,500 for 2026 and 2027 ($8,600 for 2026 and 2027 from age 50), minus anything you put into traditional IRAs for the same year, and less if your income is inside the phase-out range.

What is the Roth IRA income limit for married filing separately?

If you lived with your spouse at any time during the year, the phase-out range is $0 to $10,000 of modified AGI and it is not adjusted for inflation. If you did not live with your spouse at any time during the year, you use the single range.

What is the Roth IRA contribution deadline?

Contributions for a year can be made during that year or by the due date of the tax return for that year, not including extensions.
