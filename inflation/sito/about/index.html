About Inflation Calculator US·UK·DE

What this site is, where its numbers come from and how it is paid for.

Inflation Calculator US·UK·DE is an independent publication, part of a small network of websites that turn official data into plain tools. It converts amounts of money between dates with the consumer price indexes published by three statistics offices: the Bureau of Labor Statistics (United States), the Office for National Statistics (United Kingdom) and the Statistisches Bundesamt (Germany).

We are not a government website, a bank or a financial adviser, and we are not linked to any statistics office.

What we publish

  • Only index values taken from the official files, with the date we read them.
  • The formula and the two index values behind every result.
  • Where an index has a gap (such as October 2025 in the US), we say so and do not fill it.

Why the pages exist

People search for the value of a dollar from a given year, the inflation rate of a year, and calculators for the UK and Germany. We built a page for each question that real searches show, and no more: one page per US year, ten decades for $100 and $1,000, the inflation tables of each country, and the calculators.

How the site is paid for

The site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers never decide what we publish or change a figure.

Your data

The calculators run entirely in your browser: the amounts you type are not sent to us. See the privacy page.

General information, not financial or investment advice. The results show how an official consumer price index changed between two dates. Your own costs, pay and savings may have changed by more or less. Index values come from the BLS (US), the ONS (UK) and Destatis (Germany), linked on each page.

Inflation Calculator

Inflation Calculator US·UK·DE converts amounts of money between years with official consumer price indexes: the BLS CPI-U for the United States, the ONS CPI and RPI for the United Kingdom and the Destatis VPI for Germany. It is an independent site, not a government website and not linked to any statistics office.

Latest index: October 2026 (US), September 2026 (UK), October 2026 (Germany). Data read on 13 November 2026.
