What these prices are

The figures come from the Weekly Heating Oil and Propane Prices of the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), collected with the state energy offices (State Heating Oil and Propane Program). The EIA defines the residential propane price as:

“The price charged for home delivery of consumer grade propane intended for use in space heating, cooking, or hot water heaters in residences.”EIA, definitions, sources and explanatory notes
  • Taxes are not included. The EIA table is headed “Dollars per Gallon Excluding Taxes”.
  • It is an average for a whole state, for one day (Monday) of each week. It is not the price of any supplier, town or contract.
  • Revisions. The EIA notes: “Values shown for the previous week may be revised to account for late submissions and corrections.” When that happens the figures here change with the next update.
  • Averages per winter on this page are our own calculation: the simple average of the weekly prices published for the heating season (October to March). The EIA does not publish them.

Updates. The EIA collects these prices every Monday from October through March and publishes them the same week. This page shows the release of November 18, 2026; the next release is scheduled for November 25, 2026. We download the new file after each release and update the page once our checks pass. From April through September the EIA collects one price a month, on the second Monday, instead of one a week; those monthly prices are not part of the winter averages or of the weekly tables on this page.
