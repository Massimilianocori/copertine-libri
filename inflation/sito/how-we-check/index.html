How we check

How we check every index value

Our sources and licences, the checks the data must pass and what we could not read.

Every index value on this site comes from an official file that we download from the statistics office and keep with its address, the date we read it and a fingerprint (SHA-256). The current files were read on 13 November 2026.

Sources and licences

United States: BLS CPI-U

Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers, U.S. city average, all items, not seasonally adjusted (series CUUR0000SA0, 1982-84=100), monthly from January 1913 with the annual averages, read from the BLS data viewer. A second copy from the BLS Public Data API is kept to check it month by month. BLS data are in the public domain as works of the U.S. federal government.

United Kingdom: ONS CPI and RPI

CPI index (D7BT, 2015=100) and CPI annual rate (D7G7); Retail Prices Index long-run series (CDKO, Jan 1974=100), RPI (CHAW, Jan 1987=100) and its 12-month rate (CZBH), all from dataset MM23. Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v.3.0.

Germany: Destatis VPI

Verbraucherpreisindex für Deutschland (2020=100): the current monthly and annual table on destatis.de and the “Lange Reihen ab 1948” statistical report (VPI from 1991 and the index for all private households in the former West Germany, 1995=100). © Statistisches Bundesamt (Destatis); reproduction and distribution, also in part, permitted with the source cited. Contents of the GENESIS-Online database are under the Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0.

Before 1991 the German calculator links the West German index to the VPI at 1991 (factor 0.69551). This is our only calculated series and it is labelled on the page.

What the checks do

  • Continuity: no year or month may be missing between the first and the last value of a series. The only gap allowed is a month the office itself marks as not collected (October 2025 in the US), and it is shown on the pages.
  • Annual averages: the average of the twelve monthly values must match the published annual average within the rounding of the index.
  • Published rates: the rates we calculate from the indexes must match the rates the offices publish (ONS CPI and RPI 12-month rates, Destatis yearly and monthly rates) within 0.1 to 0.15 percentage points of rounding.
  • Official calculator: several results are compared with the BLS CPI Inflation Calculator, to the cent.
  • Two files, one series: where two official files overlap (BLS viewer and API; Destatis report and current table; RPI long-run and RPI 1987=100) they must agree.
  • Browser = reference: the calculator in your browser is run against our reference implementation on thousands of random cases.

What we could not read

  • download.bls.gov: HTTP 403 (Access Denied) to our server; we use the BLS data viewer instead.
  • www.bls.gov: HTTP 403 to our server (www.bls.gov); public-domain status of U.S. federal works per 17 U.S.C. § 105.
  • genesis.destatis.de: HTTP 401: the GENESIS-Online web service needs a registered account; we use the Destatis downloads instead.

We never try to get round a block.

When we update

Each office publishes a new month in the middle of the following month (the BLS and the ONS) or at the end of the month with a final figure in the middle of the next (Destatis). We download the files again each month, run the checks and update the pages only if all of them pass.
