Common questions

What is the rent increase in Ontario for 2027?

The rent increase guideline for 2027 is 1.9%; for 2026 it is 2.1%. On a rent of $2,000 a month, 1.9% is $38.00.

Does Ontario have rent control?

For most private residential units covered by the Residential Tenancies Act, the yearly increase is limited to the guideline unless the Landlord and Tenant Board approves more. The province lists exclusions, among them units first occupied for residential purposes after 15 November 2018 and a new tenancy after a unit turns over.

How much can a landlord raise rent in Ontario?

For most tenants, by no more than the guideline: 1.9% in 2027. At least 12 months must have passed since the last increase or the start of the tenancy, and the landlord must give written notice in the proper form at least 90 days before.

How is the Ontario rent increase guideline calculated?

Ontario says it uses the Ontario Consumer Price Index from Statistics Canada, with data from June to May for the following year’s guideline, and that the guideline is capped at 2.5%.
