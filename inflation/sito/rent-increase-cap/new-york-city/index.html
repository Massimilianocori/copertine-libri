The Rent Guidelines Board sets the adjustment for rent-stabilized apartments and lofts once a year; the current order was adopted on 25 June 2026 and applies to leases that begin between 1 October 2026 and 30 September 2027:

“Order Number 58 - Apartments and Lofts, rent levels for leases commencing October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027.”New York City Rent Guidelines Board, “2026-27 Apartment/Loft Order #58” · dated 25 June 2026 · read on 18 November 2026
“These rent adjustments will apply to rent stabilized apartments with leases commencing on or after October 1, 2026 and through September 30, 2027.”New York City Rent Guidelines Board, “2026-27 Apartment/Loft Order #58” · dated 25 June 2026 · read on 18 November 2026
“Together with such further adjustments as may be authorized by law, the annual adjustment for leases for apartments shall be:”New York City Rent Guidelines Board, “2026-27 Apartment/Loft Order #58” · dated 25 June 2026 · read on 18 November 2026
“any lease or tenancy for a period up to and including one year shall be deemed a one-year lease or tenancy, and any lease or tenancy for a period of over one year and up to and including two years shall be deemed a two-year lease or tenancy”New York City Rent Guidelines Board, “2026-27 Apartment/Loft Order #58” · dated 25 June 2026 · read on 18 November 2026

These guidelines are for rent-stabilized apartments only. They are not a limit for market-rate apartments, and the order itself allows for other adjustments authorized by law. This page cannot tell you whether an apartment is rent stabilized or what its lawful rent is.

Common questions

What did the Rent Guidelines Board vote for 2026–27?

On 25 June 2026 the board adopted Order #58: 0% for one-year leases and 0% for two-year leases of rent-stabilized apartments that begin between 1 October 2026 and 30 September 2027. The year before (Order #57) it was 3% and 4.5%.

What is the NYC rent increase for stabilized apartments?

For a lease that starts between 1 October 2026 and 30 September 2027: 0% on a one-year lease and 0% on a two-year lease. For a lease that started between 1 October 2025 and 30 September 2026: 3% and 4.5%.

How does rent stabilization work in NYC?

For the yearly increase: the Rent Guidelines Board sets a percentage for one-year and two-year leases, counted on the lawful rent charged on 30 September, and the order applies to leases that begin from 1 October to the next 30 September. Other rules of rent stabilization are outside this page.

How much can a landlord raise rent in NYC?

For a rent-stabilized apartment, by the Rent Guidelines Board percentage: 0% (one year) or 0% (two years) for leases beginning 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027, plus any other adjustment authorized by law. The board’s orders do not set a limit for apartments that are not rent stabilized.
