Common questions

What is the BC rent increase for 2027?

The 2027 rent increase limit for residential tenancies in British Columbia is 2.2%; the 2026 limit is 2.3%. On a rent of $2,000 a month, 2.2% is $44.00.

Does BC have rent control?

British Columbia limits increases for existing tenants: rent can only be increased once every 12 months, within the yearly limit set by the Residential Tenancy Branch, and with at least three full months’ notice.

How do I calculate a rent increase in BC?

Multiply the current rent by the yearly limit (2.2% for 2027) and do not round up: the province says landlords cannot round up when calculating the allowable increase. The calculator on this page does that; the province’s own calculator also gives the earliest date.
