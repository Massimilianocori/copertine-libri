Common questions

What is the average UK inflation rate?

With the CPI, prices rose on average 2.8% a year from 1988 to 2025 and 3.3% a year over the last ten years (2015–2025). Over the long run the RPI series rose 4.2% a year from 1900.

What were UK inflation rates over the last 10 years?

CPI annual rates published by the ONS: 2016: 0.7%; 2017: 2.7%; 2018: 2.5%; 2019: 1.8%; 2020: 0.9%; 2021: 2.6%; 2022: 9.1%; 2023: 7.3%; 2024: 2.5%; 2025: 3.4%.
