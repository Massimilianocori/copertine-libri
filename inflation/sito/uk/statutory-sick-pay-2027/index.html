Compared with inflation

The standard rate of Statutory Sick Pay rises by 3.5% from £123.25 to £127.55 a week. The latest CPI 12-month rate published by the Office for National Statistics is 3.5% (September 2026). The two figures measure different things over different periods: the rate is fixed once for the year, the CPI changes every month. Common questions

How much is Statutory Sick Pay?

The standard rate of Statutory Sick Pay is £123.25 a week in 2026 to 2027 and £127.55 a week in 2027 to 2028, a rise of £4.30 (3.5%). The DWP published the 2027 to 2028 figure as a proposed rate on 11 November 2026.

How much is SSP?

SSP is £127.55 a week at the standard rate in 2027 to 2028 (£123.25 in 2026 to 2027). GOV.UK says an eligible employee gets the weekly rate or 80% of normal weekly earnings, whichever is lower, for up to 28 weeks. The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.

What is the SSP rate from April 2027?

The proposed standard rate for 2027 to 2028 is £127.55 a week, against £123.25 for 2026 to 2027.

How much is Statutory Maternity Pay?

The standard weekly rate of Statutory Maternity Pay is £201.11 in 2027 to 2028, up from £194.32 in 2026 to 2027; the earnings threshold goes from £129.00 to £133.00 a week. Statutory Paternity, Adoption and Shared Parental Pay have the same standard rate. The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.

How much is Maternity Allowance?

The standard rate of Maternity Allowance is £201.11 a week in 2027 to 2028, up from £194.32 in 2026 to 2027. The Maternity Allowance threshold stays at £30.00 a week. The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.
