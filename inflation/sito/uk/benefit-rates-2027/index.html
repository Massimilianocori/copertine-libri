Benefit rates for 2027 to 2028

Universal Credit, PIP, Attendance Allowance, DLA, Carer’s Allowance and Jobseeker’s Allowance: the rate for 2026 to 2027, the proposed rate for 2027 to 2028 and the change, from the DWP document.

  • Figures quoted from GOV.UK

  • No advice, no products

What is official, and since when. The minimum wage rates for April 2027 were announced by the Government on 28 October 2026. The benefit and pension rates were published by the Department for Work and Pensions on 11 November 2026 under the title “Proposed benefit and pension rates 2027 to 2028”: they are the rates set out in the ministerial statement of 28 October 2026 and stay proposed until the legislation that brings them into force is made. If a rate changes, this page changes with the source. Sources read on 18 November 2026.

Compared with inflation

The Universal Credit standard allowance for a single person aged 25 or over rises by 4.3% from £424.90 to £443.21 a month. The latest CPI 12-month rate published by the Office for National Statistics is 3.5% (September 2026). The two figures measure different things over different periods: the rate is fixed once for the year, the CPI changes every month. Common questions

What are the PIP rates?

In 2027 to 2028 the daily living component is £79.40 a week (standard) or £118.60 (enhanced), and the mobility component is £31.35 (standard) or £82.80 (enhanced). In 2026 to 2027 they are £76.70, £114.60, £30.30 and £80.00. The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.

How much is Universal Credit?

The monthly standard allowance in 2027 to 2028 is £353.87 for a single person under 25, £443.21 for a single person aged 25 or over, £551.11 for joint claimants both under 25 and £695.71 for joint claimants when one or both are 25 or over. GOV.UK says the payment also depends on any extra amounts, any money taken off and earnings. The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.

What are the DLA rates?

In 2027 to 2028 the care component is £31.35, £79.40 or £118.60 a week (lowest, middle, highest) and the mobility component is £31.35 or £82.80 a week (lower, higher). The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.

What are the Attendance Allowance rates?

Attendance Allowance is £79.40 a week at the lower rate and £118.60 at the higher rate in 2027 to 2028, up from £76.70 and £114.60 in 2026 to 2027. The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.

What is the Carer’s Allowance rate?

Carer’s Allowance is £89.50 a week in 2027 to 2028, up from £86.45 in 2026 to 2027 (£3.05 more a week). The 2027 to 2028 figures are the proposed rates the DWP published on 11 November 2026.
