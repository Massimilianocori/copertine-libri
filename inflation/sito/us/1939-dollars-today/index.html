Value of a dollar

What is a 1939 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1939 = $24.28 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1939 has the same buying power as$24.28in October 2026. Prices are 2,328% higher than the 1939 average, and inflation in 1939 itself was −1.4%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1939 annual average 13.9; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1939

The annual average CPI-U went from 14.1 in 1938 to 13.9 in 1939: a change of −1.4%. From December 1938 to December 1939 prices changed 0.0%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1939 had the 103rd highest annual inflation rate.
