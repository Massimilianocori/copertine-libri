Every year's inflation from the official Consumer Price Index of the Bureau of Labor Statistics, with the value of a dollar from each year today.

US inflation in 2025 was2.6%measured with the annual average CPI-U. The latest 12-month change that the published index allows is 3.7% (September 2026).

Two ways to measure a year's inflation

Annual average change compares the average index of the whole year with the average of the year before. December to December compares the last month of each year. Both are calculated here from the official CPI-U published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (not seasonally adjusted, 1982-84=100).

Common questions

What is the annual inflation rate?

It is the change in prices over a year. With the CPI-U, the annual average for 2025 was 321.943 against 313.689 in 2024, an annual inflation rate of 2.6%. Over the 12 months to September 2026 the index rose 3.7%.
