Value of a dollar

What is a 1921 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1921 = $18.86 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1921 has the same buying power as$18.86in October 2026. Prices are 1,786% higher than the 1921 average, and inflation in 1921 itself was −10.5%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1921 annual average 17.9; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1921

The annual average CPI-U went from 20.0 in 1920 to 17.9 in 1921: a change of −10.5%. From December 1920 to December 1921 prices changed −10.8%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1921 had the 112th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1921 to 2025: 2.82% a year.
