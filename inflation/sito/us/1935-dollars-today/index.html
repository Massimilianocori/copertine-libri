Value of a dollar

What is a 1935 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1935 = $24.64 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1935 has the same buying power as$24.64in October 2026. Prices are 2,364% higher than the 1935 average, and inflation in 1935 itself was 2.2%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1935 annual average 13.7; October 2026 337.511.

Each row multiplies the 1935 amount by 337.511 ÷ 13.7 = 24.6358. The other way round, $100 in October 2026 buys what $4.06 bought in 1935.

The BLS publishes the 1935 index to one decimal place (13.7), so amounts converted from 1935 are accurate to about ±0.4%: $2,463.58 for $100 could be up to $8.99 higher or lower.

The annual average CPI-U was also 13.7 in 1932, so the annual-average amounts for that year are the same as for 1935. The months were different: see the monthly table below.

Inflation in 1935

The annual average CPI-U went from 13.4 in 1934 to 13.7 in 1935: a change of +2.2%. From December 1934 to December 1935 prices changed +3.0%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1935 had the 65th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1935 to 2025: 3.57% a year.
