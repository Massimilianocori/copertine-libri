What is a 1959 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1959 = $11.60 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1959 has the same buying power as$11.60in October 2026. Prices are 1,060% higher than the 1959 average, and inflation in 1959 itself was 0.7%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1959 annual average 29.1; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1959

The annual average CPI-U went from 28.9 in 1958 to 29.1 in 1959: a change of +0.7%. From December 1958 to December 1959 prices changed +1.7%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1959 had the 96th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1959 to 2025: 3.71% a year.
