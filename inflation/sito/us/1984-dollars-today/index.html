Value of a dollar

What is a 1984 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1984 = $3.25 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1984 has the same buying power as$3.25in October 2026. Prices are 225% higher than the 1984 average, and inflation in 1984 itself was 4.3%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1984 annual average 103.9; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1984

The annual average CPI-U went from 99.6 in 1983 to 103.9 in 1984: a change of +4.3%. From December 1983 to December 1984 prices changed +3.9%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1984 had the 30th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1984 to 2025: 2.80% a year.
