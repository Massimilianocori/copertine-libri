Value of a dollar

What is a 1927 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1927 = $19.40 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1927 has the same buying power as$19.40in October 2026. Prices are 1,840% higher than the 1927 average, and inflation in 1927 itself was −1.7%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1927 annual average 17.4; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1927

The annual average CPI-U went from 17.7 in 1926 to 17.4 in 1927: a change of −1.7%. From December 1926 to December 1927 prices changed −2.3%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1927 had the 104th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1927 to 2025: 3.02% a year.
