Value of a dollar

What is a 1933 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1933 = $25.96 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1933 has the same buying power as$25.96in October 2026. Prices are 2,496% higher than the 1933 average, and inflation in 1933 itself was −5.1%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1933 annual average 13.0; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1933

The annual average CPI-U went from 13.7 in 1932 to 13.0 in 1933: a change of −5.1%. From December 1932 to December 1933 prices changed +0.8%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1933 had the 108th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1933 to 2025: 3.55% a year.
