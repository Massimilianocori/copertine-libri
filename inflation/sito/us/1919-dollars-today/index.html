What is a 1919 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1919 = $19.51 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1919 has the same buying power as$19.51in October 2026. Prices are 1,851% higher than the 1919 average, and inflation in 1919 itself was 14.6%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1919 annual average 17.3; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1919

The annual average CPI-U went from 15.1 in 1918 to 17.3 in 1919: a change of +14.6%. From December 1918 to December 1919 prices changed +14.5%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1919 had the 4th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1919 to 2025: 2.80% a year.
