What is a 2002 dollar worth today?

$1 from 2002 = $1.88 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 2002 has the same buying power as$1.88in October 2026. Prices are 88% higher than the 2002 average, and inflation in 2002 itself was 1.6%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 2002 annual average 179.9; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 2002

The annual average CPI-U went from 177.1 in 2001 to 179.9 in 2002: a change of +1.6%. From December 2001 to December 2002 prices changed +2.4%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 2002 had the 78th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 2002 to 2025: 2.56% a year.
