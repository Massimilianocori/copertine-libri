Value of a dollar

What is a 1937 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1937 = $23.44 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1937 has the same buying power as$23.44in October 2026. Prices are 2,244% higher than the 1937 average, and inflation in 1937 itself was 3.6%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1937 annual average 14.4; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1937

The annual average CPI-U went from 13.9 in 1936 to 14.4 in 1937: a change of +3.6%. From December 1936 to December 1937 prices changed +2.9%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1937 had the 37th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1937 to 2025: 3.59% a year.
