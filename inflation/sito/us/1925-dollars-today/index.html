Value of a dollar

What is a 1925 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1925 = $19.29 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1925 has the same buying power as$19.29in October 2026. Prices are 1,829% higher than the 1925 average, and inflation in 1925 itself was 2.3%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1925 annual average 17.5; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1925

The annual average CPI-U went from 17.1 in 1924 to 17.5 in 1925: a change of +2.3%. From December 1924 to December 1925 prices changed +3.5%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1925 had the 61st highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1925 to 2025: 2.95% a year.
