Value of a dollar

What is a 1914 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1914 = $33.75 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1914 has the same buying power as$33.75in October 2026. Prices are 3,275% higher than the 1914 average, and inflation in 1914 itself was 1.0%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1914 annual average 10.0; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1914

The annual average CPI-U went from 9.9 in 1913 to 10.0 in 1914: a change of +1.0%. From December 1913 to December 1914 prices changed +1.0%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1914 had the 90th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1914 to 2025: 3.18% a year.
