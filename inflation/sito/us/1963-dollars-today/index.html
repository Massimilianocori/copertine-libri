Value of a dollar

What is a 1963 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1963 = $11.03 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1963 has the same buying power as$11.03in October 2026. Prices are 1,003% higher than the 1963 average, and inflation in 1963 itself was 1.3%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1963 annual average 30.6; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1963

The annual average CPI-U went from 30.2 in 1962 to 30.6 in 1963: a change of +1.3%. From December 1962 to December 1963 prices changed +1.6%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1963 had the 83rd highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1963 to 2025: 3.87% a year.
