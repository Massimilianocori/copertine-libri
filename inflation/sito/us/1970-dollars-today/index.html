Value of a dollar

What is a 1970 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1970 = $8.70 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1970 has the same buying power as$8.70in October 2026. Prices are 770% higher than the 1970 average, and inflation in 1970 itself was 5.7%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1970 annual average 38.8; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1970

The annual average CPI-U went from 36.7 in 1969 to 38.8 in 1970: a change of +5.7%. From December 1969 to December 1970 prices changed +5.6%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1970 had the 23rd highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1970 to 2025: 3.92% a year.
