Value of a dollar

What is a 1913 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1913 = $34.09 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1913 has the same buying power as$34.09in October 2026. Prices are 3,309% higher than the 1913 average.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1913 annual average 9.9; October 2026 337.511.

Each row multiplies the 1913 amount by 337.511 ÷ 9.9 = 34.0920. The other way round, $100 in October 2026 buys what $2.93 bought in 1913.

The BLS publishes the 1913 index to one decimal place (9.9), so amounts converted from 1913 are accurate to about ±0.5%: $3,409.20 for $100 could be up to $17.22 higher or lower.
