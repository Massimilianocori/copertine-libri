Value of a dollar

What is a 1929 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1929 = $19.74 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1929 has the same buying power as$19.74in October 2026. Prices are 1,874% higher than the 1929 average, and inflation in 1929 itself was 0.0%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1929 annual average 17.1; October 2026 337.511.

Each row multiplies the 1929 amount by 337.511 ÷ 17.1 = 19.7375. The other way round, $100 in October 2026 buys what $5.07 bought in 1929.

The BLS publishes the 1929 index to one decimal place (17.1), so amounts converted from 1929 are accurate to about ±0.3%: $1,973.75 for $100 could be up to $5.77 higher or lower.

The annual average CPI-U was also 17.1 in 1923, 1924 and 1928, so the annual-average amounts for those years are the same as for 1929. The months were different: see the monthly table below.

Inflation in 1929
