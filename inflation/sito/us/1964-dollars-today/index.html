What is a 1964 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1964 = $10.89 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1964 has the same buying power as$10.89in October 2026. Prices are 989% higher than the 1964 average, and inflation in 1964 itself was 1.3%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1964 annual average 31.0; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1964

The annual average CPI-U went from 30.6 in 1963 to 31.0 in 1964: a change of +1.3%. From December 1963 to December 1964 prices changed +1.0%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1964 had the 84th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1964 to 2025: 3.91% a year.
