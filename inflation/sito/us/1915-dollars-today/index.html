What is a 1915 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1915 = $33.42 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1915 has the same buying power as$33.42in October 2026. Prices are 3,242% higher than the 1915 average, and inflation in 1915 itself was 1.0%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1915 annual average 10.1; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1915

The annual average CPI-U went from 10.0 in 1914 to 10.1 in 1915: a change of +1.0%. From December 1914 to December 1915 prices changed +2.0%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1915 had the 92nd highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1915 to 2025: 3.20% a year.
