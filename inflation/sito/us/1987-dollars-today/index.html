Value of a dollar

What is a 1987 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1987 = $2.97 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1987 has the same buying power as$2.97in October 2026. Prices are 197% higher than the 1987 average, and inflation in 1987 itself was 3.6%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1987 annual average 113.6; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1987

The annual average CPI-U went from 109.6 in 1986 to 113.6 in 1987: a change of +3.6%. From December 1986 to December 1987 prices changed +4.4%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1987 had the 36th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1987 to 2025: 2.78% a year.
