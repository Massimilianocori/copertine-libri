Value of a dollar

What is a 1941 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1941 = $22.96 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1941 has the same buying power as$22.96in October 2026. Prices are 2,196% higher than the 1941 average, and inflation in 1941 itself was 5.0%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1941 annual average 14.7; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1941

The annual average CPI-U went from 14.0 in 1940 to 14.7 in 1941: a change of +5.0%. From December 1940 to December 1941 prices changed +9.9%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1941 had the 26th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1941 to 2025: 3.74% a year.
