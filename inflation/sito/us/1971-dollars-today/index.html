Value of a dollar

What is a 1971 dollar worth today?

$1 from 1971 = $8.33 in October 2026, with the official CPI-U of the US Bureau of Labor Statistics.

$1 in 1971 has the same buying power as$8.33in October 2026. Prices are 733% higher than the 1971 average, and inflation in 1971 itself was 4.4%.

Official CPI-U (BLS): 1971 annual average 40.5; October 2026 337.511.

Inflation in 1971

The annual average CPI-U went from 38.8 in 1970 to 40.5 in 1971: a change of +4.4%. From December 1970 to December 1971 prices changed +3.3%. Among the 112 years from 1914 to 2025, 1971 had the 29th highest annual inflation rate.

Average inflation from 1971 to 2025: 3.91% a year.
