SIMPLE IRA

SIMPLE IRA contribution limits for 2026 and 2027

Employee deferral limits, the higher limits for certain employers and the catch-up amounts, from the IRS notices for 2015–2027.

  • Figures quoted from IRS documents
  • 2015–2027, including 2027
  • No advice, no products

The SIMPLE IRA deferral limit is$17,000for 2026 and $17,500 for 2027. Some plans of certain eligible employers have a higher limit ($18,100 in 2026, $19,100 in 2027). From age 50 the catch-up adds $4,000 in most plans.

$17,500general limit, 2027
$19,100higher limit for certain employers, 2027
$4,000catch-up 50+, most plans, 2027
$5,250catch-up at 60–63, 2027

The IRS wording for 2027
“The limitation under section 408(p)(2)(E)(i)(III) that generally applies to salary reduction contributions under a SIMPLE retirement account or elective contributions under a SIMPLE 401(k) plan is increased from $17,000 to $17,500.”Notice 2026-66 · read on 13 November 2026
“The limitation under section 408(p)(2)(E)(i)(I) or (II) for employee elective deferrals for SIMPLE retirement accounts maintained by certain eligible employers is increased from $18,100 to $19,100.”Notice 2026-66 · read on 13 November 2026

Common questions

What is the SIMPLE IRA max contribution?

Employee deferrals are limited to $17,000 in 2026 and $17,500 in 2027 in most plans, or $18,100 and $19,100 in plans of certain eligible employers, plus the catch-up from age 50.

What is the SIMPLE IRA catch-up contribution?

For employees aged 50 or over: $4,000 for 2026 and 2027 in most plans, $3,850 in the higher-limit plans, and $5,250 for those who turn 60, 61, 62 or 63 during the year.
