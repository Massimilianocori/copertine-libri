How this list works

What it is

HUD keeps the official list of condominium projects approved for FHA-insured mortgages in its FHA Condominium Lookup, a search form that shows 25 results at a time. Every week we download the whole list for all 54 states and territories and publish it by place. 6,518 of the 6,535 current approvals run exactly three years, the standard term set by HUD's rule.

What it is not

It is not HUD's site and not a loan approval. An approval can expire, be renewed or be withdrawn after our last download, and some approvals cover only part of a project. Always confirm with the HUD lookup and your lender. Read the methodology.

Source

U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), FHA Condominium Lookup: projects with status "Approved", plus projects whose approval expired or was withdrawn in the last 24 months. HUD data as of October 26, 2026; we re-download the full list every week (last download October 26, 2026).

HUD's list is public-domain U.S. government data. We show the project name, address, county, unit count (when HUD states one), FHA concentration and dates. How we build this list.
