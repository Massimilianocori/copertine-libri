Methodology

How we build the list

Where the data comes from, how we check it and what we leave out. HUD data as of October 17, 2026.

Source

Everything on this site comes from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's FHA Condominium Lookup, the official search of condominium projects approved for FHA-insured mortgages. HUD's page states the date of its data ("as of"); the current edition is October 17, 2026.

How we download it

  • Every week we run HUD's search for each of the 54 states and territories in its menu, with the search type "Project" (one row per condominium project; the separate rows for phases, which HUD calls submissions, are not repeated).
  • Three searches per state: status "Approved"; status "Expired" with an expiration date in the last 24 months; status "Withdrawn" with a status date in the last 24 months.
  • HUD shows 25 results per page. We read every page, one request at a time, a second or two apart, and check that the rows we read match the total HUD reports for each search. If they do not match, the update stops and the site is not changed.
  • Each week we also look up a random sample of 20 projects again, one by one, on HUD's lookup and compare every field we publish.

What we publish

  • Project name, street, city, ZIP code and county, as HUD lists them (we convert HUD's capital letters to normal capitalization).
  • HUD condo ID, to look the project up on HUD's site.
  • Units: HUD has no separate field for the number of units, only a free-text description ("Composition of Project", for example "48 UNITS, 9 BUILDINGS"). We take the number only when the text gives one clear figure, a total ("168 total units") or a single number followed by "units"; numbers of buildings or phases and figures such as "12 units per building" are ignored. Today 5,531 of 6,539 approved projects have a unit count; the others show "–".
  • FHA share: HUD's "FHA Concentration", the share of units with FHA-insured mortgages as listed by HUD.
  • Approved until: HUD's expiration date. "Expiring soon" means within 6 months of HUD's data date (by April 17, 2027).
  • Recently expired: projects with status "Expired" and an expiration date from October 17, 2024 to October 17, 2026; recently withdrawn: status "Withdrawn" with a status date in the same period. HUD's "Expired" search also returns a few projects whose approval ends on the data date itself and that still show "Approved" (11 today): we list them among the recent ones as "Approval ends".

We do not publish HUD's free-text description or its comments: they sometimes contain internal notes, and we cannot check them one by one. They are on HUD's page for each project.

Which places get a page

  • States and territories: every one with at least one approved project, or one that expired or was withdrawn in the last 24 months. The state page lists all its approved projects; California, with more than 700, lists the ones that are not on a county or city page.
  • Counties: at least 10 approved projects. Cities: at least 8. Smaller places are in the tables of the state or county page and in the search, so every project is listed somewhere. A county page is skipped when it would repeat the list of its state; when a city and its county have almost the same list (at least 90% of projects in common), there is a single page, the city's, which names the county.
  • Cities are grouped by name within a state ("ST PETERSBURG" and "SAINT PETERSBURG" are the same city). Counties are as written by HUD.

Limits

  • HUD updates its list every day; we update weekly. A project can be approved, renewed or withdrawn after our last download.
  • Some approvals cover only certain phases or buildings. The list shows the project, not each unit.
  • Names and addresses are HUD's, including its abbreviations and occasional typos.
  • This is not a loan approval. The lender checks the project and the loan.

ApprovedCondos republishes HUD's public list of FHA-approved condominium projects, by state, county and city. It is an independent site, not affiliated with HUD, FHA, VA or any lender.
