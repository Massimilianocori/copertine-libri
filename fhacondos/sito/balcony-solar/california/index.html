Is balcony solar legal in California?

Plug-in solar rules for condo owners and renters in California: the law, the limits and what your association can ban. Checked on November 6, 2026.

  • Official sources linked
  • Checked November 6, 2026
  • No legal or electrical advice

Yes, a law is coming: as of November 6, 2026, Governor Newsom has signed SB 868 on portable solar generation devices (September 30, 2026), but we could not read its text on the Legislature's site, so check the details there.[1][2][3]

What the law says

  • On September 30, 2026 the governor announced that he had signed SB 868 by Senator Scott Wiener, "Electricity: portable solar generation devices".[1]
  • The Legislature's site refused our request (403), so we have not read the text, the limits or the start date.[2]
  • Bright Saver's tracker gives January 1, 2027 as the start date, a 1,200-watt limit and devices that plug into standard 120-volt outlets, with a notice to the utility that may be required after installation.[3]

Limits: watts, outlet and utility

  • We have not read the limits in the law itself. According to Bright Saver (third party): up to 1,200 watts, standard 120-volt outlet, no interconnection with the utility.[2][3]

Condos and HOAs: what the association can and cannot ban

  • We could not read whether SB 868 says anything about condominium associations or HOAs. Until you can, treat your association's rules as still in force and ask the board in writing.[2]

Before you buy a kit, check three things. 1. The law itself, through the links in the sources below: laws change, and this page summarizes them. 2. Your electric utility: whether it wants a notice or a form. 3. Your condo or HOA documents (declaration, bylaws, rules) and the board's written answer about balconies and railings.

This page is general information, not legal or electrical advice. For your rights, ask a lawyer licensed in your state; for wiring, outlets and panels, ask a licensed electrician.

Is balcony solar legal in California?

What is SB 868 in California?

SB 868 is a 2026 bill by Senator Scott Wiener titled "Electricity: portable solar generation devices". The governor announced on September 30, 2026 that he had signed it.
