About ApprovedCondos

An independent site that makes HUD's list of FHA-approved condos easy to find and read.

ApprovedCondos answers one question that decides many condo purchases: can I buy this condo with an FHA loan?

HUD publishes the official answer in its FHA Condominium Lookup, a search form that shows 25 results at a time and only after you submit a search. We copy the whole list every week and publish it by state, county and city, with the expiration date of each approval, the approvals that expire soon and the ones that expired recently.

Rules we follow

  • Every listing comes from HUD's data, with the HUD date on every page and a link to the official lookup.
  • We check our copy against HUD's totals every week and re-check a random sample of projects one by one.
  • We publish only information about condominium projects: names, addresses, counts and dates. No personal data.
  • No advice: this is information to check with HUD and your lender, not a loan approval.

Who we are

ApprovedCondos is an independent project run by Massimiliano Cori. It is not affiliated with HUD, FHA, VA, any lender or any real estate company. The site may show advertising and, in the future, clearly marked links to lenders; they never change the list.

Corrections

Found a mistake? Write to us; we check it against HUD's lookup and correct the page at the next update.
