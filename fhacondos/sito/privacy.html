Privacy

Privacy notice

What we collect (very little) and why. No cookies, no tracking.

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Massimiliano Cori (Italy), who runs ApprovedCondos as an independent project. Contact: the contact form.

What we collect

Only what you type in the contact form: your email, the project or page and your message, plus the page you came from. The search box and the table filters run in your browser: what you type is not sent to us. If you switch the light or dark theme, your choice is stored in your browser only.

The data on this site

The listings come from HUD's public FHA Condominium Lookup. We publish only information about condominium projects (name, address, county, unit count, FHA share and dates), not the names or contact details of people.

Why

To answer your messages and correct the site. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

Who processes data for us

The site and its form are hosted by Netlify, Inc., which stores form data on our behalf and may process it outside the EU with the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses). Netlify keeps technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. We do not sell or share your data.

Cookies and visit counter

This site sets no cookies. To count visits we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter: it sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, builds no profiles and does not store your IP address, your full browser signature or any identifier. It keeps only aggregate numbers (page, hour or day, browser, country, screen width) on servers in the European Union and shares nothing with third parties; see GoatCounter's privacy policy. If we add advertising or affiliate links, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where required before any advertising cookies are used.

How long

Messages are kept for up to 12 months after our last exchange.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection (Articles 15–22 GDPR) and you can complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the authority where you live. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information.

ApprovedCondos

ApprovedCondos republishes HUD's public list of FHA-approved condominium projects, by state, county and city. It is an independent site, not affiliated with HUD, FHA, VA or any lender. Approval can change at any time: confirm a project's current status in the official HUD lookup and with your lender before you rely on it. Nothing here is mortgage, legal or financial advice.
