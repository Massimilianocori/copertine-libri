Approvals expiring in the next 6 months (25)

Renewal can be requested from six months before to six months after the expiration date (24 CFR 203.43b(h)(4)), so an approval that is about to expire may be renewed, or it may lapse. If you are buying in one of these projects, ask your lender to check the status again shortly before closing.
