What the chart covers

Schedule 8 sets the “rates of monthly basic pay (37 U.S.C. 203(a)) for members of the uniformed services”, so the same table applies in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force and Coast Guard.[1] By law the rates rise every January 1 by the percentage change in the Employment Cost Index for private-industry wages, unless Congress or the President sets a different figure.[6] See the raise history and the status of the 2027 raise.

Basic pay is one element of military compensation. The law lists the basic allowance for subsistence and the basic allowance for housing as separate elements;[6] this site covers basic pay only.

Travel rates

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

When did the 2026 military pay chart take effect?

On January 1, 2026. The rates are in Schedule 8 of Executive Order 14368 of December 18, 2025, published in the Federal Register on December 23, 2025.[1]

Is the pay chart the same for the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Space Force and Coast Guard?

Yes. Schedule 8 sets one table of monthly basic pay for members of the uniformed services, and its notes name senior positions in all of these services.[1]

How much do warrant officers make in 2026?

Monthly basic pay runs from $4,056.60 for a W-1 with 2 years or less of service to $13,308.30 for a W-5 with over 38 years.[1]

Is basic pay the whole military paycheck?

No. Basic pay is one element of compensation; the law lists the basic allowance for subsistence and the basic allowance for housing as separate elements. This site covers basic pay only.[6]
