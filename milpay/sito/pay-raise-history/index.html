Raise history

Military Pay Raise History, 2017–2026

Every January raise measured on the official Federal Register pay tables, next to the figure from the Employment Cost Index formula in the law.

Each January 1 the basic pay tables of the uniformed services go up by one percentage for all grades, unless Congress or the President sets a different figure.[13] Below, the raise is measured directly on the official tables: for each year we compare the same cells (grade × years of service) in two consecutive Schedule 8 tables of the Federal Register and take the median change, rounded to 0.1%. Next to it is the percentage the law’s formula gives: the 12-month change in the Employment Cost Index for private-industry wages and salaries in the July–September quarter two years earlier.[4],[13]

“Cells at that raise”: how many of the cells we could compare changed by the median percentage (within 0.06 points). The others are mostly rows that changed by a different amount and cells at a monthly cap. “Not read”: for January 2021 too few cells of the 2020 and 2021 tables (scanned images) could be read reliably, so we show only the formula figure. The ECI figure for January 2017 is not in the data we read.

What the table shows

In every year we could measure, the raise in the table equals the formula figure from the Employment Cost Index. In the 2026 table, rows E-1 to E-5 are more than 3.8% above the January 2025 table at some years of service; the 2026 rates on the E-1 to E-5 pages are the figures in force.[11],[12]

The law also lets the President send Congress an alternative pay plan before September 1 of the previous year, in a national emergency or serious economic conditions.[13]

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

How much did military pay go up each year?

From 2018 to 2026 the January raises were 2.4%, 2.6%, 3.1%, 3.0% (formula figure), 2.7%, 4.6%, 5.2%, 4.5% and 3.8%, measured on the official pay tables.[13]

When do military pay raises take effect?

On January 1. The law says the rates of basic pay are increased effective on January 1 of each year.[13]
