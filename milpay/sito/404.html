Military Pay Chart

Military Pay Chart publishes the 2026 monthly basic pay tables of the U.S. uniformed services, copied from Schedule 8 of Executive Order 14368 in the Federal Register, with a page for every pay grade, and the GSA per diem rates for federal travel in the continental U.S. Independent site, not a government website.

Official tables read on November 13, 2026.
