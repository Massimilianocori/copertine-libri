California Per Diem Rates FY 2027

Maximum lodging by month and daily M&IE for the 32 GSA areas in California, plus the standard rate of $113 + $68 everywhere else. FY 2027 and FY 2026.

32Areas with their own rate in FY 2027[1]
$113 + $68Standard rate elsewhere in California: lodging + M&IE
$325Highest lodging rate: San Francisco
$68 to $92M&IE per day in California

FY 2027: October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027. FY 2026 table below for earlier trips. GSA files read on November 13, 2026.

Places in California not listed below get the standard CONUS rate. GSA: “If the city is not listed, but the county is, then the per diem rate is the rate for that entire county.”[2] Lodging is a maximum per night (taxes are not included); M&IE is per day, 75 percent on the first and last day of travel.[2]

These are the federal rates GSA sets for official travel by federal employees.[2] Travel rules for California state government employees are set by the state and are not covered here.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the per diem rate in California?

In FY 2027 (October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027) GSA lists 32 areas in California with their own rate: maximum lodging from $129 to $325 a night depending on place and month, and M&IE of $74 to $92 a day. Everywhere else in California the standard rate applies: $113 for lodging and $68 for M&IE. These are federal travel rates, not California’s own rates for state employees.[1],[2]
