Virginia Per Diem Rates FY 2027

Maximum lodging by month and daily M&IE for the 9 GSA areas in Virginia and the part of the state in the Washington, DC area, plus the standard rate of $113 + $68 everywhere else. FY 2027 and FY 2026.

9Areas with their own rate in FY 2027, plus part of the Washington, DC area[1]
$113 + $68Standard rate elsewhere in Virginia: lodging + M&IE
$295Highest lodging rate: Washington, DC area
$68 to $92M&IE per day in Virginia

FY 2027: October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027. FY 2026 table below for earlier trips. GSA files read on November 13, 2026.

Places in Virginia not listed below get the standard CONUS rate. GSA: “If the city is not listed, but the county is, then the per diem rate is the rate for that entire county.”[2] Lodging is a maximum per night (taxes are not included); M&IE is per day, 75 percent on the first and last day of travel.[2]

Washington, DC area: GSA’s District of Columbia area also takes in the cities of Alexandria, Falls Church and Fairfax and the counties of Arlington and Fairfax in Virginia, and the counties of Montgomery and Prince George’s in Maryland.[1] In Virginia, the cities of Alexandria, Falls Church and Fairfax and the counties of Arlington and Fairfax get the Washington, DC rate ($192 to $295 a night for lodging depending on the month and $92 for M&IE in FY 2027), not the standard rate. It is the last row of each table below.

These are the federal rates GSA sets for official travel by federal employees.[2] Travel rules for Virginia state government employees are set by the state and are not covered here.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the per diem rate in Virginia?

In FY 2027 (October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027) GSA lists 9 areas in Virginia with their own rate: maximum lodging from $113 to $262 a night depending on place and month, and M&IE of $68 to $80 a day. The cities of Alexandria, Falls Church and Fairfax and the counties of Arlington and Fairfax are part of GSA’s Washington, DC area: lodging from $192 to $295 a night and M&IE of $92 a day. Everywhere else in Virginia the standard rate applies: $113 for lodging and $68 for M&IE. These are federal travel rates, not Virginia’s own rates for state employees.[1],[2]
