When the next W-3 raise comes

Two things raise basic pay. The first is time in service: you move to the next column of the table when you pass the number of years it names. In the W-3 row the rate goes up at:

  • Over 2 years: $5,440.50 a month (+$217.20)
  • Over 3 years: $5,664.30 a month (+$223.80)
  • Over 4 years: $5,736.90 a month (+$72.60)
  • Over 6 years: $5,970.90 a month (+$234.00)
  • Over 8 years: $6,431.10 a month (+$460.20)
  • Over 10 years: $6,910.50 a month (+$479.40)
  • Over 12 years: $7,136.40 a month (+$225.90)
  • Over 14 years: $7,397.70 a month (+$261.30)
  • Over 16 years: $7,665.90 a month (+$268.20)
  • Over 18 years: $8,150.40 a month (+$484.50)
  • Over 20 years: $8,476.50 a month (+$326.10)
  • Over 22 years: $8,671.80 a month (+$195.30)
  • Over 24 years: $8,879.70 a month (+$207.90)
  • Over 26 years: $9,162.60 a month (+$282.90)

After over 26 years the W-3 rate does not rise with more service in the 2026 table.

The second is the annual adjustment: by law the rates of basic pay rise on January 1 of each year.[2] In 2026 the table rose 3.8% over 2025 in the rows we compared.[1],[3] See the status of the 2027 raise and the raise history.
