About Military Pay Chart

What this site is, what it publishes and how it is paid for.

Military Pay Chart is an independent publication, part of a small network of websites that turn official data into plain-language reference pages. It shows the U.S. military basic pay tables in a form that is easy to read on a phone, with one page per pay grade.

We are not affiliated with the Department of Defense, any military branch, the Coast Guard, DFAS or any recruiter. We do not encourage anyone to enlist or advise on careers or money. The site gives general information, not financial advice.

What we publish

  • Only figures printed in the official tables of the Federal Register, each linked to its source.
  • Arithmetic on those figures (yearly amounts, differences, the next step), always labelled as ours.
  • Where an official source cannot be read, or a figure is not final, we say so instead of filling the gap.

How the site is paid for

The site shows advertising served by Google AdSense. Advertisers never decide what we publish.

Contact

Use the contact and corrections form. For questions about your own pay, ask your unit’s finance office: they have your records.

Military Pay Chart

Military Pay Chart publishes the 2026 monthly basic pay tables of the U.S. uniformed services, copied from Schedule 8 of Executive Order 14368 in the Federal Register, with a page for every pay grade. Independent site, not a government website.
