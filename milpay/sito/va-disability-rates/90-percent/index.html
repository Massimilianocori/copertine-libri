Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

How much is 90% VA disability in 2026?

$2,362.30 a month for a Veteran with no dependents, or $28,347.60 a year, at the rates effective December 1, 2025. With a spouse it is $2,559.30 a month, and with a spouse and one child $2,704.30.[1]

How much does a spouse add at 90%?

$197 a month: $2,559.30 with a spouse against $2,362.30 alone. If the spouse receives Aid and Attendance, VA adds $181 more.[1]
