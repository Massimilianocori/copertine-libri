2027 VA Disability Rates: What Is Official So Far

The 3.6% cost-of-living increase, the bill in Congress and VA’s rate table are different things. Here is what each official source says, with its date.

Status as of November 15, 2026. Nothing on this page is a forecast: each line is what an official source says on that date.

What is official so far

The percentage: 3.6%. The Social Security Administration’s notice in the Federal Register of October 26, 2026 says “there will be a 3.6 percent cost-of-living increase in Social Security benefits effective December 2026”.[1]

VA follows Social Security. VA writes on its rate page: “We’re required by law to match the percentage of cost-of-living adjustments made to Social Security benefits.” “These adjustments help to make sure that the purchasing power of your benefits keeps up with inflation.”[4]

The law for this year. Each year’s increase comes from an act of Congress. For the rates in force now it was Public Law 119-42, approved on November 25, 2025.[6] For the next ones the Government Publishing Office published on November 13, 2026 the enrolled bill H.R. 8552, the Veterans’ Compensation Cost-of-Living Adjustment Act of 2026: an enrolled bill is the final text as passed by both the House and the Senate. It says: “Each dollar amount described in subsection (b) shall be increased by the same percentage as the percentage by which benefit amounts payable under title II of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 401 et seq.) are increased effective December 1, 2026”.[2] On November 15, 2026 the list of published public laws on govinfo.gov ran to Public Law 119-138 (November 3, 2026) and did not include it yet.[3]

The amounts: not published. On November 15, 2026 VA’s rate page, last updated November 2, 2026, still showed the tables effective December 1, 2025.[4] The bill also says: “The Secretary of Veterans Affairs shall publish in the Federal Register the amounts specified in section 2(b), as increased under that section”.[2] On November 15, 2026 the newest such VA notice was still the one of February 17, 2026, with the amounts effective December 1, 2025; the two before it are dated July 18, 2025 and January 8, 2024.[5],[7]

Why there is no 2027 table here yet

We do not publish a “2027 VA pay chart” worked out from the percentage. The law sets the percentage and one rounding rule, for dependents at partial ratings: “The amounts payable under this paragraph, if not a multiple of $1, shall be rounded down to the nearest dollar.”[8] No official source we could read says how each increased amount is rounded to the cent, and when we applied the announced percentage to VA’s published amounts for the last two years, the result did not match VA’s own figures in every case.[7],[9] A table built that way could be a cent off, and it would not be VA’s table.

Until VA publishes the new amounts, the official rates are the 2026 rates, effective December 1, 2025. This page and the rate tables are updated when VA’s table changes.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the VA COLA increase for 2027?

3.6% is the Social Security cost-of-living increase effective December 2026, and VA says it is required by law to match that percentage. As of November 15, 2026 VA had not published the new rate tables.[1],[4]

When does the VA disability increase take effect?

The bill passed by Congress, H.R. 8552, sets the increase to take effect on December 1, 2026. On November 15, 2026 it was not yet in the list of published public laws.[2],[3]

Will VA disability pay go up in 2027?

The bill passed by Congress raises the rates from December 1, 2026 by the same percentage as Social Security benefits, which is 3.6%. VA's table with the new amounts had not been published as of November 15, 2026.[1],[2],[4]

What are the VA disability rates right now?

The rates effective December 1, 2025: $180.42 a month at 10% up to $3,938.58 at 100% for a Veteran with no dependents.[4]
