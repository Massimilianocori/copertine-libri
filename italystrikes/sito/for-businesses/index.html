Italy strike data for travel businesses

For tour operators, travel agencies, hotels, relocation services and travel apps: every Italian transport strike from the official register, in English, as a calendar or as data, updated twice a day.

What you can use today, free

  • Calendars (iCal) for all of Italy, trains, flights, local transport and 15 cities: see the strikes calendar. Add them to a shared team calendar.
  • JSON data with every strike of the last weeks and the months ahead, in English and Italian, with sectors, cities and airports already worked out: vista.json.
  • Pages to link for your customers: today, tomorrow, each city and airport.

The data comes from the strike register of the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Strikes and cancellations marked with the Commissione di garanzia sciopero (via onData, CC BY 4.0) come from its strike calendar: credit it too. If you reuse it, credit the Ministry's register and link to Italy Strikes Today.
