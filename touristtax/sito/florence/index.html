Florence tourist tax 2026: how much per night (calculator)

Imposta di soggiorno (Comune di Firenze). The calculator below uses the official rates and rules: a fixed amount per person per night by accommodation type, maximum taxable nights, exemption under 12.

Florence 2026: €3.50–€8.00 per person per night

Official source: Comune di Firenze - Giunta resolution 535 of 10/12/2024: tariffs valid from 1 February 2025 · checked on 10 October 2026

Formula used: Tax = rate per person per night × guests aged 12 or over × taxable nights (maximum 7 taxable nights per stay).

Example: 2 adults, 3 nights from 10 October 2026, Hotel or RTA 5 stars → €48.00 in total.

Not tax advice. Tourist taxes are set by local or national authorities and can change. They are usually collected by your hotel or host, who applies the official rules: check the final amount with your accommodation.

Practical notes

  • Paid per person per night by non-residents, for a maximum of 7 consecutive nights.
  • Children are exempt until their 12th birthday.
  • People staying for documented work reasons pay for at most 7 nights in a calendar year.

Who collects it: The property manager, who must give a receipt.

Exemptions (official)

  • Children up to their 12th birthday
  • Up to 2 people assisting a hospitalised patient
  • Day-hospital patients
  • Students enrolled at the University of Florence
  • People with severe disability (Law 104/92 art. 3(3)) and one companion
  • Police, fire brigade and civil protection on duty
