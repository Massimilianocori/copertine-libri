Hakuba tourist tax 2026: how much per night (calculator)

Hakuba Village accommodation tax + Nagano Prefecture accommodation tax (宿泊税). The calculator below uses the official rates and rules: rate by accommodation type.

Hakuba 2026: ¥200–¥1,900 per person per night (by price band)

Official source: Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications - consent to the new Hakuba Village accommodation tax (bands; reduced for the first 3 years; in force 1 June 2026) · checked on 10 October 2026

Formula used: Tax = band amount (from the price per person per night = room price ÷ guests) × guests × nights.

Example: 2 guests, room price ¥40,000 per night, 3 nights from 10 October 2026, Hotels, ryokan, simple lodging (pensions, lodges) and minpaku in Hakuba Village → ¥2,400 in total.

Not tax advice. Tourist taxes are set by local or national authorities and can change. They are usually collected by your hotel or host, who applies the official rules: check the final amount with your accommodation.

Practical notes

  • Since 1 June 2026 guests in Hakuba pay a village tax and a prefectural tax, per person per night, based on the accommodation charge per person per night.
  • Nagano Prefecture charges half its normal rate in Hakuba (because the village has its own tax): ¥100 for the first three years, then ¥150.
  • No tax below ¥6,000 per person per night. Totals shown are village + prefecture.
  • Nozawa Onsen also introduced its own tax on 1 June 2026 (a percentage): not covered on this page.

Who collects it: The accommodation (special collection).

Exemptions (official)

  • Stays below ¥6,000 per person per night
  • Educational and research stays of kindergarten to university students (and staff)
  • Nursery and certified centre events
