Rhodes tourist tax 2026: €0.50–€15 per room per night

In Rhodes you pay €0.50 to €15 per room per night (not per person), depending on the type of accommodation and the season: April-October €2–€15; November-March €0.50–€4.

Formula used

Tax = rate per room (or unit) per night × rooms × nights.

Example: 1 room, 3 nights from 26 October 2026, Hotel 1-2 stars → €6.00 in total.

Not tax advice. Tourist taxes are set by local or national authorities and can change. They are usually collected by your hotel or host, who applies the official rules: check the final amount with your accommodation.

Practical notes

  • The fee is charged per room or apartment per night, not per person: two people sharing a room pay one fee.
  • April to October is the high-season rate; November to March the low-season rate. For stays across two months with different rates, the receipt is issued per month.
  • The guest pays it at the property after the stay and before departure, and receives a separate receipt. It is not subject to VAT.
  • The same national rate applies everywhere in Greece. Cruise passengers pay a separate cruise fee (not covered here).

Who collects it: The hotel, rental host or property manager, at the end of the stay.

Exemptions (official)

  • Accommodation provided free of charge (the fee is not imposed)
