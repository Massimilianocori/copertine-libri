Rome tourist tax 2026: how much per night (calculator)

Contributo di soggiorno (Roma Capitale). The calculator below uses the official rates and rules: rate by accommodation type, maximum taxable nights, age exemption.

Rome 2026: €3.00–€10.00 per person per night

Official source: Roma Capitale - Contributo di soggiorno tariffs from 1 October 2023 (Giunta resolution 255/2023), with maximum nights · checked on 10 October 2026

Formula used: Tax = rate per person per night × guests aged 10 or over × taxable nights (maximum 10 taxable nights per stay; some categories have their own maximum (see the table)).

Example: 2 adults, 3 nights from 10 October 2026, Hotel 5 stars or higher → €60.00 in total.

Not tax advice. Tourist taxes are set by local or national authorities and can change. They are usually collected by your hotel or host, who applies the official rules: check the final amount with your accommodation.

Practical notes

  • Paid per person per night by non-residents, up to 10 consecutive nights in the same property (5 for campsites).
  • Children are exempt until their 10th birthday.

Who collects it: The hotel, B&B or rental host (and booking platforms for short-term rentals).

Exemptions (official)

  • Children up to their 10th birthday
  • Guests of youth hostels (ostelli della gioventù)
  • People needing medical care and one companion per hospitalised patient (signed declaration)
  • Coach drivers and tour leaders of organised groups (one per 25 participants)
  • Police and armed forces on public-order duty
  • Other cases listed in art. 5 of the regulation
