Florida hotel tax 2026: every county, with a calculator

Florida charges its 6% state sales tax on hotel rooms and other rentals of six months or less, plus the county discretionary sales surtax (0% to 2% in 2026) and, in 62 of the 67 counties, a local option transient rental tax (which includes the tourist development tax) of 2% to 7%: 7% to 14% of the room price in total, according to the Florida Department of Revenue (forms DR-15TDT, revision 10/26, and DR-15DSS for 2026, checked on 7 November 2026).

Official sources: Form DR-15TDT, Local Option Transient Rental Tax Rates (Tourist Development Tax Rates) (Florida Department of Revenue) · Form DR-15DSS, Discretionary Sales Surtax Information for Calendar Year 2026 (Florida Department of Revenue) · checked on 7 November 2026

Formula used

Tax = room price per night × nights × the sum of the percentages that apply. Each percentage counts only on the nights it is in force (Florida county surtaxes have start and end dates) and, in Texas, only when the price is at least $15 a night for the state tax and $2 a night for local taxes. Hotels apply the taxes to the room charge and may round each tax: check the final amount on your bill.

Not tax advice. This page adds up the rates in official state files. Your hotel or host applies the taxes and may round them; some cities and districts charge taxes of their own that a state file does not show. Check the final amount on your booking.

How the Florida hotel tax adds up

  • Individual Florida counties and certain cities may impose a local option tax on the rental or lease of living, sleeping, or housekeeping accommodations for a term of six months or less. The local tax imposed is in addition to the 6% state sales tax and any applicable discretionary sales surtax.
  • The discretionary sales surtax applies when the rental of living or sleeping accommodations (transient rentals) occurs in a county with a surtax; the $5,000 cap on the surtax does not apply to transient rentals (Discretionary Sales Surtax page).
  • For the shaded counties in the chart below, the local option transient rental taxes are reported and remitted to the Florida Department of Revenue (DOR). For all other counties, the local option transient rental taxes are reported and remitted directly to the county; however, sales tax and discretionary sales surtax on transient rentals are always reported and remitted to the Department.
  • Cities: in Miami-Dade County the form gives 7% for Miami Beach and 4% for Surfside and Bal Harbour; currently, only three municipalities in Miami-Dade County (Bal Harbour, Miami Beach, and Surfside) are eligible to impose this tax. (municipal resort tax, Local Option Taxes page).
  • Not all counties notify the Department of changes in their local option transient rental tax rate. If the local option transient rental tax is collected by the county, contact that county to verify the tax rate.

Surtax changes

According to the 2026 form, these surtaxes end on 31 December 2026: Brevard County: the whole 1%; Charlotte County: the whole 1%; Liberty County: 0.5% (of 1.5%); Santa Rosa County: 0.5% (of 1%). The Department publishes the form for the next year every November; we update the rates when it does.

Questions

Does Florida have a tourist tax?

Yes, county by county: 62 of the 67 counties charge a local option transient rental tax, often called the tourist development tax, of 2% to 7% on rentals of six months or less. Liberty County, Calhoun County, Sumter County, Union County and Lafayette County have none (0%) on form DR-15TDT. It comes on top of the 6% state sales tax and the county surtax.

What is the Florida hotel tax rate?

7% to 14% of the room price in 2026, depending on the county: 6% state sales tax, the county surtax (0% to 2%) and the tourist development tax. In Orange County (Orlando) it is 12.5%.

Do vacation rentals pay the Florida tourist tax?

Yes for rentals of six months or less: the local option tax applies to the rental or lease of living, sleeping, or housekeeping accommodations for a term of six months or less (form DR-15TDT), and the Department of Revenue lists beach houses, condominiums, timeshare resorts and vacation houses among the short-term rentals that must register to collect sales tax.

What is the tourist development tax in each Florida county?

The table on this page lists all 67 counties from form DR-15TDT (revision 10/26): 2% to 7%, with special rates by area in Bay, Miami-Dade, Nassau, Okaloosa and Walton counties. In counties marked “County” the county collects it and the Department advises contacting the county to verify the rate.

How do I calculate Florida hotel tax?

Add up the three rates for the county and apply them to the room price: in Orange County $200.00 a night for 3 nights at 12.5% is $75.00 in tax. The calculator does it for any county and date.
