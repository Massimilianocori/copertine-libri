Fouesnant tourist tax 2026: how much per night (calculator)

Fouesnant 2026: €0.22–€5.06 per person per night; unclassified: 5% of the price until 31 December 2026

  • Per person per night
  • Under 18s exempt
  • Finistère, France

Taxe de séjour in Fouesnant (Finistère), 2026: the official municipal rates plus the additional departmental and regional taxes, from the French tax administration's open data.

Official source: DGFiP open dataset "Tarifs taxe de séjour – DELTA" (Licence Ouverte 2.0), 2026 rates for FOUESNANT (INSEE code 29058), dataset updated 29 June 2026, checked on 10 October 2026

Formula used

Classified accommodation: tax = (municipal rate + additional taxes) × guests aged 18 or over × nights. Unclassified: rate % × (price per night excluding taxes ÷ all occupants), capped at the highest rate adopted by the town, + additional taxes, × guests aged 18 or over × nights.

Example: 2 adults, 3 nights from 26 October 2026, Hotel 3 stars → €6.60 in total.

Not tax advice. Tourist taxes are set by local or national authorities and can change. They are usually collected by your hotel or host, who applies the official rules: check the final amount with your accommodation.

Practical notes

  • The tax is charged per person and per night by the accommodation, on top of the room price.
  • Under-18s are exempt by law (art. L. 2333-31 of the General Code of Local Authorities), as are seasonal workers employed in the town and people in emergency or temporary housing.
  • For unclassified accommodation (most Airbnb-type rentals) the tax is a percentage of the price per person per night excluding taxes, capped at the highest rate adopted by the town (€4.60 before additional taxes).
  • Additional taxes are set by the department and, in some regions, by the region or transport authority: they are already included in the totals.

How the cap and the per-person cost work is explained in the official guide of the French Directorate General for Local Authorities (guide pratique, fiches 4 and 6, June 2026).
