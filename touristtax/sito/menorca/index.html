Menorca tourist tax 2026: how much per night (calculator)

Tourist stay tax (impuesto sobre estancias turísticas, 'ecotasa'). The calculator below uses the official rates and rules: rate by accommodation type, season, age exemption.

Menorca 2026: €1.00–€4.00 per person per night

Official source: Law 2/2016 of 30 March on the tourist stay tax in the Balearic Islands - consolidated text, Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE-A-2016-4175) · checked on 10 October 2026

Formula used: Tax = rate per person per night × guests aged 16 or over × taxable nights (low season (1 Nov - 30 Apr): -75%; 9th day onwards: -50%).

Example: 2 adults, 3 nights from 10 October 2026, Hotel 5 stars, 5 stars grand luxury or 4 stars superior (incl. city hotels and hotel-apartments) → €24.00 in total.

Not tax advice. Tourist taxes are set by local or national authorities and can change. They are usually collected by your hotel or host, who applies the official rules: check the final amount with your accommodation.

Practical notes

  • Low season (1 November to 30 April): the tax is reduced by 75%.
  • From the 9th day of a stay in the same establishment: a further 50% reduction (on top of the low-season reduction, if any).
  • Under-16s do not pay. Stays for reasons of force majeure are also exempt.
  • The tax is charged per person and per day (or fraction) of stay, and collected by the establishment.
  • The same Balearic rate applies everywhere on Menorca.

Who collects it: The hotel or rental operator, usually at check-out.

Exemptions (official)

  • Children under 16
  • Stays due to force majeure
