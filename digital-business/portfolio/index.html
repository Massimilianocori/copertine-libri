FAQ

Questions, answered.


Does AI UGC actually convert?

We're a new studio — judge it on a three-video pilot made for your own product, not a promise. We focus on feed-native, hook-first creative and give you the volume to test — you keep what performs. We sell creative and testing volume, not guaranteed ROAS.

Why not just use an AI video tool myself?

Self-serve tools charge less per clip, but you do all the work: writing prompts, picking an AI avatar, sitting through bad generations, then editing it together yourself. With Scrollcraft you send a product link and get back a finished, ready-to-post ad — no software to learn, no wasted credits on takes that don't work, no editing on your end.

What do you need from me?

A product URL and a few brand notes. Brand photos or guidelines help but aren't required to start.

Which platforms and formats?

Whatever you're running. Tell us the placement and we produce in that ratio from the start — 9:16 for Reels, Stories and TikTok, 4:5 for the Meta feed, 1:1 for square placements, 16:9 for YouTube and site embeds. Nothing is cropped down from another format, so the framing is right for the placement it's made for.

What is the Holiday Ad Pack?

Our Q4 offer: 10 finished videos plus 10 hook variants for $1,490. Order by October 24 and we deliver by November 10, in time for Black Friday and the holidays.

Can I test before committing?

Yes. Start with a three-video pilot for $450, delivered in 5 business days. Or a one-off ad to your brief is $179, and the Starter batch is a one-time $845 for 5 videos plus 5 hook variants. None of these is a subscription.

What is a hook variant?

The same video with a different opening line — the first three seconds re-shot. Hooks decide whether an ad works, so you need more than one to test. Every plan includes them free; on their own they're $49 each.

Do I own the ads?

Yes. Full usage rights are included on every plan, at no extra cost — no per-month licensing, no whitelisting fees.

How fast do I get them?

Most batches ship within a few days. Growth gets priority queue, Scale can request 24h rush.
