Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Scrollcraft (www.scrollcraft.design) is a one-person service producing AI-made UGC-style video ads for DTC brands, run by Massimiliano. For anything on this page, email hello@scrollcraft.design.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the brand name, product URL, your email, the format you need, and any notes you add. This is sent via Formspree and delivered to our inbox — we use it only to prepare your free sample or reply to your request. We don't sell or share it with anyone else.

If you place an order, payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details — Stripe's own privacy policy covers that data.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The only external services it talks to are Formspree (contact form) and Stripe (checkout), both only when you actively submit a form or click to pay.

What you get when you order

Every paid order comes with full usage rights to the video(s) you receive — you can run them on any ad platform, with no extra licensing or whitelisting fees. The free sample is a watermarked preview only, meant to show quality before you spend anything; it isn't a file you're licensed to publish.

Cancellations & refunds

One-off orders (Single ad, Starter batch) are made to your brief once you confirm the details — if we haven't started production yet, we'll refund you in full; once we've started, we'll work with you on revisions before considering a refund, since the free sample already let you judge quality upfront at no risk.

Monthly plans (Growth, Scale) can be cancelled anytime — you keep whatever's already been delivered, and you won't be charged again after cancelling.

Changes

We may update this page as the service evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date above.
