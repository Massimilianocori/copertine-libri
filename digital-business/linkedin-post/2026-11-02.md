The most expensive word in AI video is "whatever".

Whatever lighting looks good. Whatever location fits. Whatever the model decides the model should wear.

Every element you don't decide, the AI decides for you. And it decides the average: the most common kitchen, the most common smile, the most common golden-hour light. That's why so much AI content looks like the same ad.

So the rule we work by: nothing goes into a generation that hasn't been chosen on purpose first. Product, casting, styling, location, light, camera moves, sound. Each one has a reference and a reason before anything is rendered.

It's slower at the start. It's much faster than redoing a video because nobody can say why it feels generic.

For the creative directors here: which element do you see left to chance most often?
