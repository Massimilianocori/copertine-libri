The AI changed our model's face between two shots.

Same prompt. Same reference. Same outfit. Shot one: her. Shot two: someone who looked like her cousin.

Nobody watching a 15-second ad will say "that's a different face." They'll just feel something is off, and scroll.

So we wrote a rule. Before any shot becomes video, every starting frame goes side by side with the approved casting photo. Face, hair, nails, jewelry, product label. If one detail doesn't match, the frame is thrown out and redone. After the video, we check it frame by frame again.

It's slow. It's also the difference between "AI ad" and "ad."

For the agencies here: what's the one detail that makes you reject AI creative on sight?
