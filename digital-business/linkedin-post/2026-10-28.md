A brief that says "make it pop" is not a brief.

In 20 years of advertising, the best work I saw always started with one sentence that a stranger could understand: who is this for, what do they believe now, what should they believe after.

AI hasn't changed that. It has made it more urgent. When a tool can produce a hundred versions in an afternoon, a vague brief doesn't give you a hundred ideas. It gives you the same idea a hundred times.

So before anything is generated, we ask for that one sentence. If nobody can write it, the problem isn't production.

For the agencies here: what's the one line you wish every client brief included?
