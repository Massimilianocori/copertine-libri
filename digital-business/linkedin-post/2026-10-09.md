AI can generate any image you ask for.
It can't tell you which one to ask for.

After 20 years in advertising, the hard part was never making the picture. It was deciding what the ad should say in the first three seconds, to one specific person, about one specific problem.

That part hasn't changed. If anything, it matters more now: when every brand can produce 50 variations by lunch, the ones that win are the ones built on a real idea, not on more output.

So before we generate a single frame, we write one sentence: who is this for, and what should they feel? If we can't write it, we don't generate.

For the media buyers here: what's the first thing you check when a new batch of creative lands on your desk?
