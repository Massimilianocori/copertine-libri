"Can you make it look less like AI?" is the wrong brief.

Viewers don't scroll past an ad because it was made with AI. They scroll past because it looks like every other ad: the same smile, the same kitchen, the same slow push-in.

AI didn't create that problem. Stock footage had it. Templates had it. AI just makes it faster to produce more of the same.

The fix isn't hiding the tool. It's the same thing it always was in advertising: a specific person, a specific moment, a specific reason to care. Decided by someone, not left to defaults.

For the marketers here: what's the last ad that made you stop scrolling, and why?
