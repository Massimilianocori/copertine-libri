One script, three hooks. That's the cheapest test in performance video.

Most teams test whole new concepts when an ad stops working. Usually the body of the ad is fine. It's the first two seconds that stopped earning attention.

A simple way to set it up:
- Keep the same middle and the same ending.
- Write three different openings: the problem, the result, the surprising detail.
- Run them side by side and let the hold rate decide.

AI makes this cheaper than it used to be: changing an opening no longer means a new shoot. The thinking behind each hook still has to come from someone who understands the audience.

For the media buyers here: when an ad fatigues, do you change the hook first or the whole concept?
