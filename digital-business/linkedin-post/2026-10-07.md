We paid 84 credits to learn this.

We generated a 12-second AI creator video. Clean face, right product, steady light. Then we played it.

You could hear her voice. Her lips never moved.

The model had read our script as a voice-over, because that's what we had written: a block of dialogue after the shot list. The video looked finished and was unusable.

The rule we now follow on every talking clip:
1. Each line goes inside the shot where it's spoken ("he says: '…', lips in sync").
2. Lines only go in shots where the face is visible.
3. Close-ups of hands and product stay silent.
4. Before anything ships, we watch it frame by frame with the sound on.

The video below is what it looks like when the rule is followed: one take, every line spoken on camera.

AI makes a convincing video in minutes. It doesn't tell you when it's wrong. That check is still a human job.
