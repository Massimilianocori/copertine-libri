Ten versions of the same ad are not ten ads.

Change the background, swap the music, recut the first second: to the viewer it's the same message. To the platform, increasingly, it's the same signal.

A genuinely different angle starts earlier, at the idea:
- a different person speaking (the skeptic, not the fan)
- a different problem (the morning routine, not the ingredient list)
- a different first frame (the result, not the product)

Three real angles will teach you more than thirty cosmetic variations. And they are cheaper to make than most teams think.

For the media buyers here: how many truly different concepts are you testing per product right now?
