The first three seconds of an ad are not an intro. They are the ad.

Most of the creative I've reviewed in 20 years spent those seconds on a logo, a slow push-in, or someone saying "Hey guys." By the time the real point arrived, the thumb had already moved.

The rule we use now is simple: whatever would make someone stop, put it in frame one. The result, the problem, the surprising detail. Not the setup.

AI made it cheap to produce more videos. It didn't make the first three seconds any less important. If anything, more output means the opening has to work harder.

For the media buyers here: what's the strongest opening frame you've seen this month?
