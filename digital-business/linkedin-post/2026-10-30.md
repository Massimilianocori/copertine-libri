Sound off is the default. Most ads still aren't built for it.

A large share of short-form video is watched without audio, on a bus, in an office, in bed next to someone sleeping. Yet many ads still put the whole message in the voiceover.

Three checks before an ad goes live:
- Mute it. Does the first second still say what it's about?
- Read only the on-screen text. Is there a reason to keep watching?
- Pause on any frame. Is the product recognisable?

If an ad only works with sound, it only works for part of the audience.

For the media buyers here: do you still review new creative with the sound on first?
