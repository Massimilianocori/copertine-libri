In AI video, the product label is where things quietly go wrong.

AI video is very good at making a product look right at a glance. It is much worse at keeping the small print right: a letter swaps, a logo drifts, the cap changes color between shots.

For a brand, that's not a detail. It's their product, on screen, saying something it doesn't say.

So every frame with a product now goes through one check before it moves on: the real packaging photo next to the frame, label by label. If one letter is off, the frame is redone.

The viewer may never consciously see it. The brand manager always will.

For the brand side here: what's the one product detail you'd never let an agency get wrong?
