## 5. Idee video da proporre a voce (Dress Collar Polo, 3 angoli diversi)

Regole: niente promesse di risultati; nessuna testimonianza inventata ("I love this shirt" detto da un attore AI come se fosse un cliente: vietato, FTC e doc 37); il capo si ricostruisce **solo dalle sue foto prodotto ufficiali** (ERRORI 4-5); ogni dettaglio è da ricerca e brief approvato prima di generare (CLAUDE.md). Queste sono idee da discutere, non un brief.

1. **"The Sweater Test" (angolo prodotto: il colletto che non cede)** · 15 s, 9:16.
   Un uomo in riunione, maglione girocollo, colletto perfetto. Si toglie il maglione: è una polo a manica corta. Dettaglio macro del colletto che resta in piedi. Testo finale: "Dress shirt on top. Polo underneath."
   A voce: "It's your own hack, just shot like a campaign instead of a phone video."
2. **"One Shirt, 7 to 11" (angolo versatilità: una camicia, tutta la giornata)** · 15-20 s.
   Stesso uomo, stessa polo, quattro scene veloci: ufficio, golf, aperitivo, cena. Il nostro punto forte: **stesso volto, stessa camicia, stessa luce in ogni scena**.
   A voce: "Four locations, zero shoot days."
3. **"The gift he'll actually wear" (angolo regalo per le feste)** · 15 s, per Q4.
   Cena di famiglia, luce calda. Lui apre il pacco, la indossa subito sotto il blazer. Taglio finale sul colletto. Adatto all'Holiday Pack (più tagli e hook: "Stop buying him ties.", "The shirt he'll wear to every holiday party.").
   A voce: "This one is for whoever buys for him, a different audience from your usual ads."

Per un pilota da 3 video: un angolo a testa. Così sono tre concetti diversi, non tre varianti (doc 38 §1, Meta Andromeda).

## 6. Obiezioni probabili e risposte (inglese)

| Obiezione | Risposta |
|---|---|
| **"My audience likes real, founder-led content. AI feels fake."** | "Keep doing those, they clearly work. We don't replace you on camera. We make the campaign layer you'd never shoot from your phone: new locations, new models, seasonal concepts. Every video is labelled as AI, which Meta requires anyway for realistic AI people. The pilot is small so you can judge it on your own shirt." |
| **"AI can't get the shirt right. The collar is the whole product."** (al posto dei "cani realistici") | "That's the right worry, and it's exactly what we check. We work only from your official product photos. Every clip gets a frame-by-frame check: collar shape, buttons, fabric, hands, faces. If the collar isn't right, the clip doesn't reach you. That's why the storyboard is approved before anything is generated." |
| **"Will the people look real? Hands, faces changing between shots?"** | "Fair, a lot of AI ads have those problems. Same face, same styling in every shot is the first thing on our checklist. The spec spot I showed you was built that way." |
| **Prezzo: "$150 a video is a lot for AI" / "Arcads is $11"** | "If someone on your team runs those tools, that's the cheaper route. What you pay us for is direction, three genuinely different angles, the frame-by-frame check and finished files. What do you pay today per finished video, including your own time?" |
| **Prezzo: "Can you do the first one free?"** | "We don't do free work. That's how we keep quality and focus. The pilot is the small, fixed-price way to test us: $450 for three videos." |
| **Tempi: "I need it for Black Friday" / "5 days is slow"** | "Five business days after the approved brief is what I can promise with the checks in place. If there's a hard date, tell me now and I'll say honestly whether we can make it, before you pay anything." |
| **Diritti: "Who owns it? Can I use it everywhere, in stores too?"** | "On full payment you get a perpetual, worldwide licence to use, edit and publish the videos in your advertising, online and in store. Music comes with its licence. AI-generated material can't be guaranteed copyright-exclusive, and I'd rather tell you that now." |
| **"Who have you worked with?"** | "We're a new studio. What you see is spec work, not client jobs. I've spent 20 years in advertising, that's where the direction comes from. That's also why the first step is a small, paid pilot." |
| **"Is the Tom Ford spot a real Tom Ford job?"** | "No. It's a spec spot we made to show the craft. Not commissioned and not connected to the brand." |
| **"Not now."** | "Understood. Is there a launch or a season when it would matter, maybe a store opening? I'll write then, not before." |
