Rules that pause or extend deadlines in Ohio

Minors and people of unsound mind

If the person with the claim is a minor or of unsound mind when it accrues, the period starts after the disability ends.

is, at the time the cause of action accrues, within the age of minority or of unsound mind, the person may bring it within the respective times limited by those sections, after the disability is removed.

Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.16 · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[1]

Defendant out of state, absconding or hiding

Time while the person being sued is out of Ohio, has absconded or is concealed is not counted (this does not apply to statutes of repose).

After the cause of action accrues if the person departs from the state, absconds, or conceals self, the time of the person's absence or concealment shall not be computed as any part of a period within which the action must be brought.

Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.15(A) · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[2]

Saving statute: one year to refile

If a timely case fails otherwise than on the merits, a new action may be filed within one year or within the original period, whichever is later.

may commence a new action within one year after the date of the reversal of the judgment or the plaintiff's failure otherwise than upon the merits or within the period of the original applicable statute of limitations, whichever occurs later.

Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.19(A) · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[3]

Claims against a government in Ohio

Claims against cities, counties and other political subdivisions

An action against a political subdivision for injury, death or loss to person or property must be brought within two years, or any shorter period the Revised Code sets.

shall be brought within two years after the cause of action accrues, or within any applicable shorter period of time for bringing the action provided by the Revised Code.

Ohio Rev. Code § 2744.04(A) · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[4]

Recent changes in Ohio

Contracts: written 8 → 6 years, oral 6 → 4 years (Senate Bill 13)

Effective 16 June 2021

The version of § 2305.06 in force until June 16, 2021 gave eight years for written contracts; § 2305.07 gave six years for contracts not in writing. Senate Bill 13 (134th General Assembly) cut them to six and four years and added the six-year rule for consumer transactions. A transition rule in that bill covers claims that accrued before June 16, 2021 (quoted below on the Ohio pages): our calculator does not compute those dates.

an action upon a specialty or an agreement, contract, or promise in writing shall be brought within eight years after the cause of action accrued.

Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.06 (version of September 28, 2012) · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[5]

Claims that accrued before June 16, 2021: the transition rule

Effective 16 June 2021

For contract claims that already existed when Senate Bill 13 took effect (June 16, 2021, the effective date shown in the official code), the act sets the deadline at the earlier of two dates: the new period counted from the effective date, or the end of the period that applied before. Which earlier period applied depends on the kind of contract and on when the claim accrued; the calculator does not compute it.

For causes of action that are governed by section 2305.06 of the Revised Code and that accrued prior to the effective date of this act, the period of limitations shall be six years from the effective date of this act or the expiration of the period of limitations in effect prior to the effective date of this act, whichever occurs first.

For causes of action that are governed by division (A) of section 2305.07 of the Revised Code that accrued prior to the effective date of this act, the period of limitations shall be four years from the effective date of this act or the expiration of the period of limitations in effect prior to the effective date of this act, whichever occurs first.

For causes of action that are governed by division (C) of section 2305.07 of the Revised Code that accrued prior to the effective date of this act, the period of limitations shall be six years from the effective date of this act or the expiration of the period of limitations in effect prior to the effective date of this act, whichever occurs first.

Senate Bill 13 (134th General Assembly), Sections 4 and 5 · official text (Ohio General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[6]

§ 2305.06 amended again (Senate Bill 219)

Effective 23 September 2026

The official code shows § 2305.06 amended by Senate Bill 219 (136th General Assembly), effective September 23, 2026. The current text still says six years.

September 23, 2026 – Amended by Senate Bill 219 - 136th General Assembly

Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.06 · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[7]
