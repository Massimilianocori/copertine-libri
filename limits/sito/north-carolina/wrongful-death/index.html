What the North Carolina statute says

  • Wrongful death 2 years

    Within two years -

    (4) Actions for damages on account of the death of a person caused by the wrongful act, neglect or fault of another under G.S. 28A-18-2; the cause of action shall not accrue until the date of death.

    N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-53(4) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[1]

When the clock starts

The statute says the claim does not accrue until the date of death.

For example, counted from 15 January 2025, the general deadline would be Wrongful death: 15 January 2027. Exceptions below can move it.

Exceptions and special rules for wrongful death

No death claim if the injury claim was already barred

If the person would have been barred from suing for the injury by the medical malpractice or ten-year rules, no wrongful death action can be brought.

Provided that, whenever the decedent would have been barred, had he lived, from bringing an action for bodily harm because of the provisions of G.S. 1-15(c) or 1-52(16), no action for his death may be brought.

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-53(4) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[1]

Rules that pause or extend deadlines in North Carolina

These general rules apply to many North Carolina deadlines, including this one, subject to their own conditions.

Minors and people who are insane or incompetent

A person under 18, insane or incompetent when the claim accrues may sue within the period after the disability is removed.

(a) A person entitled to commence an action who is under a disability at the time the cause of action accrued may bring his or her action within the time limited in this Subchapter, after the disability is removed,

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-17(a) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[2]

If the claim is against a government

Claims against State agencies: Industrial Commission

Tort claims against State departments and agencies go to the Industrial Commission within three years (two years after a death).

All claims against any and all State departments, institutions, and agencies shall henceforth be forever barred unless a claim be filed with the Industrial Commission within three years after the accrual of such claim,

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143-299 · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[3]

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the statute text. General information, not legal advice.

What is the statute of limitations for wrongful death in North Carolina?

Two years under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-53(4), and the statute says the claim does not accrue until the date of death. If the person could no longer have sued for the injury when alive, no death action may be brought.[1]
