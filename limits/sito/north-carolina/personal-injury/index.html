North Carolina statute of limitations for personal injury

Generally 3 years under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-52(16). Statute text, when the clock starts, exceptions and a deadline calculator. Read on 13 November 2026.

What the North Carolina statute says

  • Personal injury 3 years

    Within three years an action -

    (16) Unless otherwise provided by law, for personal injury or physical damage to claimant's property, the cause of action, except in causes of actions referred to in G.S. 1-15(c), shall not accrue until bodily harm to the claimant or physical damage to his property becomes apparent or ought reasonably to have become apparent to the claimant, whichever event first occurs.

    N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-52(16) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[1]

When the clock starts

The claim does not accrue until bodily harm becomes apparent, or ought reasonably to have become apparent.

Outer limit: 10 years

Even when the regular period has not run, the statute sets a final cut-off counted from the act or omission.

no cause of action shall accrue more than 10 years from the last act or omission of the defendant giving rise to the cause of action.

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-52(16) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[1]

For example, counted from 15 January 2025, the general deadline would be Personal injury: 15 January 2028, with the outer limit on 15 January 2035. Exceptions below can move it.

Rules that pause or extend deadlines in North Carolina

These general rules apply to many North Carolina deadlines, including this one, subject to their own conditions.

Minors and people who are insane or incompetent

A person under 18, insane or incompetent when the claim accrues may sue within the period after the disability is removed.

(a) A person entitled to commence an action who is under a disability at the time the cause of action accrued may bring his or her action within the time limited in this Subchapter, after the disability is removed,

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-17(a) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[2]

If the claim is against a government

Claims against State agencies: Industrial Commission

Tort claims against State departments and agencies go to the Industrial Commission within three years (two years after a death).

All claims against any and all State departments, institutions, and agencies shall henceforth be forever barred unless a claim be filed with the Industrial Commission within three years after the accrual of such claim,

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143-299 · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[3]
