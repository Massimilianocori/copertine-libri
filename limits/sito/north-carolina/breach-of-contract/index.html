North Carolina · Breach of contract

North Carolina statute of limitations for breach of contract

Generally 3 or 10 years under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-52(1) and related sections. Statute text, when the clock starts, exceptions and a deadline calculator. Read on 13 November 2026.

What the North Carolina statute says

North Carolina sets more than one period for breach of contract claims, depending on the kind of claim:

  • Written or oral contract 3 years

    Within three years an action -

    (1) Upon a contract, obligation or liability arising out of a contract, express or implied, except those mentioned in the preceding sections or in G.S. 1-53(1).

    N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-52(1) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[1]

  • Sealed instrument 10 years

    Within ten years an action -

    (2) Upon a sealed instrument or an instrument of conveyance of an interest in real property, against the principal thereto.

    N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-47(2) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[2]

When the clock starts

North Carolina uses the same three years for written and oral contracts; a contract under seal has ten.

For example, counted from 15 January 2025, the general deadline would be Written or oral contract: 15 January 2028; Sealed instrument: 15 January 2035. Exceptions below can move it.

Exceptions and special rules for breach of contract

Contracts with local governments: two years

An action against a local unit of government on a contract must be brought within two years.

(1) An action against a local unit of government upon a contract, obligation or liability arising out of a contract, express or implied.

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-53(1) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[3]

Rules that pause or extend deadlines in North Carolina

These general rules apply to many North Carolina deadlines, including this one, subject to their own conditions.

Minors and people who are insane or incompetent

A person under 18, insane or incompetent when the claim accrues may sue within the period after the disability is removed.

(a) A person entitled to commence an action who is under a disability at the time the cause of action accrued may bring his or her action within the time limited in this Subchapter, after the disability is removed,

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-17(a) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[4]

If the claim is against a government

Claims against State agencies: Industrial Commission

Tort claims against State departments and agencies go to the Industrial Commission within three years (two years after a death).

All claims against any and all State departments, institutions, and agencies shall henceforth be forever barred unless a claim be filed with the Industrial Commission within three years after the accrual of such claim,

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143-299 · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[5]
