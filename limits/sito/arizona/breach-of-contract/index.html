Arizona · Breach of contract

Arizona statute of limitations for breach of contract

Generally 6, 3 or 4 years under Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-548(A)(1) and related sections. Statute text, when the clock starts, exceptions and a deadline calculator. Read on 13 November 2026.

What the Arizona statute says

Arizona sets more than one period for breach of contract claims, depending on the kind of claim:

  • Written contract signed in Arizona 6 years

    A. An action for debt shall be commenced and prosecuted within six years after the cause of action accrues, and not afterward, if the indebtedness is evidenced by or founded on either of the following:

    1. A contract in writing that is executed in this state.

    Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-548(A)(1) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

  • Oral agreement (debt not in writing) 3 years

    There shall be commenced and prosecuted within three years after the cause of action accrues, and not afterward, the following actions:

    1. For debt where the indebtedness is not evidenced by a contract in writing.

    Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-543(1) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[2]

  • Written instrument signed outside Arizona 4 years

    There shall be commenced and prosecuted within four years after the cause of action accrues, and not afterward, the following actions:

    3. Upon a judgment or decree of a court rendered without the state, or upon an instrument in writing executed without the state.

    Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-544(3) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[3]

Arizona's contract sections are written in terms of “debt”. Whether they cover a breach that is not a claim for money owed is a question for an Arizona attorney.

When the clock starts

From the day the claim accrues, usually the breach.

For example, counted from 15 January 2025, the general deadline would be Written contract signed in Arizona: 15 January 2031; Oral agreement (debt not in writing): 15 January 2028; Written instrument signed outside Arizona: 15 January 2029. Exceptions below can move it.

Exceptions and special rules for breach of contract

Sale of goods

A claim for breach of a contract of sale under Arizona's commercial code (title 47, chapter 2) follows the deadline in § 47-2725 instead of these sections.

4. An action arising under the provisions of title 47, chapter 2, for breach of any contract of sale, which action shall be governed by section 47-2725, notwithstanding any other provision of this section or of section 12-543 or 12-548.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-544(4) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[3]

Employment contracts: one year

Breach of an oral or written employment contract (including handbooks that set no time limit) has one year.

3. For breach of an oral or written employment contract including contract actions based on employee handbooks or policy manuals that do not specify a time period in which to bring an action.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-541(3) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[4]

Rules that pause or extend deadlines in Arizona

These general rules apply to many Arizona deadlines, including this one, subject to their own conditions.

Minors and people of unsound mind

Time while the person with the claim is under 18 or of unsound mind (at accrual) is not counted.

is at the time the cause of action accrues either under eighteen years of age or of unsound mind, the period of such disability shall not be deemed a portion of the period limited for commencement of the action.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-502 · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[5]

Defendant out of state

Time while the person being sued is outside Arizona is not counted.

The time of such person's absence shall not be counted or taken as a part of the time limited by the provisions of this chapter.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-501 · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[6]

Saving statute: six months

If a timely case ends other than on the merits (and not by abatement, voluntary dismissal or dismissal for lack of prosecution), a new action may be filed within six months.

may commence a new action for the same cause after the expiration of the time so limited and within six months after such termination.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-504(A) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[7]

If the claim is against a government

Claims against public entities: 180-day notice, one year to sue

A notice of claim must be filed within 180 days, and the lawsuit within one year.

Any claim that is not filed within one hundred eighty days after the cause of action accrues is barred and no action may be maintained thereon.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. §§ 12-821.01(A), 12-821 · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[8]
