Washington statute of limitations for personal injury

Generally 3 years under RCW 4.16.080(2). Statute text, when the clock starts, exceptions and a deadline calculator. Read on 13 November 2026.

What the Washington statute says

  • Injury to the person 3 years

    The following actions shall be commenced within three years:

    (2) An action for taking, detaining, or injuring personal property, including an action for the specific recovery thereof, or for any other injury to the person or rights of another not hereinafter enumerated;

    RCW 4.16.080(2) · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

When the clock starts

The section does not define when an injury claim accrues; the calculator counts from the date you enter.

For example, counted from 15 January 2025, the general deadline would be Injury to the person: 15 January 2028. Exceptions below can move it.

Exceptions and special rules for personal injury

Libel, slander, assault and battery: two years

Some intentional torts have a shorter two-year period.

(1) An action for libel, slander, assault, assault and battery, or false imprisonment.

RCW 4.16.100(1) · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

Rules that pause or extend deadlines in Washington

These general rules apply to many Washington deadlines, including this one, subject to their own conditions.

Minors, incompetence and imprisonment before sentencing

If the person with the claim is under 18, legally incompetent, or imprisoned on a criminal charge before sentencing when the claim accrues, that time is not counted.

the time of such disability shall not be a part of the time limited for the commencement of action.

RCW 4.16.190 · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

Defendant out of state or concealed

Time while the person being sued is out of the state or concealed is not counted.

the time of his or her absence or concealment shall not be deemed or taken as any part of the time limit for the commencement of such action.

RCW 4.16.180 · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

Filing versus service: 90 days

An action counts as commenced when the complaint is filed or the summons is served, but the other step must follow within 90 days or the filing does not stop the clock.

For the purpose of tolling any statute of limitations an action shall be deemed commenced when the complaint is filed or summons is served whichever occurs first.

RCW 4.16.170 · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

If the claim is against a government

Claims against local governments: 60-day waiting period

A tort claim against a local government must first be presented; suit cannot start until 60 days later, and the deadline is tolled during those 60 days.

The applicable period of limitations within which an action must be commenced shall be tolled during the sixty calendar day period.

RCW 4.96.020(4) · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[2]
