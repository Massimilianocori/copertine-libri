Breach of contract

Breach of contract statute of limitations by state

General periods in ten states range from three years (Arizona, North Carolina, Virginia, Washington) to ten years (North Carolina). Statute text and exceptions for each state.

The deadline to sue for breach of contract often depends on whether the contract is written or oral. Some states use one period for both. This page compares ten states (written and oral agreements).

Estimate, not legal advice. Deadlines can be shorter or longer than the general period. Talk to a licensed attorney in your state.

State by state

Texas: 4 years

Every action for which there is no express limitations period, except an action for the recovery of real property, must be brought not later than four years after the day the cause of action accrues.

Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.051 · official text (Texas Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[1]

Florida: 5 years (written contract)

(2) WITHIN FIVE YEARS.—

(b) A legal or equitable action on a contract, obligation, or liability founded on a written instrument,

Fla. Stat. § 95.11(2)(b) · official text (Florida Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[2]

Other periods: oral contract (not founded on a written instrument): 4 years (Fla. Stat. § 95.11(3)(j)).

Ohio: 6 years (written contract)

an action upon a specialty or an agreement, contract, or promise in writing shall be brought within six years after the cause of action accrued.

Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.06 · official text (Ohio Legislative Service Commission), read on 13 November 2026[3]

Other periods: oral contract: 4 years (Ohio Rev. Code § 2305.07(A)).

Washington: 6 years (written contract)

The following actions shall be commenced within six years:

(1) An action upon a contract in writing, or liability express or implied arising out of a written agreement,

RCW 4.16.040(1) · official text (Washington State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[4]

Other periods: oral contract: 3 years (RCW 4.16.080(3)).

Virginia: 5 years (written and signed contract)

2. In actions on any contract that is not otherwise specified and that is in writing and signed by the party to be charged thereby, or by his agent, within five years whether such writing be under seal or not;

Va. Code § 8.01-246(A)(2) · official text (Virginia General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[5]

Other periods: oral contract (or written but unsigned): 3 years (Va. Code § 8.01-246(A)(4)).

North Carolina: 3 years (written or oral contract)

Within three years an action -

(1) Upon a contract, obligation or liability arising out of a contract, express or implied, except those mentioned in the preceding sections or in G.S. 1-53(1).

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-52(1) · official text (North Carolina General Assembly), read on 13 November 2026[6]

Other periods: sealed instrument: 10 years (N.C. Gen. Stat. § 1-47(2)).

Michigan: 6 years

(9) The period of limitations is 6 years for an action to recover damages or money due for breach of contract that is not described in subsections (2) to (8).

MCL 600.5807(9) · official text (Michigan Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[7]

Arizona: 6 years (written contract signed in arizona)

A. An action for debt shall be commenced and prosecuted within six years after the cause of action accrues, and not afterward, if the indebtedness is evidenced by or founded on either of the following:

1. A contract in writing that is executed in this state.

Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-548(A)(1) · official text (Arizona State Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[8]

Other periods: oral agreement (debt not in writing): 3 years (Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-543(1)); written instrument signed outside arizona: 4 years (Ariz. Rev. Stat. § 12-544(3)).

Massachusetts: 6 years

Section 2. Actions of contract, other than those to recover for personal injuries, founded upon contracts or liabilities, express or implied, except actions limited by section one or actions upon judgments or decrees of courts of record of the United States or of this or of any other state of the United States, shall, except as otherwise provided, be commenced only within six years next after the cause of action accrues.

Mass. Gen. Laws c. 260, § 2 · official text (Massachusetts Legislature), read on 13 November 2026[9]

Minnesota: 6 years

Except where the Uniform Commercial Code otherwise prescribes, the following actions shall be commenced within six years:

(1) upon a contract or other obligation, express or implied, as to which no other limitation is expressly prescribed;

Minn. Stat. § 541.05, subd. 1(1) · official text (Minnesota Office of the Revisor of Statutes), read on 13 November 2026[10]
