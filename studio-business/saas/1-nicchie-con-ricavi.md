### 1. Micro-app Shopify (upsell, sconti, inventario)

**Verificato**
- Kaching Appz (Erikas Mališauskas, ex freelance): portfolio upsell a "6-figure MRR"; prima app $0→$1.000 MRR in 4 mesi, 1.000 utenti nel primo mese via outreach manuale — https://www.indiehackers.com/post/qdReVAgLjz6EpW4OrJSI
- AMA IH: app bootstrap a $25k MRR in <1 anno — https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-bootstrapped-a-shopify-app-to-25k-mrr-in-less-than-a-year-ama-89f3d4c471 ; altra a 78k CAD MRR in 3 anni — https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-bootstrapped-a-shopify-app-to-78k-cad-mrr-in-3-years-ask-me-anything-5fbbc2b886
- Listing Acquire: app sconti $5.700 MRR, 230 merchant (~$25/merchant/mese) — https://app.acquire.com/startup/moplldz7q0-shopify-discounts-app-166-growth-68k-arr-5700-mrr-230-merchants
- Shopify trattiene 0% fino a $1M lifetime (dal 2025). Stocky (inventario) chiuso il 31/8/2026: EZstock, app solo-dev nata per sostituirlo — https://www.indiehackers.com/product/ezstock-purchase-orders
- **Rischio documentato**: Checkout X, €600k MRR, ucciso da cambio API Shopify — https://www.indiehackers.com/post/bootstrapping-to-600k-mrr-and-getting-killed-by-shopify-the-story-of-checkout-x-6349ed91ac

**Ipotesi**: mediana app <$1k MRR, top 10% $5-50k (Week One Labs, stima). Clienti: App Store (ranking+recensioni, 3-6 mesi di rodaggio). Costruibilità: alta (API/Polaris standard). Concorrenza: alta su upsell/recensioni, media su inventario/ordini d'acquisto post-Stocky.

### 2. Integrazioni tra due software diffusi

**Verificato**
- Sync2Sheets (Notion↔Google Sheets): 1 fondatore, 0 dipendenti, $0 marketing, ~$9k MRR (altra fonte $8.400, 40k utenti); distribuzione via add-on Google Workspace e community Notion/Reddit — https://superframeworks.com/blog/sync2sheets
- HelpKit (Notion→help center): $1.020 MRR, 51 clienti dopo 5 mesi — https://www.indiehackers.com/post/ama-my-bootstrapped-notion-2-knowledge-base-saas-just-hit-1000-mrr-fa954648a5
- TaskRobin (email→Notion/Airtable): 100+ paganti dopo 1 anno — https://www.indiehackers.com/product/taskrobin-io

**Ipotesi**: coppie HubSpot↔QuickBooks ecc. hanno ricavi non pubblici. Costruibilità: alta (webhook + OAuth). Rischi: Zapier/Make e integrazioni native; soffitto ~$10-20k MRR per coppia.
