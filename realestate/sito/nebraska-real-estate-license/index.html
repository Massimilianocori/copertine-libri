This page covers a first salesperson license for someone who is not licensed in another state. Every step and figure comes from the commission; where its pages do not give a figure, we say so instead of guessing.

The steps, from the commission’s checklist

  1. Check the basics. At least 19 years old, a high school diploma or equivalent, legally eligible to work in the U.S.[1]
  2. Complete 96 hours of education: 90 clock hours of approved pre-license courses (three 30-hour courses) and the 6-hour Professional Practice Standards course.[1]
  3. Submit the application with the application fee ($175) and the examination fee ($150 per exam).[1],[2]
  4. Fingerprints and criminal history check.[1]
  5. Pass the exam, scheduled with Pearson. You need 60 of 80 on the national part and 38 of 50 on the state part.[3],[4]
  6. Buy errors and omissions insurance and find a designated broker.[1]
  7. Send the license issuance form and fee ($90) within 30 days of passing.[1],[2]

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What do I need for a Nebraska real estate license?

To be at least 19 with a high school diploma or equivalent, 96 hours of education (90 hours of approved pre-license courses plus the 6-hour Professional Practice Standards course), a criminal history check, a pass on both parts of the exam, errors and omissions insurance and a designated broker.[1]

How long does it take to get a real estate license in Nebraska?

The commission says the typical application process takes approximately 4 to 8 weeks, not counting the time you need for the courses. Your application and background report are valid for one year from the date the application is received.[1]

How many hours of classes does Nebraska require?

96 hours in total: three approved 30-hour pre-license courses (90 clock hours) and the 6-hour Professional Practice Standards course.[1]

How much does a Nebraska real estate license cost?

The commission's fee schedule lists $175 for the license application, $150 for each examination and $90 for the salesperson original license: $415 in all, before courses, fingerprints and insurance.[2]

Can I retake the Nebraska real estate exam?

Yes. The commission says applicants may retake the examination multiple times during their eligibility period, and that an appointment and a fee are required for each retake ($150 within one year of application).[2],[3]
