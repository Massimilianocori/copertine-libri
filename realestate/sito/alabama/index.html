A pass rate is not a rating of a school. It shows how that school’s past students did on the exam. Schools enroll different students, and with few exams the percentage is mostly chance. We publish the official figures as they are and do not rank or recommend schools.

How to read the Alabama figures

The Alabama Real Estate Commission (AREC) publishes two figures for each school: students who passed on their first attempt, and all attempts including retakes.[1]

“The displayed school statistics represent the number of students who have taken and passed the license examination on their first attempt (1st) and the total number of students who have taken and passed the license examination, including retakes (All).”

Alabama Real Estate Commission, Exam Pass Rates by School[1]

The commission’s page does not print start and end dates. It describes the period like this:

“Data reflects the previous 12 months and is updated the 10th of every month.”

Alabama Real Estate Commission, Exam Pass Rates by School[1]

We read the page on November 14, 2026. It lists 107 approved schools; 40 of them had no first-attempt salesperson exams in the period and show a dash. The commission prints no statewide total, so the top row is our sum of the school rows: 1,653 of 2,378 first attempts passed (69.5%), and 2,173 of 3,960 of all attempts (54.9%).[1]

The “all attempts” rate is usually lower than the first-attempt rate, because people who fail and try again are counted each time. City and format (classroom or online) are the ones on the commission’s page. This page shows the salesperson exam; broker exam figures are on the official page.

Getting a Alabama real estate license

Before the exam you need 60 hours of approved pre-licensing education and you must be at least 19 years old. The steps, deadlines and official fees are on a separate page.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the pass rate for the Alabama real estate exam?

The commission publishes rates by school, not a statewide rate. Adding up the school figures for the previous 12 months, 1,653 of 2,378 first attempts at the salesperson exam passed (69.5%), and 2,173 of 3,960 of all attempts including retakes (54.9%). The sums are ours.[1]

How hard is the Alabama real estate exam?

Going by the commission's school figures, about 69 in 100 first attempts at the salesperson exam passed in the previous 12 months. You have 6 months after finishing the prelicense course to pass; after that the course must be retaken.[1],[2]

How do I compare real estate classes in Alabama?

The commission lists 107 approved schools with their exam results, city and format (classroom or online). In the table you can filter by format and see, next to each rate, how many students it is based on. Only 15 schools had 30 or more first-attempt exams in the period.[1]
