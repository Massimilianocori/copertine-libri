How to get a Texas real estate license

The steps, hours, deadlines and official fees for a Texas real estate sales agent license, taken from the commission’s own pages and quoted word for word.

This page covers a first sales agent license for someone who is not licensed in another state. Every step and figure comes from the commission; where its pages do not give a figure, we say so instead of guessing.

The steps, in TREC’s order

  1. Check that you qualify. You must be a citizen of the United States or a lawfully admitted alien, at least 18 years old, and meet TREC’s standards of honesty, trustworthiness and integrity.[1]
  2. Register for a REALM Portal account. It gives you the pre-license education application number that your school needs.[1]
  3. Complete 180 classroom hours of qualifying courses with a TREC-approved provider: Principles of Real Estate I (30 hours), Principles of Real Estate II (30 hours), Law of Agency (30 hours), Law of Contracts (30 hours), Promulgated Contract Forms (30 hours), Real Estate Finance (30 hours).[1]
  4. Apply online and pay the fee ($206 in all) in the REALM Portal, after your courses are finished and uploaded.[1],[2]
  5. Have your fingerprints taken and pass the background check ($40, paid to the fingerprint company, if you were never fingerprinted for TREC).[1],[2]
  6. Pass the exam, given by Pearson ($58, paid to the exam provider).[1],[2]
  7. Find a sponsoring broker. Your license is issued inactive and becomes active when a broker sponsors you.[1]

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What are the requirements for a Texas real estate license?

You must be at least 18, a U.S. citizen or lawfully admitted alien, and meet TREC's standards of honesty, trustworthiness and integrity. You need 180 classroom hours of qualifying courses, a fingerprint background check, a pass on the exam and a sponsoring broker to be active.[1]

How much does a Texas real estate license cost?

The official fees add up to $304: $206 for the application, $58 for the exam and $40 for fingerprints, under the fee schedule effective September 1, 2026. The price of the 180 hours of courses is on top and depends on the school.[2]

Can I get a Texas real estate license online?

The application is filed online in TREC's REALM Portal, and TREC's table of approved providers includes schools that teach online. Fingerprints are taken in person, and the exam is given by Pearson.[1]

How do I become a real estate agent in Texas?

In TREC's order: register in the REALM Portal, complete 180 hours of qualifying courses, apply and pay the fee, have your fingerprints taken, pass the exam and find a sponsoring broker. The application expires one year after it is filed.[1]

Do I need fingerprints for a TREC license?

Yes. TREC says you are required by law to have fingerprints on file with the Texas Department of Public Safety so that a background check can be done; fingerprints on file for other agencies are not accepted. The fee is $40.[1],[2]
