A pass rate is not a rating of a school. It shows how that school’s past students did on the exam. Schools enroll different students, and with few exams the percentage is mostly chance. We publish the official figures as they are and do not rank or recommend schools.

How to read the Texas figures

The Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) counts first attempts only: retakes are not in these figures, and TREC does not publish a pass rate that includes them.[2]

“The percentage of students who passed the Sales Agent licensing exam on their first attempt, within the past two years.”

Texas Real Estate Commission, Provider Exam Passage Rates for Sales Agents and Brokers[2]

A student’s result goes to the school where they took most of their courses:

“If a student took the majority of his or her qualifying courses from the same provider in the two years prior to taking the licensing exam for the first time, the student's exam results are attributed to that provider.”

Texas Real Estate Commission, Provider Exam Passage Rates for Sales Agents and Brokers[2]

The top row is TREC’s own total: 29,980 first-attempt sales agent exams, 16,824 passed, 56.12%. The 30 providers in the table add up to exactly those numbers.[1]

TREC also uses pass rates to supervise schools. It compares each provider’s overall rate (sales agent and broker exams together, 56.18% for all providers) with a minimum, which works out at 44.94% for this period:[1]

“Minimum Pass Rate (MPR) is equal to 80% of the Overall Pass Rate.”

Texas Real Estate Commission, Provider Exam Passage Rates for Sales Agents and Brokers[2]

“A provider whose Overall Pass Rate is greater than or equal to the Minimum Pass Rate (MPR) benchmark is considered in good standing.”

Texas Real Estate Commission, Provider Exam Passage Rates for Sales Agents and Brokers[2]

This page shows the sales agent exam only. Broker exam figures and each provider’s standing against the minimum are on the official page.[2] Some providers register their name with exclamation marks around it; we show the name without them and change nothing else.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the pass rate for the Texas real estate exam?

On the sales agent exam, 56.12% of first attempts passed (16,824 of 29,980) in the period December 1, 2023 to November 30, 2025. That is the Texas Real Estate Commission's own figure for all education providers together.[1]

How hard is the Texas real estate exam?

The official numbers give an idea: 56.12% of first attempts at the sales agent exam passed in the period covered (16,824 of 29,980), so a little under half did not pass the first time. If you fail either part three times, TREC requires more education before you can test again.[1],[3]

Which online real estate schools are in the Texas table?

TREC's table does not say whether a provider teaches online or in a classroom. It lists 30 providers with their first-attempt results; the ones with the most exams in the period are Champions School of Real Estate LTD (15,547 exams, 53.24%), AceableAgent (8,516, 65.81%) and Kaplan Real Estate Education (1,280, 53.20%).[1]

Where does TREC publish school pass rates?

On its page “Provider Exam Passage Rates for Sales Agents and Brokers”, which loads a data file from the same website. The figures here are copied from that file, with the period and the number of exams next to each rate.[1],[2]
