A pass rate is not a rating of a school. It shows how that school’s past students did on the exam. Schools enroll different students, and with few exams the percentage is mostly chance. We publish the official figures as they are and do not rank or recommend schools.

How to read the Nebraska figures

The Nebraska Real Estate Commission (NREC) posts a report for each quarter on its public page “License Examination Performance by School”.[2] This page shows the most recent quarterly report that the commission linked from that page when we read it: January 1 to March 31, 2026.[1] We use only reports linked there. The exam has two parts, national and state, and the report counts them separately, for first-time takers and for everyone (first-time takers plus repeaters).

“This report shows the performance on the Nebraska real estate license examination by school for candidates taking the examination for the first time during the reporting period.”

Nebraska Real Estate Commission, License Examination Performance by School[2]

The pass mark is 60 correct answers out of 80 on the national part and 38 out of 50 on the state part.[2]

One quarter in Nebraska is a small number of exams. The commission says so itself:

“The Commission would caution readers to be aware of the total number of students that are being tested from any school.”

Nebraska Real Estate Commission, License Examination Performance by School[2]

“percentages have less to tell when the number of students is quite small.”

Nebraska Real Estate Commission, License Examination Performance by School[2]

In this quarter only 2 of the 12 schools had 30 or more first-time takers on the national part. The report we read has no statewide row, so the top row is our sum of the school rows: first-time takers passed 69 of 118 national parts (58.5%) and 31 of 118 state parts (26.3%).[1]

The report is a printout prepared by the exam company, Pearson, which the commission posts on its own website. It is a scanned document: we typed its figures by hand and checked that every row adds up (passed plus failed equals graded, first-time takers plus repeaters equals the total, the percentages match). It shows whole percentages. Broker exam rows are in the official report; a school with broker exams only is marked.

Common questions

Questions people in the United States search for, answered from the official sources.

What is the pass rate for the Nebraska real estate exam?

In the quarter January 1 to March 31, 2026, first-time takers passed 69 of 118 national parts of the salesperson exam (58.5%) and 31 of 118 state parts (26.3%). These are our sums of the school rows in the commission's report.[1]

How many questions do you need to pass the Nebraska real estate exam?

The commission says the number of correct answers needed is 60 out of 80 on the national part and 38 out of 50 on the state part. The exam is given by Pearson.[2],[3]

How do I compare real estate classes in Nebraska?

The commission's report for the quarter January 1 to March 31, 2026 lists 12 schools with exam results. Most have very few students in a quarter: only 2 had 30 or more first-time takers. The commission itself warns that percentages say little when the number of students is small.[1],[2]
